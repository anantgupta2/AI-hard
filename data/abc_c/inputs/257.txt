=== MANUSCRIPT: A hyperbolic group with no geometric CAT(0) action [preprints/A-hyperbolic-group-with-no-geometric-CAT0-action-September-25-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We construct a hyperbolic group with a finite classifying space that admits no geometric action on a proper complete CAT(0) space. Consequently, a finite aspherical simplicial complex with a linear combinatorial disk-filling inequality need not have a finite locally CAT(0) homotopy model, and hence need not have a finite locally CAT(−1) model. Here metric models carry geodesic length metrics inducing the given complex topology.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exist a finite connected two-dimensional aspherical simplicial complex $K$ and a constant $C<\infty$ with the following properties. \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item Every combinatorial edge loop of length $\ell$ in the universal cover of $K$ bounds a disk diagram with at most $C\ell$ triangular faces. \item The group $\pi_1K$ admits no geometric action on a proper complete $\CAT(0)$ space. \end{enumerate} In particular, $\pi_1K$ is word-hyperbolic, and no finite simplicial complex homotopy equivalent to $K$ admits a locally $\CAT(0)$ geodesic length metric inducing its given topology. Consequently, no such model admits a compatible locally $\CAT(-1)$ geodesic length metric.
[theorem] \label{thm:diagram} Fix sufficiently small positive $\alpha,\beta,\delta$ with $\beta>7\alpha$, and put $f=2-\alpha$ and $p=3-\beta$. As $n\longrightarrow\infty$, the probability tends to one that $P$ is aspherical and its letter presentation satisfies a linear disk isoperimetric inequality.

Local comparison geometry is more restrictive. The geometric realization question asks whether every hyperbolic group admits a geometric action on a proper $\CAT(0)$ space, or on a proper $\CAT(-1)$ space.
