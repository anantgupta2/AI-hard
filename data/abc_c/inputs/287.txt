=== MANUSCRIPT: An isomorphism of the free group factors [preprints/An-isomorphism-of-the-free-group-factors-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We solve the free group factor isomorphism problem affirmatively by proving that $`L(\mathbb F_2)`$ and $`L(\mathbb F_3)`$ are isomorphic as tracial von Neumann algebras. The classical free group factor alternative then implies that all interpolated free group factors, including $`L(\mathbb F_\infty)`$, are isomorphic and have fundamental group $`\mathbb R_{\gt 0}`$.
[theorem] [Isomorphism of consecutive ranks] \label{thm:rank-step} For every integer $n\geq3$ there is a unital trace-preserving normal $*$-isomorphism \[ L(\F_n)\cong L(\F_{n+1}). \]
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exists a unital normal trace-preserving $*$-isomorphism \[ \Phi:L(\F_2)\longrightarrow L(\F_3). \]

PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- Free entropy offered another approach to distinguishing ranks. A free semicircular $n$-tuple generates $L(\F_n)$ \cite[Remarks~1.10--1.11]{voiculescu1990} and has free entropy dimension $\delta=n$ \cite[Section~2.4(c),(d)]{voiculescu2002entropy}. Voiculescu asked whether $\delta$ of a finite self-adjoint tuple depends only on the tracial von Neumann algebra it generates \cite[Section~2.6]{voiculescu2002entropy}. Such invariance would distinguish the finite ranks. Our isomorphisms give the opposite conclusion: Corollary~\ref{cor:entropy-dimensions} gives a negative answer and proves the same failure for $\delta_0,\delta^*$, and $\delta^\star$. Each of the four dimensions attains every integer value $n\geq2$ on finite self-adjoint generating tuples of $L(\F_2)$. The proof transports semicircular tuples for the microstates dimensions $\delta,\delta_0$ and group-algebra tuples for the nonmicrostates dimensions $\delta^*,\delta^\star$; the two tuple families need not coincide.
- \subsection{A bound independent of support size} For a finitely supported real function $k$ on $\Gamma$, set \[ s(k)=\sum_{g\in\Gamma}k(g)S_g. \] The coefficient norm $\norm{k}_{\ell^2}$ below is distinguished from the tracial $L^2$ norm $\normtwo{\cdot}$ of operators. The next estimate uses the creation, annihilation, and diagonal decomposition underlying free-product Khintchine inequalities; see Ricard and Xu~\cite[Section~2, Lemmas~2.1--2.3 and Corollary~2.4]{ricard_xu2006}. We include the scalar self-adjoint argument to give an explicit constant independent of the number of summands. \begin{lemma}[Norm of a free sum]\label{lem:free-sum} For every such $k$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:free-sum} \norm{s(k)}\leq K\norm{k}_{\ell^2},\qquad K=3\pi. \end{equation} \end{lemma} \begin{proof} We prove the more general bound \begin{equation}\label{eq:general-free-sum} \norm{\sum_j z_j} \leq 2\Bigl(\sum_j\normtwo{z_j}^2\Bigr)^{1/2} +\max_j\norm{z_j} \end{equation} for a finite free family of subalgebras and centered self-adjoint $z_j$ in the respective components.
