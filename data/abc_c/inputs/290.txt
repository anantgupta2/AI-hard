=== MANUSCRIPT: Expected amenable subalgebras preserving core commutants [preprints/Expected-amenable-subalgebras-preserving-core-commutants-September-23-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that every inclusion $`N\subset M`$ of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals and a faithful normal conditional expectation contains an expected amenable subalgebra $`P\subset N`$ such that $`P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)`$, where $`c(M)`$ denotes the continuous core of M. This resolves the relative bicentralizer conjecture in this setting.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $N\subset M$ be a unital inclusion of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals, and suppose that there is a faithful normal conditional expectation $E_N:M\to N$. There exist a unital amenable von Neumann subalgebra $P\subset N$ and a faithful normal conditional expectation $F:N\to P$ such that \[ P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M). \] Here amenable means injective.
[corollary] \label{cor:masa} Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, there is a maximal abelian subalgebra $A\subset N$ with a faithful normal conditional expectation $F:N\to A$ such that \[ A'\cap c(M)=c(A)\vee\bigl(N'\cap c(M)\bigr). \]
[proposition] [Binormal states and spectral shifts]\label{prop:inputs} In this setting the following hold. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item If a state $\Phi$ on $\mathbf B(\HH)$ satisfies \[ \Phi\circ\lambda=\Phi\circ\rho=\varphi,\qquad \Phi(e_N)=1,\qquad \Phi(f(X_M))=f(0)\quad(f\in C_0(\R)), \] then, for $a,b\in B$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:binormal} \Phi(\lambda(a)\rho(b))=\langle a\Omega b,\Omega\rangle. \end{equation} \item For each $s\in\R$ there is a unital completely positive map $\theta_s:\mathbf B(\HH)\to\mathbf B(\HH)$ such that \begin{align} \theta_s(e_N)&=e_N,& \theta_s\circ\rho&=\rho,\label{eq:shift1}\\ \theta_s\circ\lambda&=\lambda\circ\gamma_s\circ E_B,& \theta_s(f(X_M))&=f(X_M-s)\quad(f\in C_0(\R)).\label{eq:shift2} \end{align} These maps need not be normal. \end{enumerate}

- \paragraph{History and the relative conjecture.} Popa's solution of Kadison's problem for irreducible expected inclusions with separable preduals and type II bottom factor provides a model for finding amenable subalgebras that retain prescribed commutation properties \cite{Popa1981}. For type III factors, modular asymptotic centralization supplies a related approach. The bicentralizer of a faithful normal state consists of those elements that commute asymptotically with every bounded sequence asymptotically centralizing the state in predual norm. Connes introduced the problem of its triviality in the classification of injective factors \cite{Connes1982,Connes1985}. Haagerup solved the amenable type $\mathrm{III}_1$ case, completing the uniqueness theorem for the injective type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor \cite{Haagerup}. For type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factors with separable predual, he characterized triviality by norm-density of faithful normal states whose centralizers have trivial relative commutant \cite[Theorem~3.1]{Haagerup}. - The connection with multiplication is the central point. Set $H=L^2(N,\varphi)$, $K=L^2(B,\varphi)$, and $\Omega=\varphi^{1/2}$. Consider maps from $H\otimes K$ to $L^2(M,\varphi)$, initially given for $x\in N$ and $a\in B$ by \[ R(x\Omega\otimes a\Omega)=xa\Omega,\qquad L(x\Omega\otimes a\Omega)=ax\Omega. \] We prove that these maps are isometries and that \[ R=L\exp(iX\otimes Y), \] where $X$ generates the modular action on $H$ and $Y$ generates the bicentralizer flow on $K$. This is the required fixed-point criterion.
