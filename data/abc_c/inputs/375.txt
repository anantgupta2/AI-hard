=== MANUSCRIPT: A positive resolution of De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight [preprints/De-Giorgis-conjecture-in-dimension-eight-September-26-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We resolve De Giorgi's conjecture positively in dimension eight: every entire C2 solution $`u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)`$ of $`\Delta u=u^3-u`$ with an everywhere positive directional derivative is a planar heteroclinic. We prove that every stable solution $`v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]`$ of this equation is a constant well or a planar heteroclinic, without an energy-growth assumption.
[theorem] [Monotone rigidity in dimension eight] \label{thm:monotone} Let $u\in C^2(\R^8,(-1,1))$ satisfy \[ \Delta u=u^3-u,\qquad \partial_8u>0\quad\text{in }\R^8. \] Then there are $e\in\mathbb S^7$ with $e_8>0$ and $c\in\R$ such that \[ u(x)=\tanh\!\left(\frac{e\cdot x-c}{\sqrt2}\right) \qquad\text{for every }x\in\R^8. \]
[theorem] [Stable rigidity in dimension seven] \label{thm:stable} Let $v\in C^2(\R^7,[-1,1])$ be a stable solution of $\Delta v=v^3-v$. Then either $v\equiv1$, $v\equiv-1$, or \[ v(x)=\tanh\!\left(\frac{a\cdot x-b}{\sqrt2}\right) \qquad\text{for some }a\in\mathbb S^6,\ b\in\R. \] No energy-growth assumption is required.
[theorem] [Finite-density stable rigidity]\label{thm:finite} Let $4\le n\le7$. Suppose $v\in C^2(\R^n,[-1,1])$ solves $\Delta v=v^3-v$ and satisfies \[ \int_{\R^n}\bigl(|\nabla\varphi|^2+(3v^2-1)\varphi^2\bigr)\dd x \ge0 \qquad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_c^1(\R^n). \] If $M_\infty<\infty$, then $v\equiv1$, $v\equiv-1$, or $v(x)=g(a\cdot x-b)$ for some $a\in\mathbb S^{n-1}$ and $b\in\R$.

- The conjecture connects phase transitions with the Bernstein problem for minimal hypersurfaces. For the quartic potential \[ W(s)=\frac14(1-s^2)^2, \] the rescaled phase-transition energies have surface tension times perimeter as their variational limit \cite{ModicaMortola1977,Modica1987}. The graph condition in the minimal-surface problem is important: nonflat minimizing cones occur in ambient dimension eight, whereas nonaffine entire minimal graphs first occur in ambient dimension nine \cite{Simons1968,BDG1969}. The corresponding distinction between stable and monotone Allen--Cahn solutions will also enter our proof.
- Theorem~\ref{thm:stable} has a geometric parallel in the stable Bernstein theorem for complete, connected, two-sided stable smooth minimal immersions of boundaryless six-manifolds into $\R^7$: their images are entire affine hyperplanes, with no volume-growth hypothesis \cite[Theorem~1.1]{HongLiWang2026}. The Bernstein theorem is not an input to our argument.
