FAMILY 328: Nonexpansive fixed points in reflexive Banach spaces
SUBJECT: Functional analysis
OPENAI SUMMARY: Resolves Kirk's reflexive-space fixed-point problem: every nonexpansive selfmap of a nonempty closed bounded convex subset of a real reflexive Banach space has a fixed point. The result uses the original norm, without assuming uniform convexity.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Fixed Points of Nonexpansive Maps in Reflexive Banach Spaces [preprints/Fixed-Points-of-Nonexpansive-Maps-in-Reflexive-Banach-Spaces-September-24-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: Every nonexpansive selfmap of a nonempty closed bounded convex subset of a real reflexive Banach space has a fixed point. This resolves the reflexive-space fixed point problem for the given norm.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $X$ be a real reflexive Banach space, and let $C\subseteq X$ be nonempty, norm closed, bounded, and convex. If $F:C\to C$ satisfies \[ \norm{F(a)-F(b)}\le \norm{a-b}\qquad(a,b\in C), \] then $F$ has a fixed point.
[corollary] [Commuting families]\label{cor:commuting} Let $X$ be a real reflexive Banach space, and let $C\subseteq X$ be nonempty, norm closed, bounded, and convex. For any pairwise commuting family $\mathcal S$ of nonexpansive selfmaps of $C$, the common fixed set \[ \operatorname{Fix}(\mathcal S) =\{x\in C:T(x)=x\text{ for every }T\in\mathcal S\} \] is nonempty and is a nonexpansive retract of $C$. That is, there is a nonexpansive map $R:C\to\operatorname{Fix}(\mathcal S)$ that is the identity on $\operatorname{Fix}(\mathcal S)$.
[proposition] [An anchor point] \label{prop:anchor} The set $A$ is a nonempty compact semigroup of nonexpansive selfmaps of $K$ and contains an idempotent $P$. There is a point $x\in K$ with $P(x)=x$ such that the nonempty weakly compact set \begin{equation} \label{eq:anchor-tail-set} D_x=\bigcap_{m\geq1} \overline{\{h(x):h\in\mathcal H_m\}}^{\,\mathrm{weak}} \end{equation} satisfies \begin{equation} \label{eq:anchor-convex-hull} x\in\overline{\conv D_x}^{\,\norm{\cdot}}. \end{equation}
PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- A Banach space has the \emph{fixed point property for nonexpansive maps} if every nonexpansive selfmap of each nonempty closed bounded convex subset has a fixed point. Here nonexpansiveness is measured in the given norm. We prove the following theorem.
- Further results established the property for substantial classes beyond the uniformly convex case. Maurey proved it for reflexive subspaces of $L^1$ in the inherited norm \cite[Theorem~1]{Maurey1981}. Lin proved the weak fixed point property for spaces with a $1$-unconditional basis, meaning that changing signs of basis coefficients preserves the norm \cite[Theorem~1]{Lin1985}. Here the weak fixed point property restricts the domains to weakly compact convex sets; in a reflexive space it is equivalent to the property considered here. Garc\'ia-Falset, Llorens-Fuster, and Mazcu\~n\'an-Navarro proved the property for uniformly nonsquare spaces \cite{GarciaFalset2006}; see also the proof of \citet{Dowling2008}. This condition requires a uniform gap below $1$ in at least one of $\norm{(a+b)/2}$ and $\norm{(a-b)/2}$ for every pair $a,b$ in the unit ball. These theorems concern the specified norm, with additional structure.
- Hanebaly previously proposed a proof of this same original-norm assertion \cite[Theorem~1]{Hanebaly2019}, and subsequently announced a further proof \cite{Hanebaly2021}. More recent discussion of the general question appears in \citet[Section~1]{Nourouzi2026}, who records it as unresolved. These statements of the literature are distinct from the argument given here. Our proof proceeds through an adaptive anchor, compact families of predicted outputs, and weighted switching on a tree.
- We argue by contradiction for the remainder of the proof and fix $K,T,Y$, and $(z_i)$ as in Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}. A sequence $(u_j)$ in $K$ is an \emph{approximate fixed point sequence} if $\norm{Tu_j-u_j}\to0$. The next lemma is the diameter lemma of Goebel and Karlovitz \cite{Goebel1975,Karlovitz1976}; we include its proof. Karlovitz also records independent development of this lemma by P.~M.~Fitzpatrick \cite[p.~159]{Karlovitz1976}.
