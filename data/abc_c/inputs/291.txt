FAMILY 291: Cuntz comparison, nuclear dimension, and equivariant Jiang–Su stability
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
OPENAI SUMMARY: Proves equivariant Jiang–Su stability for every countable discrete amenable group action on a simple separable unital infinite-dimensional nuclear stably finite Jiang–Su-stable C∗-algebra, resolving this case of Szabó's conjecture without restrictions on trace dynamics. The family also proves the unital Toms–Winter conjecture, equating strict comparison, finite nuclear dimension and Jiang–Su stability in the simple separable unital infinite-dimensional nuclear setting.

ABSTRACT: Every action of a countable discrete amenable group on a simple, separable, unital, infinite-dimensional, nuclear, stably finite complex C∗-algebra that is already Jiang–Su stable absorbs the trivial action on the Jiang–Su algebra up to cocycle conjugacy. No restriction is imposed on the action on the tracial-state simplex. This proves the unital, stably finite case of Szabó's Conjecture A on automatic equivariant Jiang–Su stability.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(A\) be a simple, separable, unital, infinite-dimensional, nuclear, stably finite complex \(\mathrm C^*\)-algebra satisfying \(A\cong A\otimes\Zalg\). Put \[ B=A\otimes\Zalg,\qquad \beta_g=\alpha_g\otimes\operatorname{id}_{\Zalg}. \] There are a unital \(*\)-isomorphism \(\Phi:A\to B\) and unitaries \(u_g\in B\), \(g\in G\), such that \begin{align} u_e&=1,& u_{gh}&=u_g\beta_g(u_h),\label{eq:cocycle}\\ \Phi(\alpha_g(a))&=u_g\beta_g(\Phi(a))u_g^* &&(g\in G,\ a\in A).\label{eq:conjugacy} \end{align}
[theorem] [Szab\'o--Wouters]\label{thm:absorption} Let a countable discrete amenable group act on a separable, unital, simple, nuclear, \(\Zalg\)-stable \(\mathrm C^*\)-algebra with nonempty tracial-state space. If the action has equivariant uniform property Gamma, it is cocycle conjugate to its tensor product with the trivial action on \(\Zalg\).

=== MANUSCRIPT: Cuntz comparison and Jiang–Su absorption [preprints/Cuntz-comparison-and-Jiang-Su-absorption-September-23-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that strict comparison in the extended-functional sense implies Jiang–Su absorption for separable simple nuclear non-elementary C∗-algebras. This resolves the corresponding implication of the Toms–Winter regularity problem, including nonunital algebras and allowing unbounded traces. More generally, every separable nuclear C∗-algebra whose Cuntz semigroup is almost unperforated and fully almost divisible absorbs the Jiang–Su algebra.
[theorem] [Simple comparison and absorption] \label{ext:thm:main} Let $A$ be a separable simple nuclear non-elementary $C^*$-algebra. If $A$ has strict comparison in the sense of Equation~\eqref{ext:eq:comparison}, then $A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\Z$.
[theorem] [Absorption from condition \textup{(C)}] \label{thm:condition-C} Let $A$ be a nonzero separable nuclear $C^*$-algebra satisfying \textup{(C)}. For every fixed free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\mathbb N$, there is a unital $*$-homomorphism $\Z\to F_\omega(A)$. Consequently $A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\Z$.
[corollary] [The canonical Cuntz-semigroup map] \label{cor:canonical} Let $A$ be a separable nuclear $C^*$-algebra, and let $\iota:A\to A\otimes_{\min}\Z$ be $\iota(a)=a\otimes1_\Z$. If $\Cu(\iota):\Cu(A)\to\Cu(A\otimes_{\min}\Z)$ is an isomorphism, then $A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\Z$.

- \maketitle \begin{abstract} We prove that strict comparison in the extended-functional sense implies Jiang--Su absorption for separable simple nuclear non-elementary $C^*$-algebras. This resolves the corresponding implication of the Toms--Winter regularity problem, including nonunital algebras and allowing unbounded traces. More generally, every separable nuclear $C^*$-algebra whose Cuntz semigroup is almost unperforated and fully almost divisible absorbs the Jiang--Su algebra. \end{abstract}
- The reverse comparison implication has a substantial history of partial results. Matui and Sato proved it for unital separable simple nuclear infinite-dimensional algebras with finitely many extremal traces, using excision in small central sequences and property \textup{(SI)} \cite[Theorem~1.1]{MatuiSato2012}. Sato, Kirchberg--R\o rdam, and Toms--White--Winter extended the tracial-boundary approach to nonempty compact extreme boundaries of finite covering dimension \cite[Corollary~1.2]{Sato2012}, \cite[Corollary~7.9]{KirchbergRordam2014}, \cite[Corollary~4.7]{TWW2015}. Castillejos, Evington, Tikuisis, and White isolated the remaining uniform central divisibility in trace: for simple separable unital nuclear algebras with nonempty tracial state space, strict comparison together with uniform property~$\Gamma$ is equivalent to $\Z$-stability \cite[Theorem~5.6]{CETW2022}. Uniform property~$\Gamma$ requires approximately central partitions that split the trace values of finitely many elements evenly, uniformly over all traces. The present argument constructs central maps from condition \textup{(C)} without imposing a tracial-boundary or uniform-$\Gamma$ hypothesis.
- Comparison and divisibility are also studied together through the notion of pureness. For the completed semigroup, the usual almost divisibility condition asks for $Nu\le x$ and $x'\le(N+1)u$ whenever $x'$ is compactly contained in $x$; together with almost unperforation this defines pureness \cite{APT2018}. Full almost divisibility as used here implies that condition. For separable simple unital non-elementary algebras of locally finite nuclear dimension, Winter proved that pureness implies Jiang--Su absorption \cite[Corollary~6.2]{Winter2012}. Under the same local nuclear-dimension hypothesis, he also obtained an abstract criterion using $W(A)$, the Cuntz semigroup of positive elements in finite matrices over $A$ \cite[Corollary~6.4]{Winter2012}. Corollary~\ref{cor:abstract-cu} instead uses the completed invariant $\Cu(A)$ and has no local nuclear-dimension assumption. The tensor-product approach to regularity of abstract Cuntz semigroups was developed by Antoine, Perera, and Thiel \cite{APT2018}; more recent work of Antoine, Perera, Thiel, and Vilalta characterizes pureness by controlled comparison and functional divisibility \cite[Theorem~C]{APTV2024}. === MANUSCRIPT: Nuclear dimension and Jiang–Su stability without elementary subquotients [preprints/Nuclear-dimension-and-Jiang-Su-stability-without-elementary-subquotients-September-23-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: For separable nuclear C∗-algebras with no nonzero elementary ideal subquotients, finite nuclear dimension is equivalent to Jiang–Su stability. More generally, $`\dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}(A_0\otimes\mathcal Z)\le1`$ for every separable nuclear A0. This proves Robert and Tikuisis's Conjecture (C1) and the nuclear-dimension equivalence in the nonsimple Toms–Winter regularity question.
[theorem] [Nonsimple nuclear regularity] \label{thm:main} \label{intro:regularity} Let $A$ be a separable nuclear complex $C^*$-algebra with no nonzero elementary ideal subquotients. The following conditions are equivalent: \begin{enumerate} \item $\nucdim(A)<\infty$; \item $A\cong A\otimes\Z$; \item $\nucdim(A)\le1$. \end{enumerate}
[corollary] [Regularity and Cuntz-semigroup criteria] \label{cor:regularity-cu} Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, let $\iota_A:A\to A\otimes\Z$ be $\iota_A(a)=a\otimes1_\Z$. The conditions of that theorem are also equivalent to each of the following: \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item the canonical map $\Cu(\iota_A):\Cu(A)\to\Cu(A\otimes\Z)$ is an isomorphism in $\mathbf{Cu}$; \item there is an abstract isomorphism $\Cu(A)\cong\Cu(A\otimes\Z)$ in $\mathbf{Cu}$. \end{enumerate} The abstract isomorphism need not be induced by a $*$-homomorphism.
[theorem] [Sharp bound after Jiang--Su absorption] \label{intro:main} For every separable nuclear complex $C^*$-algebra $A_0$, \[ \nucdim(A_0\otimes\Z)\le1. \]
