=== MANUSCRIPT: The metric Blaschke theorem [preprints/The-metric-Blaschke-theorem-September-23-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the metric Blaschke conjecture: every connected closed smooth Riemannian manifold of positive dimension whose global injectivity radius equals its diameter is, up to scale, a standard compact rank-one symmetric space.
[theorem] [The metric Blaschke theorem]\label{thm:blaschke} Let $(M,g)$ be a connected closed smooth Riemannian manifold of positive dimension. If $\inj(M,g)=\diam(M,g)$, then $(M,g)$ is isometric, up to multiplication of the metric by a positive constant, to a round sphere, a standard real, complex or quaternionic projective space, or the Cayley plane.
[theorem] [Sharp volume comparison]\label{thm:comparison} Every Blaschke manifold and its model at the same diameter satisfy \[ \Vol(M,g)\ge\Vol(M_0,g_0). \] Equality holds if and only if $(M,g)$ is isometric to $(M_0,g_0)$.
[corollary] [Reduction to an upper volume bound] \label{thm:volume-reduction} If a Blaschke manifold has volume at most that of its model at the same diameter, then it is isometric to that model.

- For the projective models, it is useful to distinguish three kinds of information: topology, volume at a fixed geodesic period, and metric isometry. Yang proved that a Blaschke manifold of complex projective \emph{homotopy type} has the model volume at the same prime geodesic period \citep[Theorem, p.~380]{Yang1991}. For the projective planes, smooth incidence geometry and the classification of \citet[Corollary~C]{KramerStolz2007} identify the point manifold diffeomorphically. Wilking records the equal-volume theorem for a manifold homeomorphic to a compact rank-one symmetric space whose unit geodesics all have the same least period, attributing it to Reznikov \citep[p.~282]{Wilking2001}. Reznikov's earlier work on the quaternionic weak Blaschke conjecture \citep{Reznikov1985HP} is another predecessor of this volume approach. The homeomorphism hypothesis in the result recorded by Wilking, and the other hypotheses just stated, will be verified at their uses.
