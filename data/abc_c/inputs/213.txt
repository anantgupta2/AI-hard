FAMILY 213: Critical percolation on every quasi-transitive graph
SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
OPENAI SUMMARY: Resolves the Benjamini–Schramm criticality conjecture for bond percolation on every infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with $`p_c\lt 1`$: at the critical probability, there is almost surely no infinite cluster. The family also establishes this conclusion for both nearest-neighbor bond and site percolation on ℤ3.
HEADLINE (v2, semi-formal): ∀ infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive G with p_c(G) < 1: P_{p_c}(∃ infinite open cluster) = 0

ABSTRACT: We prove that nearest-neighbor Bernoulli bond and site percolation on ℤ3 have no infinite cluster at their respective critical parameters. The proof combines a finite connection inequality for independent hyperedges with a finite-scale extension estimate and an adaptive exploration.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For nearest-neighbor Bernoulli bond percolation on $\mathbb Z^3$, there is almost surely no infinite open cluster at the bond critical parameter. The same assertion holds for nearest-neighbor Bernoulli site percolation at the site critical parameter.
[proposition] [Joint connection comparison]\label{cmp:joint} In independent percolation on a finite hypergraph, with arbitrary hyperedge probabilities in $[0,1]$, let $o$ be a node and let $A,T$ be node sets with $A\ne\varnothing$. Then \begin{equation}\label{cmp:joint-bound} \mathbb P(o\leftrightarrow A,\ o\leftrightarrow T) \ge \mathbb P(o\leftrightarrow A) \min_{a\in A}\mathbb P(a\leftrightarrow T). \end{equation}
[corollary] [Failure comparison]\label{cmp:failure} Consider independent bond or site percolation on a finite undirected graph, with arbitrary individual probabilities in $[0,1]$. For a vertex $o$, vertex sets $A,T$, and $b\in[0,1]$, if \[ \mathbb P(a\leftrightarrow T)\ge1-b\qquad(a\in A), \] then \begin{equation}\label{cmp:failure-bound} \mathbb P(o\leftrightarrow A,\ o\not\leftrightarrow T)\le b. \end{equation} The assertion therefore applies after any deterministic collection of bits has been fixed.

=== MANUSCRIPT: No percolation at criticality on quasi-transitive graphs [preprints/No-percolation-at-criticality-on-quasi-transitive-graphs-September-24-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that critical Bernoulli bond percolation has no infinite cluster on any infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with critical probability less than one. This resolves the bond form of the criticality conjecture of Benjamini and Schramm.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $G$ be an infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive undirected graph. If $\pc(G)<1$, then \[ \PP_{\pc(G)}(\text{there is an infinite open cluster})=0. \] Equivalently, $\PP_{\pc(G)}(|C_x|=\infty)=0$ for every vertex $x$.
[theorem] [Hutchcroft {\cite[Theorem~1.2]{Hutchcroft2016}}] \label{thm:exponential} If a connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph satisfies $\liminf_{r\to\infty}|B(x,r)|^{1/r}>1$, then it has almost surely no infinite Bernoulli bond cluster at its critical probability.
[proposition] \label{prop:two-cluster} Let $G$ be an infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph of subexponential growth. For every $p_*>0$ there is $C<\infty$ such that \[ \PP_p(H_s(e))\le C s^{-1/2} \qquad(e\in E(G),\ s\ge1,\ p_*\le p<1). \] The constant may depend on $G$ and $p_*$.
