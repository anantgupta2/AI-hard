SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
OPENAI SUMMARY: Constructs an integral complex affine fourfold $`X\not\cong\mathbb A^4`$ with $`X\times\mathbb A^1\cong\mathbb A^5`$, disproving affine-space cancellation over ℂ in dimension four. === MANUSCRIPT: An explicit failure of complex affine-space cancellation [preprints/An-explicit-failure-of-complex-affine-space-cancellation-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We construct an explicit integral complex affine fourfold X with $`X\times\mathbb A^1\cong\mathbb A^5`$ but $`X\not\cong\mathbb A^4`$. This gives a negative answer to Zariski's affine-space cancellation problem over ℂ in dimension four. The same construction disproves the Stable Coordinate Conjecture in ambient dimension five and yields smooth 𝔸3-fibrations over 𝔸1 and 𝔸2 that are not Zariski-locally trivial. These fibrations disprove the Dolgachev–Weisfeiler affine-fibration conjecture over these bases.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} The algebra $A$ in \eqref{eq:example} is an integral complex algebra of dimension four. For an independent variable $w$, \[ A[w]\simeq\kk\poly5, \qquad A\not\simeq\kk\poly4. \] Consequently the Zariski cancellation problem over the complex numbers has a negative answer in dimension four.
[corollary] [Failure of the Stable Coordinate Conjecture] \label{cor:stable-coordinate} The polynomial $H$ in \eqref{eq:example} is not a coordinate of $P=\kk[p,s,u,F,J]$, but it is a coordinate of $P[w]$ for one independent variable $w$. Thus the Stable Coordinate Conjecture over $\kk$ fails in ambient dimension five after one stabilization.
[proposition] \label{prop:stabilization} For the algebra \(A\) in \Cref{con:setup}, there is an algebraic \(\kk\)-isomorphism \[ A[w]\cong\kk\poly{5}. \]

- \maketitle \begin{abstract} We construct an explicit integral complex affine fourfold $X$ with $X\times\mathbb A^1\cong\mathbb A^5$ but $X\not\cong\mathbb A^4$. - The construction in \Cref{sec:construction,sec:degeneration} identifies an auxiliary subalgebra of $A$ with the presentation \[ S=\kk[p,x,y,z,u]/\bigl(xy-z(z+1)-p^3u\bigr), \qquad S[p^{-1}]=A[p^{-1}]. \] Under this inclusion, $p^2F$ and $p^2J$ belong to $S$. Define \[ R=S/pS=\kk[x,y,z,u]/\bigl(xy-z(z+1)\bigr),\qquad I=(\overline{p^2F},\overline{p^2J})\subset R, \] where the bars denote residues of elements of $S$ modulo $pS$. The order $\ord_p$ along $p=0$ in $S$ applies to every nonzero element of $A$. Its negative defines the \emph{valuation degree} $\deg h=-\ord_p(h)$ and a filtration of $A$. We prove the exact identification \[ G=\gr A=R[\tau,I/\tau^2]\subset R[\tau,\tau^{-1}], \qquad \deg\tau=-1,\qquad \deg R=0. \] Here $\tau$ is an indeterminate representing the initial form of $p$, and the right-hand side is generated by $R$, $\tau$, and all $i/\tau^2$ with $i\in I$. The coefficient inclusion is the one induced by $S\subset A$: the residue of $b\in S$ corresponds to its degree-zero class. This is an affine modification of $R[\tau]$ in the sense of Kaliman--Zaidenberg~\cite{KalimanZaidenberg}, and it is smooth.
- \paragraph{Organization.} \Cref{tab:ring-dictionary} records the rings used in the argument. \Cref{sec:construction} proves the stabilization. \Cref{sec:degeneration} constructs the filtration and identifies $G$. \Cref{sec:bundle} describes the torus bundle and proves the lifting statement. \Cref{sec:reduction} extracts the constrained derivation on $\Rt$, and \Cref{sec:rigidity} excludes it and completes the proof of \Cref{thm:main}. \Cref{sec:affine-fibrations} derives the two nontrivial affine-space fibrations. All locally nilpotent derivations and algebraic group actions in the proof are defined over $\kk$.
