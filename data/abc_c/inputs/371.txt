FAMILY 371: Stable blowup for the defocusing Schrödinger equation
SUBJECT: Partial differential equations
OPENAI SUMMARY: For a sufficiently large odd nonlinearity power, constructs a nonempty open set of initial data in $`H^k(\mathbb T^{12})`$, with k &gt; 8, whose solutions of the scalar defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation blow up in finite time. Thus finite-time blowup is stable under Sobolev perturbations in this supercritical regime.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Stable self-similar blowup for a supercritical defocusing Schrödinger equation on the torus [preprints/Stable-Self-Similar-Blowup-for-a-Supercritical-Defocusing-Schrodinger-Equation-on-the-Torus-September-24-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove stable self-similar finite-time blowup for a supercritical defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation on the twelve-dimensional torus. For a sufficiently large odd power, the blowup initial data contain a nonempty open set in a high Sobolev space. As a consequence, Gaussian Fourier initial data of arbitrarily high Sobolev regularity can have positive probability of finite-time blowup.
[theorem] [An open set of blowup data]\label{thm:blowup} There exist an odd integer $p\geq3$, a finite integer $k>8$, and a nonempty open set $\mathcal U\subset H^k(\mathbb T^{12};\mathbb C)$ with the following property. For every $u_0\in\mathcal U$, the unique local solution of \eqref{eq:nls} extends to \[ u\in C([0,T);H^k)\cap C^1([0,T);H^{k-2}) \] for some finite $T>0$, but has no continuous $H^k$ extension through $T$. More precisely, there are $x_*\in\mathbb T^{12}$ and $c_0>0$ such that \[ \lim_{t\uparrow T}(T-t)^{1/(p-1)}|u(t,x_*)|=c_0. \] The power can be chosen sufficiently large, and $k$ is then fixed after that choice.
[corollary] [Failure of universal Gaussian global existence] \label{cor:gaussian} For the fixed power $p$ and index $k$ in Theorem~\ref{thm:blowup}, and for every $\alpha>k+6$, the initial datum \begin{equation}\label{eq:gaussian} u_0^\omega(x)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z^{12}} g_n(\omega)\langle n\rangle^{-\alpha}e^{in\cdot x} \end{equation} belongs to $H^k$ almost surely and has positive probability of finite-time blowup. In particular, the following assertion is false: for every energy-supercritical pair $(d,p)$ there is a finite $\alpha_0(d,p)$ such that, for every $\alpha>\alpha_0(d,p)$ and every \[ d/2+2<m<\alpha-d/2, \] the local $H^m$ solution with the Gaussian datum extends almost surely to $C(\mathbb R;H^m(\mathbb T^d))$.

PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- \begin{abstract} We prove stable self-similar finite-time blowup for a supercritical defocusing nonlinear Schr\"odinger equation on the twelve-dimensional torus. For a sufficiently large odd power, the blowup initial data contain a nonempty open set in a high Sobolev space. As a consequence, Gaussian Fourier initial data of arbitrarily high Sobolev regularity can have positive probability of finite-time blowup. \end{abstract}
- Here the spatial scale is $(T-t)^{1/2}$, the amplitude is $(T-t)^{-1/(p-1)}$, and there is a logarithmic phase rotation. This is the meaning of self-similar, or type-I, blowup in this paper. The open neighborhood in Theorem~\ref{thm:blowup} is essential for Corollary~\ref{cor:gaussian}: full support of a Gaussian measure charges every nonempty open set, whereas a finite-codimension existence theorem alone does not give this conclusion. We prove the profile, spectral stability, periodic localization, and openness needed here directly; the cited blowup constructions provide historical and methodological context, not inputs to those proofs.
- All terminating decimals in this section denote exact rational numbers. Set \begin{equation}\label{free:disk} \begin{gathered} b_*=.33477606871236,\qquad Z_*=2.70506819293654,\qquad \delta=10^{-8},\\ \mathcal D=\{(b,Z)\in\mathbb R^2:(b-b_*)^2+(Z-Z_*)^2\le\delta^2\}. \end{gathered} \end{equation} The radius of the limiting core is $R=2\sqrt Z$. We prove the following statement, including its finite arithmetic ingredients. The definition of $H$ follows immediately below. The degree on $\mathcal D$ means Brouwer degree on its interior.
- The function $H(q,m,x)$ below is Tricomi's confluent hypergeometric function $U(q,m,x)$; see \cite[Equations~13.4.4 and~13.3.22]{DLMF}. We prove the regularized representation and the uniform symbol bounds needed on the imaginary rays.
- We prove the proposition in stages. The inner problem is solved on the whole parameter disk, as is the slow exterior problem. Only their final matching uses a choice of $(b_a,Z_a)$.
