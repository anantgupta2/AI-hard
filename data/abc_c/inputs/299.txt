FAMILY 299: The Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion and infinite tensor-power Jiang–Su stability
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
OPENAI SUMMARY: A nonzero unital separable complex C∗-algebra is Jiang–Su stable exactly when its norm central-sequence algebra has no characters, for every free ultrafilter. This answers the Kirchberg–Rørdam character question. Also, the infinite minimal tensor power of every such algebra without characters is Jiang–Su stable, answering the Dadarlat–Toms question.
HEADLINE (v2, semi-formal): ∀ nonzero unital separable complex C*-algebra A, ∀ free ultrafilter ω on ℕ: F_ω(A) has no characters ↔ A ≅ A ⊗_min Z

=== MANUSCRIPT: The Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion [preprints/The-Kirchberg-Rordam-character-criterion-September-25-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion: for every free ultrafilter on ℕ, a nonzero unital separable complex C∗-algebra absorbs the Jiang–Su algebra if and only if its norm central-sequence algebra has no characters. We also prove that the infinite minimal tensor power of every nonzero unital separable complex C∗-algebra without characters is $`\mathcal Z`$-stable and contains a unital copy of $`\mathcal Z`$.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $A$ be a nonzero unital separable complex $C^*$-algebra, and let $\omega$ be any free ultrafilter on $\N$. Then \[ F_\omega(A)\text{ has no characters} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\cong A\otmin\Z. \]
[theorem] \label{thm:finite} Let $D$ be a nonzero unital complex $C^*$-algebra without characters. There is an integer $m\ge1$ and a unital $*$-homomorphism \[ I(2,3)\longrightarrow D^{\otimes_{\max}m}, \] where \[ I(2,3)=\{f\in C([0,1],M_2\otimes M_3): f(0)\in M_2\otimes1_3,\ f(1)\in1_2\otimes M_3\}. \]
[corollary] [Infinite minimal tensor powers]\label{cor:infinite-tensor} Let $D$ be a nonzero unital separable complex $C^*$-algebra without characters, and write \[ B=D^{\otimes_{\min}\infty} :=\varinjlim_{n\ge1} \bigl(D^{\otimes_{\min}n},\,x\longmapsto x\otimes1_D\bigr). \] Then $B\cong B\otmin\Z$, and $B$ contains a unital copy of $\Z$.

- Kirchberg and R\o rdam asked whether the absence of characters on $F_\omega(A)$ characterizes Jiang--Su absorption among unital separable $C^*$-algebras \cite[Question~3.1]{KR}. Their work relates this question to tensor powers of algebras without characters \cite[Question~3.7 and Proposition~3.9]{KR}. The character question also appears as Problem~LXXX in the recent collection of Schafhauser, Tikuisis, and White \cite[Section~22]{STW}. We prove the proposed characterization.
- The bridge from full orthogonal elements to a homomorphism from the matrix cone $C_0((0,1])\otimes M_2$ that sends $t\mapsto t e_{11}$ to a full element already appears in the proof of \cite[Proposition~6.1]{KR}. We give a complete construction using representation-stable tensor labels. A matrix-cone map can equivalently be viewed as a completely positive contractive order-zero map on a matrix algebra, meaning one that preserves orthogonality of positive elements, as in Winter and Zacharias \cite[Corollary~4.1]{WZ}; below we construct the cone homomorphism directly. Fullness of its first matrix corner is not by itself the dimension-drop criterion. The additional task is to produce two \emph{exactly equivalent} orthogonal positive contractions, of the form $v^*v$ and $vv^*$ for a single element $v$, and compare their unit defect with a positive cutoff of one of them. This is the input to the stable-rank-free implication of R\o rdam and Winter \cite[Proposition~5.1, (ii)$\Rightarrow$(iv)]{RW}, whose needed form is proved explicitly in Section~\ref{sec:rw}.
- \subsection{Proof overview}
- \begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:kr}(1)] For any separable subalgebra $E\subset F_\omega(A)$, we construct a unital map from $D$ into $F_\omega(A)\cap E'$. Choose a countable dense unital $*$-subalgebra $D_0\subset D$ over $\mathbb Q+i\mathbb Q$. For every $x\in D_0$, choose a bounded lift $(a_l^x)_l$ in $A$ with $\sup_l\|a_l^x\|\le2\|x\|$, using the zero lift for $x=0$ and the constant unit lift for $x=1$. Such choices follow from the quotient norm, by truncating coordinate norms without changing the ultrapower class. All additive, rational-complex scalar, multiplicative and adjoint relation defects tend to zero in norm along $\omega$.
