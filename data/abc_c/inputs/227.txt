FAMILY 227: Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition
SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
OPENAI SUMMARY: For zero-field Gaussian SK heat-bath dynamics with rate-one updates per spin, proves worst-start cutoff on the $`\log n`$ scale for fixed $`0\le\beta\lt 1`$, mixing time $`n^{2/3+o(1)}`$ at β = 1, and stretched-exponential mixing from a Gibbs-sampled fixed starting configuration for β &gt; 1, in probability over disorder. At criticality, rescaled stationary and quench autocorrelation processes have universal random limits for Gaussian and Rademacher disorder; the quench limit relaxes to the stationary limit.
HEADLINE (v2, semi-formal): ∀ β∈[0,1), ∃ λ(β)>0 deterministic: with t_n = log n/(2λ(β)), ∀ε∈(0,1): worst-start TV distance d_n((1−ε)t_n)→1 and d_n((1+ε)t_n)→0 in probability over Gaussian SK disorder (cutoff)
