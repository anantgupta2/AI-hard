=== MANUSCRIPT: Relative generation and the generator problem for finite factors [preprints/Relative-generation-and-the-generator-problem-for-finite-factors-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that every type II1 factor with separable predual is generated by one operator, equivalently by two self-adjoint operators. Combined with the established direct-integral reduction to this case, this gives an affirmative solution of the generator problem for von Neumann algebras with separable predual.
[theorem] \label{thm:relative-generation} Let $P\subset M$ be an irreducible inclusion of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors, with $M$ of separable predual. The set \[ \{u\in\mathcal U(M):W^*(P,u)=M\} \] is a dense $G_\delta$ subset of the unitary group $\mathcal U(M)$ for the trace $2$-norm topology.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Every type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor $M$ with separable predual is generated by two self-adjoint elements. Equivalently, there is an $x\in M$ such that $M=W^*(x)$.
[theorem] [Popa's independence theorem] \label{thm:popa-independence} Let $P\subset M$ be an irreducible inclusion of $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors, and put $\mathbf Q=P^\omega\subset M^\omega=\mathbf M$. For every linear subspace $X\subset\mathbf M$ that is separable in $2$-norm and satisfies $\tau(x)=0$ for all $x\in X$, there is a diffuse abelian von Neumann algebra $A\subset\mathbf Q$ such that \begin{equation} \label{eq:popa-independence} \tau(x_0a_1x_1\cdots a_mx_m)=0 \end{equation} whenever $m\geq1$, $x_0\in X\cup\{1\}$, $x_1,\ldots,x_m\in X$, and $a_1,\ldots,a_m\in A$ have trace zero.

- We prove this assertion for type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors by first establishing a relative generation theorem. For a finite factor with normalized trace $\tau$, put $\norm{x}_2=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}$. An inclusion $P\subset M$ is \emph{irreducible} when $P'\cap M=\mathbb C1$. Write $\mathcal U(M)$ for the unitary group of $M$.
- \paragraph{Earlier work.} The type~I, hyperfinite, and properly infinite cases of the generator problem were established by Pearcy, Suzuki and Sait\^o, and Wogen, respectively~\cite{Pearcy1962,SuzukiSaito1963,Wogen1969}; see also the historical discussion in~\cite[Section~3]{Sherman2012}. Willig's direct-integral theorem reduces the general question to type~$\mathrm{II}_1$ factors~\cite[Corollary~2]{Willig1974}. Consequently, Theorem~\ref{thm:main} also gives single generation of every von Neumann algebra with separable predual. This last consequence uses the established direct-integral reduction, whereas our proof below concerns finite factors.
- \paragraph{The main estimate and the proof.} Our argument combines Popa's free-independence construction in tracial ultraproducts~\cite{Popa1995,Popa2014} with a perturbation whose effect accumulates along a long word. Given a unitary $u$ and a target unitary $z$, we coordinate a perturbation of $u$ across its repeated occurrences in a word with coefficients from $P$. With a small parameter $\alpha$ fixed, the perturbation has $2$-norm of order $\alpha/\sqrt\ell$, where $\ell$ is the number of occurrences. The resulting word has correlation with a test vector $\xi$ equal to $(\alpha/2)\tau(\xi^*z)$ up to an error of order $\alpha^2\norm{\xi}_2+1/\ell$.
