FAMILY 083: Hilbert transforms along Lipschitz directions
SUBJECT: Real and complex analysis
OPENAI SUMMARY: Proves a uniform strong L2 bound for the planar Hilbert transform along any Lipschitz unit vector field, at integration lengths bounded by an absolute multiple of its reciprocal Lipschitz constant. The estimate is uniform over inner truncations and yields an L2-bounded principal-value operator, establishing Stein's weak-type conjecture at this short scale.

=== MANUSCRIPT: A uniform Hilbert transform estimate for Lipschitz directions [preprints/A-uniform-Hilbert-transform-estimate-for-Lipschitz-directions-September-25-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove a uniform L2 bound for the Hilbert transform along a Lipschitz unit vector field in the plane, with integration restricted to a fixed absolute multiple of the reciprocal Lipschitz seminorm. The bound is uniform in the inner truncation and holds for fields depending on both coordinates. This gives an affirmative answer to Stein's weak-$`(2,2)`$ conjecture.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There are absolute constants \(a_*\in(0,1/2)\) and \(C_*<\infty\) such that every Lipschitz field \(v:\R^2\to S^1\) with \(\Lip(v)\le1\) satisfies \[ \sup_{0<\epsilon<a_*} \norm{H_{v,a_*}^{\epsilon}f}_{L^2(\R^2)} \le C_*\norm{f}_{L^2(\R^2)} \qquad(f\in\mathcal S(\R^2)). \] Its symmetric principal value therefore satisfies \[ \norm{H_{v,a_*}f}_2\le C_*\norm f_2, \qquad \big|\{x:|H_{v,a_*}f(x)|>\lambda\}\big| \le C_*^2\lambda^{-2}\norm f_2^2 \quad(\lambda>0). \]
[theorem] [Lacey--Li]\label{red:kakeya} Let \(v:\R^2\to S^1\) be Lipschitz and \(0<\delta<1\). Restrict to rectangles satisfying \[ L(R)\le \frac1{100\Lip(v)},\qquad \abs{R\cap v^{-1}(\operatorname{EX}(R))}\ge\delta\abs R, \] where the first restriction is void when \(\Lip(v)=0\). Then \[ \mathcal M_{v,\delta}h(z) =\sup_R\frac{\one_R(z)}{\abs R}\int_R\abs h \quad\text{satisfies}\quad \norm{\mathcal M_{v,\delta}h}_{L^{2,\infty}} \le C\delta^{-1/2}\norm h_2. \] Here the supremum is tested at every point of \(R\), and the constant is independent of \(v\) and \(\delta\).
[theorem] [Restricted estimate on a vertical band] \label{thm:band-restricted} For the fixed absolute constants in \eqref{red:local-operator}, there is an absolute \(C\) such that, for every field \(u\) with \(\abs u\le1\) and \(\Lip(u)\le C_{\rm Lip}\), every \(0<\epsilon<a\), every dyadic \(w\), and every vertical band cutoff \(P_w\), \begin{equation}\label{eq:src-1} \abs{\langle H_{u,a}^{\epsilon}P_w f,g\rangle} \le C\bigl(1+\abs{\log(|F|/|E|)}\bigr)^{-3} \sqrt{|F||E|} \end{equation} whenever \(F,E\subset I\times\R\) have positive finite measure, \(\abs f\le\one_F\), \(\abs g\le\one_E\), and \(\abs I\le b_0\).
