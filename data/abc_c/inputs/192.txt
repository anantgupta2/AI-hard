FAMILY 192: Boolean functions violate the square-root degree bound by arbitrary factors
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
OPENAI SUMMARY: Disproves the proposed square-root bound relating a Boolean function's linear Fourier coefficients to its polynomial degree. For every C &gt; 0, there is a sign-valued Boolean function f with $`\sum_i\widehat f(\{i\})\gt C\sqrt{\deg(f)}`$. Thus its total signed correlation with individual input bits can exceed the proposed bound by an arbitrary factor.
HEADLINE (v2, semi-formal): ∀ C>0 ∃ n ∃ f:{-1,1}^n→{-1,1} nonconstant: Σ_i f̂({i}) > C·√deg(f)

ABSTRACT: We disprove the Gopalan–Servedio square-root conjecture, even up to an arbitrary constant factor. For every real C &gt; 0, there is a nonconstant Boolean function $`f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}`$ on a finite sign cube such that
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every real \(C>0\), there are a positive integer \(n\) and a nonconstant Boolean function \(f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}\) such that \[ \sum_{i=1}^n\widehat f(\{i\})>C\sqrt{\deg(f)}. \] Consequently, \[ \sup_{\substack{n\ge1,\ f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}\\\deg(f)>0}} \frac{\sum_i|\widehat f(\{i\})|}{\sqrt{\deg(f)}}=\infty. \]
