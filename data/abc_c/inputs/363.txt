ABSTRACT: We prove nonuniqueness for the three-dimensional periodic hard-sphere Boltzmann equation among global entropy solutions satisfying exact local conservation of mass, momentum, and kinetic energy. We construct one nonnegative initial density with bounded velocity support and finite mass, energy, and absolute entropy that gives rise to two distinct such solutions. Both are strongly continuous in L1, and their collision gain and loss terms are integrable with every polynomial velocity weight on every bounded time interval.

- \subsection{Organization}
- The sheet gain bound controls total normalized mass. We next control its velocity tails and its behavior as the outgoing velocity approaches the axis. The key distinction is that the hot input can concentrate transversely on the axis while its axial marginal remains uniformly non-atomic.
- \begin{remark}[A sharp axial endpoint]\label{ker:matching-axis} A single normalized gain need not become small as the outgoing velocity approaches the axis. Take $c=\delta_{(0,0,u)}$ and $v_\varepsilon=(\varepsilon,0,u)$. The collision plane is $\xi_1=\varepsilon$, and \[ \frac{P(c,H)(v_\varepsilon)}{H(v_\varepsilon)} =\frac{2}{\varepsilon H(v_\varepsilon)} \int_{\R^2}H(\varepsilon,y,z)\,\dd y\,\dd z. \] The regular part of $W(\varepsilon,y,z)$ contributes $O(\varepsilon^{q-1})$. In its singular part substitute $y=\varepsilon s$. Dominated convergence, with dominating function $(1+s^2)^{-q/2}e^{-z^2}\langle z\rangle^{-m}$, gives \[ \lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0} \frac{P(c,H)(v_\varepsilon)}{H(v_\varepsilon)} =2a_q e^{u^2}\langle u\rangle^m \int_{\R}e^{-z^2}\langle z\rangle^{-m}\,\dd z>0. \] The corresponding axial input marginal converges to the absolutely continuous measure \[ 2a_q e^{u^2}\langle u\rangle^m e^{-z^2}\langle z\rangle^{-m}\,\dd z. \] Thus transverse concentration is compatible with the uniform axial interval estimate. This is the reason for using two successive gains in the axial part of the later nonconcentration argument. \end{remark}
