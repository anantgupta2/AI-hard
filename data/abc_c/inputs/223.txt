ABSTRACT: For every fixed $`1\le q\lt 4`$, critical square-lattice random-cluster Dobrushin interfaces converge as ordered curves to chordal $`\mathop{\mathrm{SLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$, where $`\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)`$, confirming the Rohde–Schramm prediction in this parameter range. This holds in every bounded Jordan domain under uniform marked boundary approximation. The complete nested plane loop collections converge to whole-plane $`\mathop{\mathrm{CLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$ in a spherical matching topology retaining multiplicities and traversals. At q = 1 we obtain Cardy's formula for square-lattice bond percolation with free boundary edges.
[theorem] [Interfaces and all nested loops] \label{intro:main} For every fixed real $1\le q<4$, the following assertions hold. \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item For every bounded Jordan domain $D$ with distinct boundary marks $a,b$, and every approximation satisfying \eqref{intro:marked-jordan}, the Dobrushin strand $\eta_n$ converges in law, in \eqref{intro:curve-metric}, to chordal $\SLE_{\kappa(q)}$ from $a$ to $b$ in $D$. \item Under the specified free infinite-volume law, $\Gamma_\delta$ converges in law as $\delta\downarrow0$ to the nested whole-plane $\CLE_{\kappa(q)}$, in the spherical collection topology just defined. The limit is M\"obius invariant. \end{enumerate} Both conclusions hold along every mesh sequence. No uniformity in $q$ is asserted.

- Rohde and Schramm formulated a square-lattice random-cluster interface conjecture at the self-dual parameter for every fixed $0<q<4$ \cite[Conjecture~9.7 and the following discussion]{RohdeSchramm2005}. They motivated the proposed relation between $q$ and the SLE parameter using the dimensions of cluster perimeters predicted by Saleur and Duplantier \cite{SaleurDuplantier1987}. Schramm later restated the problem in his collection of questions about conformally invariant scaling limits \cite[Problem~2.6]{Schramm2006}. We prove the predicted limit for every fixed $0<q<1$ in smooth Jordan domains, with the finite wired/free law, lattice approximation, and curve topology specified below. This gives the smooth-domain formulation of the prediction; the concrete unit-square formulation of Rohde and Schramm has a different boundary regularity. The argument starts from the finite law at the self-dual parameter and does not require prior identification of an infinite-volume critical point.
- \subsection{Proof strategy}
- A network connection may use identified boundary vertices, whereas an actual path must follow open physical edges. The rectangle and sector arguments first control network tests and then actual paths in sectors. To pass to an irregular disk, we descend through crosscuts of decreasing diameter until we find either a bounded physical bridge or a route with clear interior access to both walls. The local topology retains the side of every boundary approach, including the two banks of a slit. Separated neighboring crosscuts leave enough uninspected space to extend an exposed actual crossing through each end gap. This proves the pure-network and actual-path decay of Theorems~\ref{thm:pure-disk} and~\ref{thm:actual-disk}. Such irregular disks arise during exploration even from the initial simple polygons of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
