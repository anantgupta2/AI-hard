FAMILY 289: Strong Kadison–Kastler stability and its spatial boundaries
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
OPENAI SUMMARY: Proves that sufficiently close unital von Neumann algebras on the same Hilbert space are conjugate by a unitary arbitrarily close to the identity, with a universal tolerance in the operator-norm distance between unit balls. Counterexamples show that near-identity conjugacy fails for one-sided near inclusions, and that arbitrarily close norm-separable C∗-algebras need not be ambiently unitarily conjugate.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability [preprints/Universal-strong-Kadison-Kastler-stability-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that sufficiently close unital von Neumann algebras are conjugate by a unitary arbitrarily close to the identity. The tolerance depends only on the prescribed distance of that unitary from the identity, uniformly over all algebras, representations, and Hilbert spaces. This resolves the strong Kadison–Kastler conjecture.
[theorem] [Strong Kadison--Kastler stability]\label{thm:main} For every $\eps>0$ there is $\delta=\delta(\eps)>0$ such that the following holds. If $H$ is any complex Hilbert space and $M,N\subseteq\B(H)$ are unital von Neumann algebras with identity $I_H$ and $d(M,N)<\delta$, there is a unitary $u\in\B(H)$ satisfying \[ uMu^*=N,\qquad \norm{u-I_H}<\eps. \]
[theorem] [Amenable near inclusions]\label{thm:amenable} Let $R,Q\subseteq\B(H)$ be von Neumann algebras with identity $I$, with $R$ amenable. If $R\subset_\gamma Q$ and $\gamma<1/100$, there is a unitary $v\in(R\cup Q)''$ such that \[ vRv^*\subseteq Q,\qquad \norm{v-I}\le150\gamma. \] If also $Q\subset_\gamma R$ and $\gamma<1/101$, the same conclusion holds with $vRv^*=Q$.
[proposition] [UCP criterion]\label{prop:ucp} Let $P,Q$ be von Neumann algebras, let $\rho:P\to\B(H)$ be a faithful normal unital representation, and let $Q\subseteq\B(H)$ have the same identity. Suppose that $F:P\to Q$ is UCP and \[ \norm{F-\rho}_{\cb}\le e<10^{-3},\qquad \sup_{y\in Q_1}\dist(y,\rho(P)_1)\le g,\qquad g+e<1. \] Then there is a normal unital $*$-isomorphism $\theta:P\to Q$ and a unitary $u\in\B(H)$ such that \[ \norm{\theta-\rho}_{\cb}\le32e,\qquad u\rho(x)u^*=\theta(x)\ (x\in P),\qquad \norm{u-I}\le128e. \] The map $F$ need not be normal.

- For a von Neumann algebra $M\subseteq\B(H)$, write $M_1$ for its closed operator-norm unit ball. The Kadison--Kastler distance between two such algebras is \begin{equation}\label{eq:kk-definition} d(M,N)=\max\left\{ \sup_{x\in M_1}\inf_{y\in N_1}\norm{x-y},\quad \sup_{y\in N_1}\inf_{x\in M_1}\norm{x-y}\right\}. \end{equation} Kadison and Kastler introduced this metric in 1972 \cite{KadisonKastler1972}. Their perturbation problem asks whether closeness of the unit balls forces spatial isomorphism, implemented by a unitary close to the identity. We prove the uniform form of this assertion.

Thus every central element is scalar. The ultraproduct of matrix factors whose sizes tend to infinity is diffuse, since their projections have traces approaching any prescribed number in $[0,1]$. ABSTRACT: We give a negative answer to the unrestricted small-spatial-embedding problem for one-sided near inclusions of von Neumann algebras. On separable complex Hilbert spaces, we construct pairs of unital von Neumann algebras with common identity whose one-sided gaps tend to zero. Spatial embeddings of the source into the target exist, but every implementing unitary stays a fixed positive distance from the identity. The obstruction therefore concerns small implementing unitaries, rather than the existence of spatial embeddings.
