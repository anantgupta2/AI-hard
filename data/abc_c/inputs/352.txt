ABSTRACT: We construct a smooth Kähler metric in the Fubini–Study class of $`\mathbb{CP}^{10}`$ whose Calabi flow develops unbounded scalar curvature in finite time. This disproves Chen's smooth long-time existence conjecture, even in a class containing a constant-scalar-curvature metric.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is a smooth \(U(10)\)-invariant K\"ahler form \(\omega_0\) on \(\CP^{10}\), in the class \([\omega_{\mathrm{FS}}]\), whose maximal smooth Calabi flow has existence interval \([0,T_*)\) with \(0<T_*<\infty\). There are a point \(p\in\CP^{10}\) and a constant \(a>0\) such that \[ S(\omega(t))(p)\sim a(T_*-t)^{-1/2}\qquad\text{as }t\uparrow T_*. \]
[corollary] [Products with stationary factors]\label{cor:product-singularities} Let \(\omega(t)\), \(T_*\), \(p\), and \(a\) be as in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, and let \((Y,\eta)\) be a compact connected K\"ahler manifold with \(\eta\) of constant scalar curvature. With \(\pi_X\) and \(\pi_Y\) the projections from \(\CP^{10}\times Y\), the maximal smooth Calabi flow from \(\Omega_0=\pi_X^*\omega_0+\pi_Y^*\eta\) is \[ \Omega(t)=\pi_X^*\omega(t)+\pi_Y^*\eta, \qquad 0\leq t<T_*. \] For every \(y\in Y\), \(S(\Omega(t))(p,y)\sim a(T_*-t)^{-1/2}\). In particular, finite-time smooth Calabi-flow singularities occur in every complex dimension \(n\geq10\), on \(\CP^{10}\times\CP^{n-10}\), in a class containing a K\"ahler--Einstein metric.

We use the scalar-curvature convention \(S(\omega)=\operatorname{tr}_\omega\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)\). The Calabi flow is \begin{equation}\label{eq:calabi-flow} \omega(t)=\omega_0+i\partial\bar\partial\phi(t)>0, \qquad \phi(0)=0,\qquad \partial_t\phi=S(\omega(t))-\bar S, \end{equation} where \(\bar S\) is the average scalar curvature, determined by the K\"ahler class. Calabi introduced this flow to seek canonical metrics within a K\"ahler class~\cite{Calabi1982}. - The singularity is detected by one value of \(H\). At the fixed affine origin \(p\), the scalar-curvature identity is \[ S(t,p)=110\bigl(1-H(t,0)\bigr). \] We construct a smooth positive profile \(G\), bounded above and away from zero, with \(G(0)=1\) and \(G'(0)<0\), and set \[ F(t,x)=\rho^{-1}h(x/\rho),\qquad h(y)=\frac{G(y)-1}{y},\qquad \rho=\sqrt{-t},\quad t<0. \] Then \(F(t,0)=\rho^{-1}G'(0)\). Our task is to find an exact compact solution \(H=F+V\) for which positivity persists and \[ V(t,0)=O(\rho^{-1+\delta})\qquad\text{for some }\delta>0. \] The correction is therefore smaller than the leading term at the origin, preserving the desired scalar-curvature divergence. A smooth interior time slice of this solution will be the initial metric; no initial metric needs to be fixed before the construction.
- Section~\ref{sec:profile} constructs \(G\) so that \(F\) solves the scale-invariant flat equation exactly. The profile is obtained by matching a two-parameter analytic solution at the origin to a half-line solution approaching a positive constant. A finite exact-arithmetic calculation, with analytic enclosure and nonlinear error bounds, certifies the matching inequalities. This uses the framework of validated Taylor integration surveyed in~\cite{NedialkovJacksonCorliss1999}. The finite argument and its complete executable certificate appear in the appendices. The resulting profile also has a differentiable expansion at infinity; this expansion controls the compact error away from the shrinking core.
- We construct the profile used in the concentration argument. Its construction has two parts: a finite shooting estimate, proved by rational enclosures in Appendix~\ref{cert:section}, and an analytic construction on a half-line. The finite calculation belongs to the established framework of validated Taylor integration for ordinary differential equations~\cite{NedialkovJacksonCorliss1999}. Here the integer interval recurrences and complex-disk remainder bounds are given explicitly. The half-line solution is constructed by a contraction, whereas the final matching argument uses only continuity of the shooting map.
