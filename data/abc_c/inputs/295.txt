=== MANUSCRIPT: Vanishing of higher bounded Hochschild cohomology [preprints/Vanishing-of-higher-bounded-Hochschild-cohomology-September-23-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree at least two on a complex von Neumann algebra, with values in the algebra itself, has a bounded primitive. Together with the established degree-one inner-derivation theorem, this resolves the Kadison–Ringrose cohomology conjecture positively.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(M\) be a complex von Neumann algebra and let \(k\geq2\). Every \(f\in C_b^k(M,M)\) satisfying \(df=0\) has the form \(f=dg\) for some \(g\in C_b^{k-1}(M,M)\). Consequently \(H_b^k(M,M)=0\).
[proposition] [Classical reductions]\label{prop:classical} Let \(M\) be a von Neumann algebra and \(k\geq2\). \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item If \(f\in C_b^k(M,M)\) is a cocycle, there are a separately normal bounded cocycle \(f_0\) and a bounded \((k-1)\)-cochain \(b\) such that \(f=f_0+db\). \item If \(M\) has no type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) central summand, then \(H_b^k(M,M)=0\). \end{enumerate}

PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- \maketitle \begin{abstract} We prove that every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree at least two on a complex von Neumann algebra, with values in the algebra itself, has a bounded primitive. Together with the established degree-one inner-derivation theorem, this resolves the Kadison--Ringrose cohomology conjecture positively. \end{abstract}
- We obtain the needed module identity by averaging over a single unitary random walk. Averaging one cocycle input first gives only a harmonic map; the main new input is a Liouville theorem that makes such a map commute with right multiplication. Suppose that \(M\) is a type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) algebra with separable predual and faithful normal tracial state \(\tau\), and write \(\norm{x}_2=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}\). Let $\U(M)$ denote its unitary group. We construct a symmetric, countably supported probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\U(M)\), with a \(2\)-dense set of atoms, such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-harmonic} T(x)=\E_{U\sim\mu}\bigl[T(xU)U^*\bigr] \quad(x\in M) \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad T(x)=T(1)x. \end{equation} Here \(T:M\to M\) is any bounded linear map that is \(2\)-norm continuous on each operator-norm ball. No positivity or complete-boundedness assumption is imposed. The measure is fixed before the harmonic map is chosen.
- The proof of \eqref{eq:intro-harmonic} combines probabilistic and algebraic arguments. The finite free configurations come from Popa's construction of free-independent sequences~\cite{Popa1995}, in the formulation of his later ultraproduct independence theorem~\cite{Popa2014}. These give finite unitary configurations with prescribed small word traces. A hierarchy of increasingly rare, increasingly large sets of such unitaries gives a single random walk whose long independent products approach free Haar joint moments along a subsequence. Only moment convergence is required.
- Second, we use the classical harmonic-martingale construction \cite[p.~415]{Furstenberg1963} to obtain an \(L^2(M,\tau)\)-valued martingale. A nonconstant limit would produce free Haar unitaries \(g_1,g_2,\ldots\) in a tracial ultrapower and distinct \(a,b\in M\). The coordinatewise lift of \(T\) to that ultrapower sends each nonempty signed word \(w\) with pairwise distinct unsigned indices to \(aw\) or \(bw\), according to the sign of its first letter; here \(a,b\) are identified with their constant images. We prove that these identities are impossible for a bounded map with the stated continuity. The argument averages free generators, discards repeated surviving indices using Haagerup's free-group length estimate~\cite{Haagerup1979}, and passes to a unitary polar part. Positive and negative powers of that unitary would then carry different left multipliers. Uniformly bounded sine sums and a spectral partition force the multipliers to agree. This rigidity statement, proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:rigidity}, is independent of the cocycle problem.
- Throughout this section, $(M,\tau)$ is a finite von Neumann algebra of type $\mathrm{II}_1$ with separable predual and faithful normal tracial state. In particular, $M$ may have a nontrivial center. We construct a single symmetric probability measure on $\U(M)$ whose independent long products approach the joint moments of free Haar unitaries along a subsequence. The construction combines finite approximation to freeness with increasingly rare increments chosen at successive stages.
