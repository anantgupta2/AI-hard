=== MANUSCRIPT: Nontrivial Markov Type Forces Superreflexivity [preprints/Nontrivial-Markov-Type-Forces-Superreflexivity-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that every real Banach space with Markov type p &gt; 1 is superreflexive. Together with the known converse, this characterizes superreflexivity by nontrivial Markov type. This answers Naor's question: every real Banach space with nontrivial Markov type admits an equivalent uniformly smooth norm.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Every real Banach space with Markov type $p$ for at least one $p>1$ is superreflexive.
[proposition] [A finite walk with linear displacement]\label{prop:walks} Let $N$ be a spreading invariant norm on $c_{00}$ for which merging adjacent entries is contractive. For every integer $n\geq1$, there are a finite stationary reversible marked chain and an odd labeling $g$ in a finite coordinate span such that \[ N(g(e))=1\qquad\text{for every edge }e, \] and, for the first $n$ edges $E_1,\ldots,E_n$ of the stationary chain, \begin{equation}\label{eq:unit-walk} \Prob\left\{N\left(\sum_{i=1}^n g(E_i)\right) \geq\frac n{24}\right\}\geq\frac3{20}. \end{equation}
[proposition] [Reversible chart transport]\label{prop:chart-transport} Let $\Lambda$ be a finite ordered set, and let $T_s:D_s\to R_s$ be order-preserving bijections between its subsets, indexed by a nonempty finite set $\cS$ with a fixed-point-free involution $s\mapsto s^{-1}$ and $T_{s^{-1}}=T_s^{-1}$. For every $\epsilon>0$, there are an integer $B$ and a finite stationary reversible marked chain on charts $C:\Lambda\to\{1,\ldots,B\}$ whose successive edge letters are independent and uniform in $\cS$. At every stationary step $(C,s,C')$, \[ C'(a)=C(T_s(a))\quad(a\in D_s) \] holds simultaneously with probability at least $1-\epsilon$.
PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- \begin{abstract} We prove that every real Banach space with Markov type $p>1$ is superreflexive. Together with the known converse, this characterizes superreflexivity by nontrivial Markov type. This answers Naor's question: every real Banach space with nontrivial Markov type admits an equivalent uniformly smooth norm. \end{abstract}
- \paragraph{Proof strategy.} We argue by contradiction. The James--Enflo characterization supplies a nonreflexive space finitely representable in a nonsuperreflexive one. From it we construct a spreading norm $N$ on finite sequences for which summing consecutive entries is contractive. This is a concrete version of the equal-sign-additive construction of Brunel and Sucheston \cite{BrunelSucheston1974,BrunelSucheston1975,BrunelSucheston}. Equal-sign additivity means that merging adjacent coefficients of the same sign preserves the norm. The finite-representability formulation and related graph-embedding applications appear in \cite{Ostrovskii,OstrovskiiRandrianantoanina}. We include the precise version and its finite-representability proof in Appendix~\ref{app:norm}.
- All chains used in this deduction are finite and start in their reversible stationary distributions. Their sizes and the embeddings into $X$ may depend on $n$; only $K$ must be uniform. It remains to construct the walks in Proposition~\ref{prop:walks}. The proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:ordered-norm}, deferred until Appendix~\ref{app:norm}, completes the other construction used here.
