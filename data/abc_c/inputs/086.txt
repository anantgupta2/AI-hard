=== MANUSCRIPT: An L3 bound for the trilinear Hilbert transform [preprints/An-L3-bound-for-the-trilinear-Hilbert-transform-October-5-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that the trilinear Hilbert transform with fixed slopes 1, 2, 3 maps $`L^3(\mathbb R)\times L^3(\mathbb R)\times L^3(\mathbb R)`$ to $`L^1(\mathbb R)`$, resolving this case of the trilinear Hilbert transform conjecture.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is a finite constant $C$ such that \[ \norm{T(f_1,f_2,f_3)}_{L^1(\R)} \le C\prod_{j=1}^3\norm{f_j}_{L^3(\R)} \qquad(f_1,f_2,f_3\in\mathcal S(\R)). \] Consequently $T$ has a unique bounded trilinear extension from $L^3(\R)\times L^3(\R)\times L^3(\R)$ to $L^1(\R)$.
[theorem] [Uniform local estimate]\label{thm:local} For some $q\in(2,3)$, the constants $C_N(q)$ are bounded independently of $N$.
[proposition] [Normalized absorption estimate]\label{prop:normalized} For a suitable fixed $q\in(2,3)$ and every sufficiently small fixed $c>0$, there is an $A<\infty$, independent of $N$, with the following property. Suppose a finite rooted subtree has root $I_*$, all summation cells lie in at most $N$ consecutive depths, and \[ \norm{f_j}_{q,I}\le1 \qquad(0\le j\le3, I\text{ in the subtree}). \] Then \[ \sum_{I\text{ a summation cell}}|I|\,|H_I(f)| \le (A+cC_N)|I_*|. \]
