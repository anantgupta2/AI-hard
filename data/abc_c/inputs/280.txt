=== MANUSCRIPT: Strongly rational unitary vertex operator algebras and conformal nets [preprints/Strongly-rational-unitary-vertex-operator-algebras-and-conformal-nets-September-25-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: Every simple unitary strongly rational vertex operator algebra generates a completely rational conformal net. All its simple grading-restricted modules are unitarizable, its canonical fusion forms are positive, and the Carpi–Weiner–Xu functor gives a braided unitary tensor equivalence from its grading-restricted finite-length module category onto the finite-index sectors of the net. Gui's extension theorems then identify normalized irreducible finite-index local extensions of these nets with simple CFT-type conformal extensions of the vertex operator algebras.

[theorem] \label{thm:energy} Let $V$ be a simple unitary strongly rational vertex operator algebra, and let $W$ be a grading-restricted unitary $V$-module. Every vertex operator on $W$ satisfies polynomial energy bounds. More precisely, if $a\in V_d$ is quasi-primary, $d\geq2$, and $(-1)^d\Theta a=a$, then there are constants $C_a>0$ and $p_a\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$ such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:optimal-vertex-bound} \|a[m]\xi\| \leq C_a(1+|m|)^{p_a} \|(1+L_0)^{d-1}\xi\|, \qquad m\in\mathbb Z,\quad \xi\in W. \end{equation} Weight-one fields and the Virasoro field satisfy energy bounds of at most linear order. The constants may depend on $V$, $W$, and $a$.
[corollary] \label{cor:locality} Every simple unitary strongly rational VOA $V$ is CKLW-strongly local. Its smeared vertex operators generate an irreducible conformal net $A_V$.
PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- For loop-group models based on $SU(N)$, Wassermann computed Connes fusion using bounded primary fields and their transport relations \cite{Wassermann}. Gui's transport identities and Tener's transport-form framework express candidate VOA fusion inner products through ordinary vertex operators \cite{GuiUnitaryII,TenerFusion}; Gui further developed their canonical normalization and geometric positivity \cite{GuiPositivity}. Tener's positivity theorem for regular VOAs assumes a unitary structure on the algebraic fusion product and a corresponding net representation of the source module \cite[Theorem~4.4]{TenerFusion}. Our argument constructs positivity on the full algebraic fusion object before presuming that it is unitarizable. To reach the remaining simple modules, we use Carnahan--Miyamoto's fixed-point regularity and the Barron--Dong--Mason permutation construction \cite{CarnahanMiyamoto,BDM}. The required permutation product rests on the extension tensor structures of Creutzig--Kanade--McRae and McRae \cite{CKMExtensions,McRaeTwisted} and the one-slot intertwiner correspondence of Dong--Li--Xu--Yu \cite[Theorems~5.5 and~5.9]{DLXY}.
- \subsection{Proof strategy}
- The central ordering issue is that a positive form on $V$ does not initially give positive forms on its modules or fusion products. We first work with modules already carrying such forms. An intertwining operator is viewed as a charged field, with a charge module, a source module and a target module. Its creation case has the vacuum $V$ as source. This supplies a starting point for the following sequence. \begin{enumerate} \item \emph{From $C_2$-cofiniteness to a net.} In Section~\ref{sec:energy}, a finite generating family controls all normal products, and positivity of certain zero modes gives an exponential starting bound. Two contraction inequalities first give polynomial growth and then, for Hermitian quasi-primary fields of weight $d\geq2$, the energy order $d-1$ needed for locality. A sharper normal-product count and a M\"obius estimate supply the second contraction. \item \emph{Integrating modules with given positive forms.} In Section~\ref{sec:annuli}, a fixed doubled annulus bounds local insertions by the norm of the inserted state. - Tener's Propositions~3.10--3.11 \cite{TenerFusion} give two inputs we will need before general fusion positivity. First, creation satisfies the vacuum-source transport identity. Second, for unitary charge, source and target, an intertwining operator applied to finite-energy vectors has Hilbert-space values on $0<|z|<1$, holomorphic on each branch. The latter conclusion uses regularity and convergence of products with the adjoint intertwiner. It does not require an energy bound or a net representation on the source. We will distinguish this Hilbert-valued convergence from the operator bounds that we prove later.
