- The geometric reduction approach seeks to contract subvarieties for which the fundamental group of the normalization has finite image in the ambient fundamental group. Koll\'ar's rational Shafarevich maps and Campana's meromorphic $\Gamma$-reduction address this problem through sufficiently general points \citep[\S1]{Kollar1993} \citep[Theorem~3.5 and Definition~3.8]{Campana1994}.
