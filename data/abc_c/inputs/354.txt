=== MANUSCRIPT: The Isoperimetric Conjecture for the Cubic Flat Three-Torus [preprints/The-Isoperimetric-Conjecture-for-the-Cubic-Flat-Three-Torus-September-24-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the isoperimetric conjecture for the cubic flat three-torus and classify all minimizers, including the equality cases at the transition volumes $`4\pi/81`$ and $`1/\pi`$. The minimizing regions are balls, circular tubes about shortest closed geodesics, coordinate slabs, and their complements.
[theorem] \label{intro:main} For \(V\in(0,1)\), put \(v=\min(V,1-V)\). Among finite-perimeter subsets of \(\mathbb R^3/\mathbb Z^3\) of volume \(V\), the minimum perimeter is \begin{equation} I_{\rm unit}(V)= \min\left\{(36\pi)^{1/3}v^{2/3},\ 2\sqrt{\pi v},\ 2\right\}. \label{intro:profile} \end{equation} For \(0<V\le1/2\), every minimizing region is, up to an ambient isometry and a null set, one of the following: \[ \begin{array}{c|c} \text{volume range}&\text{region}\\ \hline 0<V\le 4\pi/81 & \text{a ball of radius }(3V/(4\pi))^{1/3}\\[2pt] 4\pi/81\le V\le1/\pi &\text{a solid circular tube of radius }\sqrt{V/\pi}\\ &\text{about a shortest closed geodesic}\\[2pt] 1/\pi\le V\le1/2 &\text{a slab of width }V\text{ between coordinate tori}. \end{array} \] Both listed types occur at each common endpoint […]
[theorem] [Classical flat-manifold structure]\label{pre:structure} Let \(E\) be an isoperimetric region in a complete orientable flat three-manifold, with closed boundary \(\Sigma\). Either \(\Sigma\) is a union of parallel planar surfaces, or \(\Sigma\) is connected. In the connected case: \begin{enumerate} \renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\textup{(\roman{enumi})}} \item genus zero gives a round sphere; \item genus one gives a flat quotient of a plane or a circular cylinder; \item the genus is at most four; \item in genus two or three, \(H^2|\Sigma|\le 2\pi\), where \(H=h/2\); in genus four, \(H=0\). \end{enumerate}
[proposition] [Two-volume reduction]\label{pre:reduction} If every minimizer at \(V_1\) and \(V_2\) is standard, then \(I=F\) on \([0,1]\), and every minimizer is one of the standard regions of Lemma~\ref{pre:standard}.
