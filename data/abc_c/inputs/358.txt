FAMILY 358: A three-manifold without conjugate points or nonpositive curvature
SUBJECT: Differential geometry
OPENAI SUMMARY: Constructs a closed connected orientable smooth three-manifold that admits a metric without conjugate points but no metric of nonpositive sectional curvature. This answers negatively, already in dimension three, whether the first metric-existence property implies the second.
HEADLINE (v2, semi-formal): ∃ closed connected orientable smooth 3-manifold M: (∃ C^∞ metric on M without conjugate points) ∧ ¬(∃ C^∞ metric on M with sec ≤ 0)

=== MANUSCRIPT: A Three-Manifold Without Conjugate Points and Without a Nonpositively Curved Metric [preprints/A-Three-Manifold-Without-Conjugate-Points-and-Without-a-Nonpositively-Curved-Metric-September-24-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We construct a closed connected orientable smooth three-manifold that admits a smooth Riemannian metric without conjugate points but admits no smooth Riemannian metric of nonpositive sectional curvature.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exists a closed connected orientable smooth three-manifold $M$ with a $C^\infty$ Riemannian metric without conjugate points, such that $M$ admits no $C^\infty$ Riemannian metric whose sectional curvature is everywhere at most zero.
[theorem] \label{thm:no-cat0-action} The fundamental group $\Gamma=\pi_1(M)$ of the closed three-manifold defined by \eqref{eq:torus-gluing} admits no proper cocompact isometric action on a nonempty proper $\operatorname{CAT}(0)$ space.
[corollary] \label{cor:no-local-cat0} The manifold $M$ admits no locally $\operatorname{CAT}(0)$ length metric inducing its manifold topology.
