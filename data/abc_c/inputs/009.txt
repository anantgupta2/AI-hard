=== MANUSCRIPT: Reconstruction from Milnor K-theory modulo the characteristic [preprints/Reconstruction-from-Milnor-K-theory-modulo-the-characteristic-October-5-2026/build/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: Let K and L be finitely generated extensions of transcendence degree at least two over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p. We prove that every isomorphism of their first Milnor K-groups modulo p preserving the degree-two Steinberg relations is a nonzero scalar multiple of the map induced by a unique field isomorphism. The proof recovers projective lines over the subfields of pth powers by an elementary calculation with derivations.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be finitely generated extensions of algebraically closed fields of characteristic $p$, with \[ \trdeg(K/k)\ge2,\qquad \trdeg(L/l)\ge2. \] The canonical map \[ \Isom(K,k;L,l)\longrightarrow \Isom_{\mathrm M}(V_K,V_L)/\Lambda^\times, \qquad \alpha\longmapsto[\alpha_*], \] is bijective.
[proposition] [Intersection and exponent] \label{prop:intersection} Let $F$ be a field of characteristic $p$ with $[F:F^p]<\infty$, and put $C=F^p$. Let $X,Y\in F^\times$ be $p$-independent. Suppose \[ U\in C(X)^\times,\qquad V\in C(Y)^\times, \qquad [U]\notin\Fp[X],\qquad [V]\notin\Fp[Y]. \] If \begin{equation} \label{eq:intersection-witness} X\,C(Y/X)^\times\ \cap\ U\,C(V/U)^\times\ne\varnothing, \end{equation} then there is a unique integer $n$, $1\le n<p$, such that \begin{equation} \label{eq:intersection-conclusion} U^n\in C+CX^n,\qquad V^n\in C+CY^n. \end{equation} In fact, either inclusion in \eqref{eq:intersection-conclusion} determines $n$ uniquely. In each inclusion, both coefficients are nonzero.
[proposition] \label{prop:lines} Let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be finitely generated extensions of algebraically closed fields of characteristic $p$, both of transcendence degree at least two. For every compatible isomorphism $\Theta:V_K\to V_L$, there is a unique $n\in\Fp^\times$ such that $\Psi=n\Theta$ sends every projective line over $K^p$ onto a projective line over $L^p$.

ABSTRACT: We prove the Bogomolov–Pop reconstruction conjecture for function fields of transcendence degree at least two over arbitrary algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from ℓ. The pro-ℓ abelian-by-central datum determines the perfect closure and its constant field, with precisely the Frobenius and ℓ-adic unit ambiguities.
[theorem] [Bogomolov--Pop reconstruction]\label{thm:main} Let $k$ and $l$ be arbitrary algebraically closed fields of characteristics different from $\ell$, and let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be function fields with $\trdeg(K/k),\trdeg(L/l)\ge2$. Then \[ \Phi_{K,L}:\Isom_F^i(K,L)\longrightarrow \Isom^c(\Pi_L^a,\Pi_K^a)/\Zell^\times, \qquad [\alpha]\longmapsto[\alpha^*], \] is a bijection.
[proposition] \label{local:transport} Let $K/k$ and $L/l$ be function fields over algebraically closed fields of characteristic different from~$\ell$, each of transcendence degree at least two. Let $\varphi:W_L\to W_K$ be a continuous bracket-compatible $\Zell$-module isomorphism. Then their transcendence degrees agree, say they are~$d$. For every $1\leq r<d$, the map $\varphi$ carries exactly the pairs $I_w\subset D_w$ of quasi-prime $r$-divisors of $L/l$ onto the corresponding pairs of $K/k$. For matched pairs it induces a continuous isomorphism \[ D_w/I_w\cong W_{Lw}\ \longrightarrow\ D_v/I_v\cong W_{Kv} \] that preserves alternating pairs.
[proposition] \label{local:curves} In the setting of Proposition~\ref{local:transport}, let $w$ and $v$ be matched quasi-prime $(d-1)$-divisors. Then $lw$ is algebraic over a finite field if and only if $kv$ is. When this holds, the induced isomorphism $W_{Lw}\to W_{Kv}$ carries the full family of point inertia subgroups of the respective smooth projective curves onto each other. Corresponding normalized order characters differ by units of~$\Zell$.

- Birational anabelian geometry asks how much of a field can be read from its Galois groups. Over algebraically closed constants, the arithmetic Galois action disappears. Bogomolov's program proposes that a particularly small quotient still remembers every function field of dimension at least two: the quotient in which commutators are central. We prove the Isom form of this assertion, including surfaces and the prime $2$.
- \subsection{Previous work and the proof} Bogomolov introduced the abelian-by-central approach to birational reconstruction \cite{Bogomolov1991}. The local approach detects valuations through commuting elements and their associated multiplicative characters \cite{BT2002,TopazCL2017}. Bogomolov and Tschinkel proved the surface case over algebraic closures of finite fields \cite{BT2008}, followed by higher-dimensional reconstruction \cite{BT2011}; Pop established a corresponding birational reconstruction theorem \cite{PopBI2012}. The precise Isom formulation, including its scalar and Frobenius ambiguities, is recorded in \cite[Introduction, Conjecture~1]{TopazRat2016}.
