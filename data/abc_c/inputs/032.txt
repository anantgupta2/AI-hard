=== MANUSCRIPT: The rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties [preprints/The-rational-Hodge-conjecture-for-CM-abelian-varieties-October-6-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for complex abelian varieties with complex multiplication: every rational Hodge class on such a variety is a rational linear combination of algebraic cycle classes. As consequences, we obtain the generalized Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties, the Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields, and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in arbitrary characteristic.
[theorem] \label{thm:cm} Let \(A\) be a complex CM abelian variety. For every integer \(p\geq0\), the Betti cycle-class map \[ \operatorname{cl}_B:\CH^p(A)_{\Q} \longrightarrow H^{2p}(A,\Q)\cap H^{p,p}(A) \] is surjective, where \(\CH^p(A)_{\Q}=\CH^p(A)\otimes_{\Z}\Q\) and the intersection is taken in \(H^{2p}(A,\C)\). In particular, the rational Hodge conjecture holds in every codimension on every finite product of complex CM abelian varieties and on every power of such a product.
[theorem] [Four-factor switch]\label{thm:switch} Let \(E\) be a Galois CM field and let \(v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4:G_E\to\{1,-1\}\) be odd sign functions satisfying \(v_1+v_2=v_3+v_4\). Under the Künneth inclusion, the following line is algebraic: \begin{equation}\label{eq:switch-line} U(v_1)_1\otimes U(v_2)_1\otimes U(v_3)_c\otimes U(v_4)_c \ \subset\ H^4\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^4B(v_i),\Qbar\right) \end{equation}
[proposition] \label{prop:switch-reduction} If Theorem~\ref{thm:switch} holds for every Galois CM field and every quadruple of odd sign functions satisfying its relation, then Theorem~\ref{thm:cm} holds.

- \begin{abstract} We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for complex abelian varieties with complex multiplication: every rational Hodge class on such a variety is a rational linear combination of algebraic cycle classes. As consequences, we obtain the generalized Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties, the Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields, and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in arbitrary characteristic. \end{abstract}
- For a smooth projective complex variety \(X\), the rational Hodge conjecture asks whether every rational cohomology class of type \((p,p)\) is a rational linear combination of codimension-\(p\) algebraic cycle classes. We prove this assertion for abelian varieties with complex multiplication, in every dimension and codimension.
- The elementary relation needed in the proof is \[ v_1+v_2=v_3+v_4. \] It makes every scalar conjugate of the four-factor line \[ U(v_1)_1\otimes U(v_2)_1\otimes U(v_3)_c\otimes U(v_4)_c \ \subset H^4\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^4B(v_i),\Qbar\right) \] have type \((2,2)\). We prove that this line lies in the scalar extension of the rational cycle span. Section~\ref{sec:tensors} then assembles arbitrary balanced tensors from these relations: two-row switches connect lists with the same sum, and algebraic polarization pairings contract their intermediate factors. Transfer back to the original abelian variety and descent of coefficients give Theorem~\ref{thm:cm}.
- The Hecke projectors preserve the Hodge filtration, so weak admissibility turns these extreme slopes into pure Hodge types. Applying the calculation to every scalar conjugate identifies the complete CM type of each summand. Scalar descent then expresses every relevant theta class as a complex linear combination of images of the selected CM line under rational Hodge maps. Section~\ref{sec:assembly} puts the finite families on one projective surface, expands the nonzero period, and applies the surface criterion. This completes the four-factor construction and hence the proof of the main theorem.
- Hazama's coordinate-arrangement calculation rationally spans the kernel of its Hodge matrix by antipodal pairs and elementary four-label relations \cite[Theorem~7.13]{Hazama2003CM}. His Corollary~7.14.1 states a corresponding codimension-two algebraicity reduction. We prove the particular tensor reduction needed here directly, including the transfers to auxiliary abelian factors and the algebraic contractions that assemble the relations.
- The surface criterion of Proposition~\ref{prop:surface-switch} asks for a nonzero period made from four one-forms on one surface. We construct the analytic part of that input using theta series attached to auxiliary Hermitian spaces of dimensions one and two, called multiplicity spaces. For a multiplicity line, a component of the first Taylor tensor is a holomorphic one-form. Varying the finite theta input makes its values span a cotangent fiber, so two such constructions give a nonzero wedge. For their product input, the second Taylor tensor for the orthogonal sum of the two lines has a double-alternating projection that is a nonzero multiple of that wedge. This total two-form allows us to change the two multiplicity lines while keeping their sum and its finite theta input fixed.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Algebraic Kuga–Satake correspondences and Hodge conjectures on a K3 quadratic locus [preprints/Algebraic-Kuga-Satake-correspondences-and-Hodge-conjectures-on-a-K3-quadratic-locus-September-30-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the rational Hodge and generalized Hodge conjectures in every cohomological degree of every self-power of a projective complex K3 surface whose transcendental quadratic space, with its cup-product form, admits a rational isometric embedding in $`\mathbb U_{\mathbb Q}^{\oplus2}\perp\langle-1\rangle^4`$. This includes every ample P-polarized K3 surface, including all Picard jumps, for $`P=\mathbb U\oplus D_8(-1)\oplus D_4(-1)`$. On this locus we construct algebraic correspondences inducing every prescribed standard even-Clifford Kuga–Satake tensor. More generally, for any projective complex K3 surface, algebraicity of one exact standard even-Clifford Kuga–Satake tensor implies both conjectures for every self-power.
[theorem] \label{ext:criterion} Let $S$ be a projective K3 surface. Suppose that, for one rational nonisotropic $w\in T_S$, one rational polarization, and one full even-Clifford realization, the tensor of $j_w(v)(a)=vaw$ is induced by a cycle in $\CH^2(S\times A^2)_{\Q}$. Then rational HC and rational GHC hold in every degree of every self-power of $S$.
[theorem] \label{main:theorem} Let $S$ be a smooth projective complex K3 surface. Suppose that there is a rational isometric embedding \[ (T_S,q)\hookrightarrow V_P. \] For any full even-Clifford realization $H^1(A,\Q)=C^+(T_S,q)$, any rational nonisotropic $w\in T_S$, and any rational polarization $E$ of this weight-one Hodge structure, there is a cycle \[ \Gamma_S\in\CH^2(S\times A\times A)_{\Q} \] whose action on every $x\in T_S$ is exactly $\iota_{w,E}(x)$ in $H^2(A\times A,\Q)$. The same assertion holds on a transported isogenous model with its specified tensor. Moreover, the rational Hodge conjecture and the rational generalized Hodge conjecture hold in every cohomological degree of every self-power $S^m$, for every integer $m\ge1$.

- We prove Theorem~\ref{ext:criterion}. The argument is independent of the concrete K3 family constructed later; its only Clifford premise is one algebraic realization of the exact standard tensor in that theorem.
- \begin{proof} Choose an ample divisor class $\ell$ on $A$, and use the polarization \[ e(x,y)=\int_Ax\cup y\cup\ell^{g-1},\qquad g=\dim A. \] Changing the rational polarization in the hypothesis preserves algebraicity: the change on the identified dual factor is a rational Hodge automorphism of $H^1(A,\Q)$, and hence comes from an abelian homomorphism with rational coefficients.
