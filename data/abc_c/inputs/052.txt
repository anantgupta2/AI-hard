=== MANUSCRIPT: Universal-cover splitting for compact Kähler manifolds [preprints/Universal-cover-splitting-for-compact-Kahler-manifolds-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the two-summand form of Beauville's compatible splitting conjecture. If the tangent bundle of a compact connected Kähler manifold decomposes into two integrable holomorphic subbundles of positive rank, then its ordinary universal cover admits a product decomposition whose factor tangent bundles are the lifted specified summands.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $X$ be a compact connected K\"ahler manifold of complex dimension $n\geq2$, and let \[ T_X=E_1\oplus E_2 \] be a specified decomposition into integrable holomorphic subbundles of positive ranks $r_1,r_2$. If $\pi:\widetilde X\to X$ is the ordinary universal covering map, there are connected simply connected complex manifolds $Y_1,Y_2$ and a biholomorphism $\Phi:\widetilde X\to Y_1\times Y_2$ such that \[ \dd\Phi(\pi^*E_i)=\pr_i^*T_{Y_i}\qquad(i=1,2), \] where $\dd\pi$ identifies $T_{\widetilde X}$ with $\pi^*T_X$. In particular, $\dim_\C Y_i=r_i$.
[corollary] [Non-uniruled projective manifolds]\label{cor:non-uniruled} Let $X$ be a smooth connected non-uniruled projective complex manifold, and let $T_X=E_1\oplus E_2$ be a specified holomorphic splitting into subbundles of positive ranks. Then both summands are integrable and the ordinary universal cover has a compatible product decomposition as in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
[corollary] \label{thm:nef-big} Let $X$ be a smooth connected projective complex manifold of dimension $n\geq2$ with nef and big canonical bundle $K_X$. Every specified holomorphic splitting $T_X=E_1\oplus E_2$ into positive-rank subbundles is integrable and induces a compatible product decomposition of the ordinary universal cover as in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

- In higher rank, Pereira and Touzet obtain a compatible Euclidean factor when one involutive direct summand is Hermitian flat \cite[Theorem~B]{PT13}. Druel, Pereira, Pym, and Touzet show that a regular foliation with numerically flat tangent bundle---meaning that the bundle and its dual are nef---is Hermitian flat and admits an integrable complement, again producing a compatible Euclidean factor \cite[Theorem~1.1]{DPPT24}. A different continuation mechanism applies when a foliation has a compact leaf. Druel, Pereira, Pym, and Touzet prove compatible universal-cover splitting when one foliation has a compact leaf with finite holonomy \cite[Theorem~1.4]{DPPT22}. Their argument uses global Reeb stability and a flat Ehresmann connection. Our proof replaces these additional flatness or leaf hypotheses by extension estimates for maps adapted to the two distributions.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Integrability of split tangent bundles on rationally connected manifolds [preprints/Integrability-of-split-tangent-bundles-on-rationally-connected-manifolds-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that both summands of every specified holomorphic splitting of the tangent bundle of a smooth rationally connected projective complex manifold into two positive-rank subbundles are integrable. This proves Höring's conjecture on rationally connected projective manifolds. Höring's product theorem then gives a product decomposition compatible with the specified splitting.
[theorem] [Automatic integrability]\label[theorem]{thm:main} Let \(X\) be a smooth connected projective complex manifold of dimension at least two. Suppose that two general points of \(X\) lie on the image of a morphism \(\PP^1\to X\). For every specified holomorphic decomposition \[ T_X=E_1\oplus E_2 \] into subbundles of positive rank, both \(E_1\) and \(E_2\) are integrable.
[corollary] [Compatible product]\label[corollary]{cor:product} Under the hypotheses of \cref{thm:main}, there are smooth projective manifolds \(X_1,X_2\) and an isomorphism \(X\simeq X_1\times X_2\) under which \[ E_i=\pr_i^*T_{X_i}\qquad (i=1,2). \]
[proposition] [Tangent box]\label[proposition]{prop:box} Every holomorphic map \(g:C\to X\) admits a holomorphic map \(F:S\to X\) such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:box} F|_\Delta=g,\qquad dF(T^{(1)})\subset F^*E_1,\qquad dF(T^{(2)})\subset F^*E_2. \end{equation} Neither summand is assumed integrable.
PROOF STRATEGY (paper's own words):
- \begin{abstract} We prove that both summands of every specified holomorphic splitting of the tangent bundle of a smooth rationally connected projective complex manifold into two positive-rank subbundles are integrable. This proves H\"oring's conjecture on rationally connected projective manifolds. H\"oring's product theorem then gives a product decomposition compatible with the specified splitting. \end{abstract}
- We prove that rational connectedness forces this local condition. Here and below, ``two general points'' means all pairs in some nonempty Zariski open subset of \(X\times X\). A smooth connected projective complex manifold is rationally connected if two general points lie on the image of a morphism \(\PP^1\to X\).
- Beauville studied how a tangent-bundle splitting on a compact K\"ahler manifold can lead to a product of its universal cover \cite{Bea00}. An abstract product need not make a given summand integrable: H\"oring's \cite[Example~2.14]{Hor07} gives a nonintegrable summand on a product of an abelian surface with \(\PP^1\). Rational connectedness rules out that example and supplies the abundance of rational curves used in our proof.
- \subsection*{Proof strategy}
- The construction that drives the proof does not require rational connectedness. Given any holomorphic map \(g:\PP^1\to X\) into a smooth projective manifold with a split tangent bundle, we construct \[ F:\PP^1\times\PP^1\longrightarrow X,\qquad F(s,s)=g(s), \] with the first factor direction tangent to \(E_1\) and the second tangent to \(E_2\). We call \(F\) a \emph{tangent box}. The name records these tangencies; the map may have critical points and need not give local coordinates.
