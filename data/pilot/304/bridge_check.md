# Family 304: strict bridge-novelty check

**Bridge:** sheaf Witt groups and L-theory used to prove Hilbert–Smith, via p^k equal integral parts in a lattice fixed before k. **Sub-import:** finite-group isotypic signature divisibility applied to Z_p, with |G| = p^k unbounded.

**Verdict:** novel, nothing close found. Confidence 0.6. Date 2026-10-15. Machine-readable version: `bridge_check.json`.

## What the paper says about its own antecedents
- Signature antecedents: Verdier, Balmer(–Walter), Woolf, Ranicki–Weiss, Hornbostel–Schlichting. None concern group actions.
- It contrasts its route with Yang/BRW, the regularity line, Pardon and Shelukhin, and cites no earlier signature approach to Hilbert–Smith.

## What is new
The first use of signature/Witt integrality against Hilbert–Smith: a lattice fixed before k, with p^k equal parts from germ-localized character projectors over a test target rather than the orbit space. The finite-group count is classical.

## Reading the verdict strictly
- **Main bridge:** nothing close in the arXiv corpus, Dranishnikov's survey, 304's bibliography or about 30 queries.
- **Sub-import:** if the classical finite-group template counts as "close", it becomes **close precedent exists**. Nothing found applies it to Z_p; 304's test-sphere device gets around non-manifold M/p^kZ_p.
