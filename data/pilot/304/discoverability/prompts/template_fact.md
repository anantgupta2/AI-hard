Additional established fact you may use (finite groups): if a finite group G acts freely and orientation-preservingly on a closed oriented 4m-manifold, the intersection form on H^{2m}(M; C) splits into isotypic parts indexed by the irreducible characters of G; for a free action these parts have equal signatures (up to the factor dim of the representation), so |G| divides the signature of M (Atiyah–Singer G-signature theorem; Gilmer 1981 gives a topological proof).
