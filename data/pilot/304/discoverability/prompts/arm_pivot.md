Problem. Let p be a prime and let Z_p be the additive group of p-adic integers. Show that no continuous action of Z_p on a connected (Hausdorff, second-countable, boundaryless) topological n-manifold M is faithful.

Background you may use:
- The classical reduction: by Gleason–Montgomery–Zippin–Yamabe, the Hilbert–Smith conjecture (every locally compact group acting faithfully on a connected manifold is a Lie group) is equivalent to the statement above.
- Newman's theorem (1931): for a connected manifold M and a point x, there is a neighbourhood U of x such that every nontrivial finite cyclic group acting effectively on M has an orbit not contained in U. Consequently any faithful Z_p action has, for each k, nontrivial "small" subgroups p^k Z_p whose orbits are still not uniformly small.
- Yang (1960) and Bredon–Raymond–Williams (1961): if Z_p acts effectively on an n-manifold M, the orbit space M/Z_p has integral cohomological dimension n + 2 (so it is far from a manifold); the quotient maps M/p^{k+1}Z_p -> M/p^k Z_p have deck group Z/p.
- Standard library: Haar measure on compact groups; the structure of Z_p (open subgroups p^k Z_p, quotients Z/p^k, characters); topology of manifolds; sheaf theory, derived categories and Verdier duality; algebraic topology at the level of Hatcher; semialgebraic geometry.

Additional established tools you may use (proved, with these exact statements). Throughout, after stabilizing, Z_p acts on W = O x R^{d-n} for an open G-invariant set O in M, with d odd, d > n and trivial action on the extra factor; G denotes Z_p, Y = W/G and q: W -> Y is the quotient map.
1. Test categories. For a locally compact Hausdorff space X, T^0(X) is the smallest full replete triangulated subcategory of D^b(Sh_R(X)) containing Rf_* R_B for every continuous map f from a compact finite polyhedron B to X; T(X) is its closure under direct summands. These categories are stable under Verdier duality, so they have Witt groups; write E_d(X) for the d-shifted Witt group W_d(T(X)). E_d is covariant for proper maps and homotopy invariant.
2. Fixed sphere lattice. For odd d >= 3 there is L_d = 2^{e_d} such that E_d(S^d) tensor Q = Q u_d and the image of E_d(S^d) lies in L_d^{-1} Z u_d (a lattice fixed independently of everything else).
3. Contractible quotient. For d odd and U a nonempty contractible open subset of X, E_d(X) -> W_d(T(X)/T(U)) is an isomorphism over Z.
4. Averaged degree-one test. After shrinking G to an open subgroup H that moves points less than a pinch scale, the Haar average of a ball pinch map, normalized, descends to a map a: Y -> S^d such that a composed with q (restricted to a chart) has degree 1.
5. Finite sheets. For every k there is a nonempty open V in Y over which W/p^k G -> W/G is m = p^k disjoint sheets, with a locally constant multiplier beta: q^{-1}V -> mu_m satisfying beta(g w) = zeta^{g} beta(w).
6. Moving the test. For any nonempty open V in Y minus the basepoint, some positive multiple of a is homotopic to a map h that is constant on Y \ V.

Task. Propose the single most useful new invariant, lemma or construction for proving the statement, stated precisely, with a proof sketch showing how it yields a contradiction with faithfulness. Be concrete; at most 700 words.
