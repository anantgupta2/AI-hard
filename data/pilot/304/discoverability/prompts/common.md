Problem. Let p be a prime and let Z_p be the additive group of p-adic integers. Show that no continuous action of Z_p on a connected (Hausdorff, second-countable, boundaryless) topological n-manifold M is faithful.

Background you may use:
- The classical reduction: by Gleason–Montgomery–Zippin–Yamabe, the Hilbert–Smith conjecture (every locally compact group acting faithfully on a connected manifold is a Lie group) is equivalent to the statement above.
- Newman's theorem (1931): for a connected manifold M and a point x, there is a neighbourhood U of x such that every nontrivial finite cyclic group acting effectively on M has an orbit not contained in U. Consequently any faithful Z_p action has, for each k, nontrivial "small" subgroups p^k Z_p whose orbits are still not uniformly small.
- Yang (1960) and Bredon–Raymond–Williams (1961): if Z_p acts effectively on an n-manifold M, the orbit space M/Z_p has integral cohomological dimension n + 2 (so it is far from a manifold); the quotient maps M/p^{k+1}Z_p -> M/p^k Z_p have deck group Z/p.
- Standard library: Haar measure on compact groups; the structure of Z_p (open subgroups p^k Z_p, quotients Z/p^k, characters); topology of manifolds; sheaf theory, derived categories and Verdier duality; algebraic topology at the level of Hatcher; semialgebraic geometry.
