FAMILY 184: Correspondence coloring with a fixed forbidden subgraph
OPENAI SUMMARY: Proves the Alon–Krivelevich–Sudakov coloring conjecture in correspondence-coloring form: graphs avoiding any fixed subgraph F need $`O_F(\Delta/\log\Delta)`$ colors when their maximum degree Δ is sufficiently large. Also proves the Ajtai–Erdős–Komlós–Szemerédi independence conjecture: for fixed r ≥ 4, every n-vertex Kr-free graph of average degree d ≥ 2 has an independent set of size $`\Omega_r(n\log d/d)`$.

## Scope

The formalized result gives a logarithmic improvement in the independence number of clique-free graphs. For every fixed integer $r\ge4$, there is $c_r>0$ such that every finite $K_r$-free simple graph on $n$ vertices with average degree $d\ge2$ has an independent set of size at least $c_r n\log d/d$. The constant depends only on $r$.
