FAMILY 266: Exactly three mutually unbiased bases in dimension six
SUBJECT: Mathematical physics
SUMMARY: Proves $`N(6)=3`$, resolving Zauner's dimension-six mutually unbiased bases conjecture: three such bases exist in ℂ6, but four cannot. The exclusion is a complete certified computation under the stated binary64 arithmetic and compiler conditions. An independent companion proves the Matolcsi–Ruzsa–Weiner Fourier-vanishing conjecture for order-six complex Hadamard matrices outside Tao's cubic equivalence class.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: The maximum number of mutually unbiased bases in dimension six (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the maximum number of mutually unbiased orthonormal bases in ℂ6 is three, resolving Zauner's dimension-six MUB conjecture. The upper bound is computer-assisted: under the stated binary64 arithmetic and compiler conditions, a complete execution of the documented verification pipeline excludes four arbitrary complex bases.
[theorem] [Computer-assisted]\label{thm:main} Under the arithmetic and compiler conditions in Appendix~\ref{sec:rounding}, the fresh complete execution documented in Section~\ref{execute:observed} certifies that there are no four pairwise mutually unbiased orthonormal bases in $\C^6$. Consequently, $N(6)=3$.
[proposition] [Exhaustive seed cover] \label{seed:cover} For a completed execution of the specified \texttt{covout} traversal under the arithmetic conditions of Section~\ref{sec:rounding}, the written rational midpoint centers, each with its recorded selected position, cover the seed coordinates of every exact complex Hadamard matrix up to the equivalences of Lemma~\ref{seed:equivalences}, by closed Euclidean balls of radius $0.032$. After the indicated column ordering and conversion of the centers to binary phase values, radius $0.0320001$ is sufficient.
[proposition] [Completion coverage conditional on successful termination] \label{completion:recursion} Fix a seed ball for which the frame and bound constructions are certified. Suppose that every final unitary cell is either excluded by a certified necessary-condition test or covered by a certified phase ball as in Section~\ref{sec:phase-cover}. Then those phase balls cover all feasible Hadamard completions of the seed ball, with the equivalences specified by the phase-ball certificates. The Stage A recursion establishes precisely this conclusion if it terminates with zero unresolved cells.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Exact Fourier certificates for complex Hadamard matrices of order six (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the Fourier-vanishing conjecture of Matolcsi, Ruzsa, and Weiner: every complex Hadamard matrix of order six outside Tao's cubic equivalence class has a vanishing character sum at every permutation of $`(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1)`$. We also give an exact certificate excluding seven mutually unbiased bases in ℂ6; by Weiner's completion theorem, this yields an upper bound of five. Both certificates use only integer and rational arithmetic, and the complete verifier is included.
[theorem] [Fourier vanishing]\label{thm:fourier} Let $H$ be a complex Hadamard matrix of order six. If $H$ is not equivalent to the cubic matrix $T$ in \eqref{eq:cubic-matrix}, then \[ g_H(\pi\alpha)=0\qquad\text{for every coordinate permutation }\pi. \]
[theorem] [No complete family]\label{thm:main} There do not exist seven orthonormal bases $B_r=(b_{r,0},\ldots,b_{r,5})$ of $\C^6$, $1\le r\le7$, such that \[ \bigl|\langle b_{r,i},b_{s,j}\rangle\bigr|^2=\frac16 \qquad(r\ne s,\ 0\le i,j\le5). \]
[proposition] [Single-matrix certificate] \label{prop:pair-certificate} For every $6\times6$ Hadamard matrix $H$ and for each $q\in\{q_{\mathrm{cross}},q_{\mathrm{same}}\}$, \begin{equation} 3\bigl\langle |L_{\widetilde H}|^2\bigr\rangle_H +\left\langle |g_{\widetilde H}(\alpha)g_{\widetilde H}(q)|^2 \right\rangle_H=0. \label{eq:pair-certificate} \end{equation}
