FAMILY 062: Projective contact classification and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture
SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
SUMMARY: Proves the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture: every closed connected positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension at least eight is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space. It also proves contact-Fano homogeneity and classifies smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds of complex dimension at least three: those with $`b_2=1`$ are adjoint varieties with their canonical contact structures, while those with $`b_2\ge2`$ have underlying manifold $`\mathbb P(T^*Z)`$ for a smooth projective variety Z.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: Contact Fano manifolds and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We resolve the contact-Fano homogeneity conjecture and the Riemannian LeBrun–Salamon conjecture positively. Every smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of complex dimension at least three, with its given contact distribution, is contact-isomorphic to the adjoint variety of a simple complex Lie algebra. Consequently, every closed connected smooth positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension $`4m\geq8`$ is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $(X,F)$ be a smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of dimension $2n+1$, with $n\geq1$, and let $L=T_X/F$. There exist a finite-dimensional simple complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ and an algebraic isomorphism \[ \varphi:X\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Y_{\mathfrak g} \] whose differential carries the given distribution $F$ to the canonical contact distribution on $Y_{\mathfrak g}$. Moreover, \[ L\simeq\varphi^*\bigl(\OO_{\PP(\mathfrak g)}(1)|_{Y_{\mathfrak g}}\bigr). \]
[proposition] \label{red:alternatives} Either $\operatorname{Pic}(X)=\ZZ[L]$, or $(X,F)$ is contact-isomorphic to one of the following standard contact manifolds: \begin{enumerate} \item the incidence variety \[ I=\{(\ell,H)\in\PP^{n+1}\times(\PP^{n+1})^*: \ell\subset H\}, \qquad L=\OO_I(1,1); \] \item projective space $\PP^{2n+1}$ with the contact structure induced by a nondegenerate alternating form on $\CC^{2n+2}$, and $L=\OO_{\PP^{2n+1}}(2)$. \end{enumerate} In both listed cases a connected algebraic group acts transitively and preserves the given distribution $F$.
[proposition] [Suitable contact-line pair]\label{lines:suitable-pair} There exist a point $x\in X$ and free parametrized contact lines $f_1,f_2:\PP^1\to X$, with $f_1(0)=f_2(0)=x$, such that, for affine parameters at $0$ and $a=f'_1(0)$, $b=f'_2(0)$, \begin{equation}\label{lines:pair} d\alpha_x(a,b)\neq0,\qquad P_{f_1}|_0+P_{f_2}|_0=F_x. \end{equation} In particular the two positive subspaces are complementary Lagrangian subspaces of $F_x$.
