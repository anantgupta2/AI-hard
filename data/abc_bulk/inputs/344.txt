--- MANUSCRIPT: The metric Blaschke theorem (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the metric Blaschke conjecture: every connected closed smooth Riemannian manifold of positive dimension whose global injectivity radius equals its diameter is, up to scale, a standard compact rank-one symmetric space.
[theorem] [The metric Blaschke theorem]\label{thm:blaschke} Let $(M,g)$ be a connected closed smooth Riemannian manifold of positive dimension. If $\inj(M,g)=\diam(M,g)$, then $(M,g)$ is isometric, up to multiplication of the metric by a positive constant, to a round sphere, a standard real, complex or quaternionic projective space, or the Cayley plane.
[theorem] [Sharp volume comparison]\label{thm:comparison} Every Blaschke manifold and its model at the same diameter satisfy \[ \Vol(M,g)\ge\Vol(M_0,g_0). \] Equality holds if and only if $(M,g)$ is isometric to $(M_0,g_0)$.
[proposition] [Classical Blaschke structure] \label{geom:structure} Every unit-speed geodesic is simply closed with period $2D$. For each $p\in M$, the map $v\mapsto\exp_p(Dv)$ from $S_pM$ onto the cut locus is a smooth sphere fibration whose fibers are great spheres of one dimension $k$, independent of $p$. Its differential has nullity $k$. The model is determined as follows: \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item If $k=n-1$, the model is a sphere; if $k=0$, it is a real projective space. In these two cases the metric classification is known: the manifold is isometric to its equally scaled model. \item If $0<k<n-1$, the manifold is simply connected and its integral cohomology ring has one generator of degree $k+1$. The possibilities are complex-projective type, quaternionic-projective type, and Cayley-plane type, with the dimensions listed below. \end{enumerate}
