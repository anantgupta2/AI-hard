--- MANUSCRIPT: Modularity of elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields (October-4-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the modularity conjecture for elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields: every such curve is modular, with matching local parameters at every place.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic number field and $E/K$ an elliptic curve. There is an isobaric automorphic representation $\pi_E$ of $\GL_2(\A_K)$ with trivial central character and weight zero such that, for every prime $l$ and every isomorphism $\iota:\overline\Q_l\xrightarrow{\sim}\C$, \[ r_\iota(\pi_E)\simeq H^1_{\et}(E_{\overline K},\overline\Q_l). \] At every finite place $v\nmid l$ one has \[ \iota\WD\bigl(H^1_{\et}(E_{\overline K},\overline\Q_l) |_{G_{K_v}}\bigr)^{\Fss} \simeq \rec_{K_v}\bigl(\pi_{E,v}\otimes|\det|_v^{-1/2}\bigr). \] The unitary parameter at the complex place is $z\mapsto\diag(z/|z|,\overline z/|z|)$. In particular every local factor agrees, and \[ L(E/K,s)=L(\pi_E,s-1/2). \] If $E$ has no complex multiplication, $\pi_E$ is cuspidal. [proposition] \label{geo:compact-type} Suppose that $k$ contains $\mu_m$. At $R=O$, the special fiber of the model in Lemma~\ref{geo:semistable-models} is a rational curve joined at distinct points to $m$ copies of $E$, all defined over $k$. Rotations cyclically permute the elliptic components. The Jacobian family of $C_{m,R}$ therefore extends to an abelian scheme on a Zariski neighborhood of $O$ in the parameter curve, with fiber $E^m$ at $O$.
[proposition] \label{mon:integral-module} The cycles $a_i,b_i$ form an integral symplectic basis of $H_1(C_{m,R_s},\Z)$. Consequently, for every smooth fiber of the family, \begin{equation}\label{mon:free-lattices} H_1(C_m,\Z)\simeq\Z[\Gamma_m]^2, \qquad H^1(C_m,\Z)\simeq\Z[\Gamma_m]^2. \end{equation} In particular, after comparison, $H^1_{\mathrm{et}}(C_m,\Z_\ell)$ is free of rank two over $\Z_\ell[\Gamma_m]$ for every prime $\ell$, including $\ell\mid m$.
