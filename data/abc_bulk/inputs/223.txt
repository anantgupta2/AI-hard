FAMILY 223: Random-cluster interfaces: critical, disordered, thermal, and natural-time scaling
SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
SUMMARY: Proves chordal SLEκ limits for critical square-lattice random-cluster interfaces for $`0\lt q\le4`$, with $`\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)`$: bounded Jordan domains are allowed for q ≥ 1, and smooth Jordan domains for q &lt; 1, under the stated marked-boundary approximations. For $`1\le q\le4`$, complete nested plane loops converge to CLEκ.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: Square-lattice FK interfaces and nested loops for 1 <= q < 4 (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: For every fixed $`1\le q\lt 4`$, critical square-lattice random-cluster Dobrushin interfaces converge as ordered curves to chordal $`\mathop{\mathrm{SLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$, where $`\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)`$, confirming the Rohde–Schramm prediction in this parameter range. This holds in every bounded Jordan domain under uniform marked boundary approximation. The complete nested plane loop collections converge to whole-plane $`\mathop{\mathrm{CLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}`$ in a spherical matching topology retaining multiplicities and traversals. At q = 1 we obtain Cardy's formula for square-lattice bond percolation with free boundary edges.
[theorem] [Interfaces and all nested loops] \label{intro:main} For every fixed real $1\le q<4$, the following assertions hold. \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item For every bounded Jordan domain $D$ with distinct boundary marks $a,b$, and every approximation satisfying \eqref{intro:marked-jordan}, the Dobrushin strand $\eta_n$ converges in law, in \eqref{intro:curve-metric}, to chordal $\SLE_{\kappa(q)}$ from $a$ to $b$ in $D$. \item Under the specified free infinite-volume law, $\Gamma_\delta$ converges in law as $\delta\downarrow0$ to the nested whole-plane $\CLE_{\kappa(q)}$, in the spherical collection topology just defined. The limit is M\"obius invariant. \end{enumerate} Both conclusions hold along every mesh sequence. No uniformity in $q$ is asserted.
[theorem] [Cardy's formula for square-lattice bond percolation] \label{intro:cardy} Let $\psi:D\to\H$ send $a,c,d$ to $0,1,\infty$, and put $x=\psi(b)\in(0,1)$. Under the boundary and approximation conventions above, \begin{equation}\label{intro:cardy-formula} \lim_{n\to\infty}\mathbb P(C_n) =\frac{\displaystyle\int_0^x[t(1-t)]^{-2/3}\,\dd t} {\displaystyle\int_0^1[t(1-t)]^{-2/3}\,\dd t}. \end{equation}
[proposition] [Three logarithmic comparisons]\label{exp:comparisons} With the definitions above, \begin{align} 2P+2D_*+4(C_0+C_1)&\le o(\log n),\label{exp:rectangle-budget}\\ C_0+C_1+L_0+L_1&\le o(\log n),\label{exp:corner-budget}\\ P+\sum_j\log\frac{F_-(a_j)}{F_+(a_j)} &\le o(\log n),\label{exp:charge-budget} \end{align} where the finite list of fixed charges in the last inequality satisfies \eqref{exp:charge-range} and $\sum_j a_j=1$.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Self-dual random-cluster interfaces below one (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: For every fixed $`0\lt q\lt 1`$, we prove that the Dobrushin interface of the square-lattice random-cluster model at its self-dual parameter converges to chordal SLEκ, where $`\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)\in(6,8)`$. The approximating domains are simple closed nearest-neighbor lattice polygons with distinct marked vertices, whose marked boundary parametrizations converge uniformly to those of a bounded smooth Jordan domain. Convergence holds along the full mesh sequence in the uniform metric on oriented curves modulo increasing reparametrization. The proof combines finite connection comparisons below one, localization in irregular tiled disks, and a boundary observable that determines the limiting Loewner driver.
[theorem] [Below-one interface convergence]\label{thm:main} For every fixed $q\in(0,1)$ and every marked domain approximation just specified, the laws of $\eta_\delta$ converge as $\delta\to0$ in the metric \eqref{eq:curve-metric} to chordal $\SLE_\kappa(D;a,b)$, where \[ \kappa=\frac{4\pi}{\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)}. \] The convergence holds along the full mesh sequence.
[theorem] [Positive site pressure] \label{thm:pressure} There are coefficients $c_m\ge0$, indexed by the nonzero multiindices $m\in\Z_{\ge0}^{V}$, such that \begin{equation} -\log F_H(x)=\sum_{m\ne0}c_m x^m, \qquad x^m=\prod_{i\in V}x_i^{m_i}. \label{pressure:series} \end{equation} The series converges absolutely on a polydisk containing the closed unit polydisk in its interior. If $c_m>0$, the support $\{i:m_i>0\}$ is connected in $H$.
[proposition] [Rectangle RSW for separate electrodes] \label{prop:rectangle-rsw} For every fixed $\Lambda\ge1$ there is $c_\Lambda=c_\Lambda(q)>0$ such that \begin{equation} \inf_{\substack{n,w\in\Z_{\ge1}\\w\le\Lambda n}}g_x(w,n) \ge c_\Lambda. \label{rect:rsw} \end{equation} The same holds after translation, lattice rotation, reflection, or translation to the shifted dual square lattice. All probabilities refer to separate full crossing electrodes with no other wires.
