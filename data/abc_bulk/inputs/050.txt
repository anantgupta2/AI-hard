SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry

--- MANUSCRIPT: Ample rank-two bundles on the quadric surface without Griffiths-positive metrics (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We give counterexamples to Griffiths' conjecture in rank two on the quadric surface $`\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1`$. We construct an explicit bundle G whose coordinatewise power pullbacks, tensored with $`\mathcal O(1,1)`$, are ample for every positive power, but admit no smooth strictly Griffiths-positive Hermitian metric for all sufficiently large powers.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is an algebraic vector bundle $G$ of rank two on $S$ such that, for every positive integer $m$, the bundle \[ E_m=f_m^*G\otimes P \] is ample. There is an integer $m_0$ such that $E_m$ admits no smooth Hermitian metric with strictly Griffiths-positive Chern curvature for every $m\ge m_0$.
