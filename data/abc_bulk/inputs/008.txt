--- MANUSCRIPT: The Deligne-Drinfeld conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture: the rational Grothendieck–Teichmüller Lie algebra is freely generated by one element in every odd weight at least three, with the corresponding isomorphism after weight completion.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There are homogeneous elements \(\sigma_{2k+1}\in W_{2k+1}\), one for each \(k\ge1\), for which \[ \Lie_{\Q}\langle e_3,e_5,e_7,\ldots\rangle \longrightarrow (W,\{\, ,\,\}),\qquad e_{2k+1}\longmapsto\sigma_{2k+1}, \] is an isomorphism of graded Lie algebras. In particular, \(W\) is closed under the bracket \eqref{eq:ihara}. The induced map on completions by weight is a continuous graded isomorphism.
[proposition] [Saturated reduction]\label{prop:saturated-reduction} Let \[ K_n=\Lie_R\langle x,y\rangle_n, \qquad L_n=W_n\cap K_n. \] Then \(L_n\) is a saturated finite free \(R\)-submodule of \(K_n\), and its reduction embeds in \(K_n/2K_n\). Writing its image as \(\overline L_n\), one has \begin{equation}\label{eq:saturated-dimension} \dim_{\F}\overline L_n=\dim_{\Q}W_n. \end{equation} More generally, for any rational subspace \(V_n\subset W_n\), the lattice \(M_n=V_n\cap K_n\) has an embedded reduction \(\overline M_n\subset\overline L_n\) of dimension \(\dim_{\Q}V_n\). Every element of \(\overline L_n\) satisfies the reductions of the three defining identities. If \(n>2\), it also satisfies \eqref{eq:special} and \eqref{eq:special-linearization} after reduction, together with all formal coefficient consequences of these identities.
