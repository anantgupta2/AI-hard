FAMILY 310: Quillen's conjecture in rational homology
SUBJECT: Topology
SUMMARY: Proves the rational-homology form of Quillen's conjecture for every finite group and every prime. If the largest normal p-subgroup of G is trivial, the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian p-subgroups has nonzero augmented reduced rational homology and is therefore not contractible.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: Rational homology and Quillen's conjecture (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Quillen's conjecture for all finite groups and all primes. More precisely, if a finite group G has trivial largest normal p-subgroup $`O_p(G)`$, then the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian p-subgroups of G has nonzero augmented reduced rational homology. This establishes the stronger rational-homology form of the conjecture.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every finite group $G$ and every prime $p$, if $\Op(G)=1$, then \[ \widetilde H_*(\Ap(G);\Q)\ne0. \] Here reduced homology is augmented, so that the empty poset has $\widetilde H_{-1}(\varnothing;\Q)=\Q$.
[theorem] [Reduction at two]\label{thm:reduction-two} Let $G$ be a minimal-order counterexample to \eqref{eq:HQC} for $p=2$. Then $G$ has a simple component $L$ in the following list, where the field parameter $q$ has characteristic at least five: \[ \begin{array}{ll} \PSL_n(q),\ \PSU_n(q)& n\geq4,\\ \PSp_{2n}(q)& n\geq3,\\ \Omega_{2n+1}(q)& n\geq2,\\ \mathrm P\Omega_{2n}^{\pm}(q)& n\geq4. \end{array} \]
[theorem] [Odd-prime reduction]\label{thm:reduction-odd} Suppose that, for every odd prime $p$, every odd prime power $q$ with $p\mid q+1$, and every simple $L=\PSU_n(q)$ with $n\geq5$, each elementary $p$-extension of $L$ inside $\Aut(L)$ has the Quillen dimension property. Then \eqref{eq:HQC} holds for every finite group and every odd prime.
