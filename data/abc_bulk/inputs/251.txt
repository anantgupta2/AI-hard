FAMILY 251: Amenability, unitarizability, and strong Ulam stability
SUBJECT: Group theory
SUMMARY: Resolves Dixmier's problem for all discrete groups: amenability is equivalent to every uniformly bounded Hilbert-space representation being similar to a unitary representation. For countable discrete groups, amenability is also equivalent to strong Ulam stability: sufficiently accurate unitary approximate representations are uniformly close in operator norm to genuine representations on the same, possibly infinite-dimensional, Hilbert space.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Unitarizability implies amenability for discrete groups (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that a discrete group is amenable if and only if every uniformly bounded representation on a complex Hilbert space is similar to a unitary representation. This resolves Dixmier's unitarizability problem affirmatively for discrete groups.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} A discrete group is amenable if and only if it is unitarizable. For every discrete nonamenable group \(G\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), there are a complex Hilbert space \(\mathcal K\) and a representation \(\pi:G\to\mathrm{GL}(\mathcal K)\) with \(|\pi|\le1+\varepsilon\) that is not similar to a unitary representation. If \(G\) is countable, \(\mathcal K\) may be chosen separable.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Strong Ulam Stability Characterizes Amenability (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: A countable discrete group is amenable if and only if it is strongly Ulam stable: every sufficiently accurate unitary almost representation, on any complex Hilbert space, is uniformly close in operator norm to a genuine representation on the same space. We prove the converse to Kazhdan's amenable stability theorem, answering the question of Burger, Ozawa, and Thom. The inclusion of infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces is essential.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} A countable discrete group is strongly Ulam stable if and only if it is amenable.
