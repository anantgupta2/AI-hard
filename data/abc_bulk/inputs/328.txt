FAMILY 328: Nonexpansive fixed points in reflexive Banach spaces
SUBJECT: Functional analysis
SUMMARY: Resolves Kirk's reflexive-space fixed-point problem: every nonexpansive selfmap of a nonempty closed bounded convex subset of a real reflexive Banach space has a fixed point. The result uses the original norm, without assuming uniform convexity.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Fixed Points of Nonexpansive Maps in Reflexive Banach Spaces (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: Every nonexpansive selfmap of a nonempty closed bounded convex subset of a real reflexive Banach space has a fixed point. This resolves the reflexive-space fixed point problem for the given norm.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $X$ be a real reflexive Banach space, and let $C\subseteq X$ be nonempty, norm closed, bounded, and convex. If $F:C\to C$ satisfies \[ \norm{F(a)-F(b)}\le \norm{a-b}\qquad(a,b\in C), \] then $F$ has a fixed point.
[proposition] [An anchor point] \label{prop:anchor} The set $A$ is a nonempty compact semigroup of nonexpansive selfmaps of $K$ and contains an idempotent $P$. There is a point $x\in K$ with $P(x)=x$ such that the nonempty weakly compact set \begin{equation} \label{eq:anchor-tail-set} D_x=\bigcap_{m\geq1} \overline{\{h(x):h\in\mathcal H_m\}}^{\,\mathrm{weak}} \end{equation} satisfies \begin{equation} \label{eq:anchor-convex-hull} x\in\overline{\conv D_x}^{\,\norm{\cdot}}. \end{equation}
[proposition] [Tree data] \label{prop:tree-data} There is an infinite ordered tree with the maps, points, and positive weights described above. Each nonroot node $v$ also has a budget $e_v>0$, a functional $f_v\in X^*$ with $\norm{f_v}\le1$, and the finite earlier-candidate list and predictions just defined. These data have the following properties: \begin{enumerate} \item The budgets satisfy \begin{equation} \sum_{v\ne r}e_v\le\frac1{128}. \label{eq:tree-budget} \end{equation} Define \begin{equation} \tau_0=\frac12,\qquad \tau_k=2^{-k-1} \min\{e_u:u\in L_i,\ 1\le i\le k\}\quad(k\ge1). \label{eq:tree-tolerance} \end{equation} Every node $u\in L_k$ satisfies \begin{equation} \norm{\sum_{\widehat v=u}\alpha_v y_v-x}\le\tau_k. \label{eq:child-average} \end{equation} \item For every nonroot node $v$, \begin{equation} \sum_{p\prec v}\norm{G_p(y_v)-y_v}\le e_v, \qquad f_v(y_v-z)\ge1-e_v\quad(z\in E_v). \lab
