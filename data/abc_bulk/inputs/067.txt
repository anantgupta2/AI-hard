--- MANUSCRIPT: The Campana–Peternell conjecture in dimension six (September-25-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every smooth connected complex projective Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous, resolving the Campana–Peternell conjecture in complex dimension six. As a consequence, a connected compact Kähler manifold X with nef holomorphic tangent bundle and $`\dim_{\mathbb C}X-\widetilde q(X)\leq6`$ has ordinary universal cover $`F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}`$, where F is a rational homogeneous manifold and $`\widetilde q(X)`$ is the maximal irregularity of a connected finite étale cover.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Every smooth connected complex projective Fano variety of dimension six with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous.
[proposition] \label{prop:minimal-family} Let $X$ be a smooth complex Fano manifold of dimension $n$ with nef tangent bundle, and let $V$ be a component of rational curves of minimal anticanonical degree $d$. Then $U$ and $V$ are smooth projective varieties, $\dim V=n+d-3$, the map $\pi:U\to V$ is a smooth $\mathbb P^1$-fibration, and $e:U\to X$ is smooth and surjective with connected fibres of dimension $d-2$. For every fibre $C$ of $\pi$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:relative-tangent-splitting} T_e|_C\simeq\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1}(-1)^{\oplus(d-2)}. \end{equation}
[proposition] \label{prop:pseudoindex-reduction} Let $X$ be a smooth complex Fano sixfold with $\rho(X)=1$ and nef tangent bundle. Then either $X\simeq\mathbb P^6$, $X\simeq Q^6$, or its pseudoindex is $4$ or $5$.
