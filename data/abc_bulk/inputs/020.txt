--- MANUSCRIPT: Squarefree values of quartics and power-free values of polynomials (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every irreducible integer quartic with no fixed prime-square divisor takes squarefree values with the predicted positive Euler-product density. More generally, we obtain the corresponding $`(d-2)`$-power-free density in degrees $`4\le d\le8`$. The proof combines number-field factorization, determinant estimates with adaptive auxiliary primes, and explicit low-degree geometry. Together with Browning's theorem for higher degrees, this gives the $`(d-2)`$-power-free density for every d ≥ 4.
[theorem] \label{thm:density} Let $f\in\Z[x]$ be irreducible over $\Q$, of degree $d$ with $4\le d\le8$, and put $k=d-2$. If \eqref{eq:local-admissibility} holds, then \[ S_{f,k}(X)=c_{f,k}X+o_f(X),\qquad c_{f,k}=\prod_p\left(1-\frac{\rho_f(p^k)}{p^k}\right)>0. \] No primitivity or sign condition on the leading coefficient is required.
[proposition] \label{prop:large-prime-tail} Let $f\in\Z[x]$ be primitive and irreducible, with positive leading coefficient and degree $4\le d\le8$. Put $k=d-2$. There exists $\delta=\delta_f>0$ such that \[ \#\{n\in\Z:X<n\le2X,\ p^k\mid f(n) \text{ for some prime }p>X\} \ll_f X^{1-\delta}. \]
[proposition] [Sieve transfer]\label{prop:sieve-transfer} Let $f\in\Z[x]$ be irreducible over $\Q$, of degree $d\ge2$, and let $k\ge2$. Suppose that $\rho_f(p^k)<p^k$ for every prime $p$. Write \[ f=s c g,\qquad s\in\{-1,1\},\quad c\in\N, \] where $c$ is the positive content of $f$ and $g\in\Z[x]$ is primitive with positive leading coefficient. Assume that \begin{equation} T_g(X):=\#\{n\in\Z:X<n\le2X,\ p^k\mid g(n) \text{ for some prime }p>X\}=o_{g,k}(X). \label{eq:sieve-tail-hypothesis} \end{equation} Then \[ S_{f,k}(N)=c_{f,k}N+o_{f,k}(N),\qquad c_{f,k}=\prod_p\left(1-\frac{\rho_f(p^k)}{p^k}\right)>0. \]
