SUBJECT: Group theory
SUMMARY: Every word-hyperbolic group with boundary homeomorphic to S2 admits a proper cocompact isometric action on hyperbolic three-space with finite kernel, proving Cannon's conjecture. Every torsion-free such group is therefore the fundamental group of a closed hyperbolic three-manifold.

--- MANUSCRIPT: A Modulus Proof of Cannon’s Conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every hyperbolic group whose boundary is homeomorphic to the two-sphere admits a proper cocompact isometric action on hyperbolic three-space with finite kernel. This resolves Cannon's conjecture positively.
[theorem] [Cannon's conjecture] \label{thm:main} Let $G$ be a hyperbolic group with $\partial G$ homeomorphic to $\Sphere$. There is a homomorphism $\rho:G\to\Isom(\HH^3)$ for which the action on $\HH^3$ is proper and cocompact. Its kernel is finite.
[theorem] [Bonk--Kleiner; Bourdon--Kleiner] \label{thm:uniformization} If $\sup_n M(n)<\infty$, then $(Z,d)$ is quasi-M\"obius homeomorphic to the round sphere.
[proposition] [From the boundary to hyperbolic space] \label{prop:action} If $(Z,d)$ is quasi-M\"obius homeomorphic to the round sphere, then $G$ admits a proper cocompact isometric action on $\HH^3$ with finite kernel.
