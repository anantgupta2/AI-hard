--- MANUSCRIPT: The sharp factor-of-IID threshold for the free Ising model on regular trees (September-26-2026)
ABSTRACT: For every integer d ≥ 3 and inverse temperature β ≥ 0, the free zero-field ferromagnetic Ising measure on the infinite d-regular tree is a factor of IID exactly when $`\tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}`$. We prove the positive implication, including equality, resolving the conjecture of Nam, Sly and Zhang; the strict converse is known. The spin factor can be chosen to commute with every tree automorphism on every label input.
[theorem] \label{thm:main}\label{thm:sharp} For every integer $d\ge3$ and every $\beta\ge0$, the free zero-field Ising law $\mu_{d,\beta}$ is a factor of IID in the sense of \eqref{eq:equivariance} if and only if \[ \tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}. \] In this range the factor can be chosen to commute with every automorphism on every label input.
[proposition] [Time-integrable response rows] \label{prop:row-energy} For every vertex \(v\), there is \(C<\infty\), depending only on \(d\) at the critical parameter, such that \begin{equation} \label{eq:row-energy} \begin{aligned} A_v(t)&\le1+\frac{d\theta^2}{1-b\theta^2} &&\text{if }b\theta^2<1,\quad t\ge0,\\ \E A_v(t)&\le\frac{C}{\omega(t)} &&\text{if }b\theta^2=1,\quad t>0,\\ \E\int_0^T A_v(t)\dd t&<\infty &&\text{for every }T<\infty\text{ in either case}. \end{aligned} \end{equation} The same fixed-time and integrated upper bounds hold with \(\sum_u C_{vu}(t)^2\) in place of \(A_v(t)\).
[proposition] [Infinite innovation equations] \label{prop:innovations} In the planted observation law $X_v(t)=t\sigma_v+B_v(t)$, every finite subvector of $(W_v)_{v\in\mathcal V}$ is a standard vector Brownian motion in both the raw observation filtration $(\mathcal F_t^0)$ and its usual augmentation $(\mathcal F_t)$. In particular $W$ has product Wiener law.
