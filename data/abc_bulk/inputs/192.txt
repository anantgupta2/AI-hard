FAMILY 192: Boolean functions violate the square-root degree bound by arbitrary factors
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
SUMMARY: Disproves the proposed square-root bound relating a Boolean function's linear Fourier coefficients to its polynomial degree. For every C &gt; 0, there is a sign-valued Boolean function f with $`\sum_i\widehat f(\{i\})\gt C\sqrt{\deg(f)}`$. Thus its total signed correlation with individual input bits can exceed the proposed bound by an arbitrary factor.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Unbounded Violations of the Square-Root Degree Bound (September-26-2026)
ABSTRACT: We disprove the Gopalan–Servedio square-root conjecture, even up to an arbitrary constant factor. For every real C &gt; 0, there is a nonconstant Boolean function $`f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}`$ on a finite sign cube such that
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every real \(C>0\), there are a positive integer \(n\) and a nonconstant Boolean function \(f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}\) such that \[ \sum_{i=1}^n\widehat f(\{i\})>C\sqrt{\deg(f)}. \] Consequently, \[ \sup_{\substack{n\ge1,\ f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}\\\deg(f)>0}} \frac{\sum_i|\widehat f(\{i\})|}{\sqrt{\deg(f)}}=\infty. \]
[proposition] [Amplification criterion]\label{prop:amplification} Let \(1\le d<r\) be integers, and let \(\mathcal W\) be a measurable observation rule on \(r\) real inputs. Suppose: \begin{enumerate} \item On any product of finite input alphabets, the indicator of each output value is a finite linear combination of functions each depending on at most \(d\) input coordinates. \item There is an \(\eta>0\) such that, whenever centered variance-one laws \(Z_j\) converge weakly to a standard normal with uniformly bounded fourth moments, independent copies satisfy, for all sufficiently large \(j\), \[ \Var\!\left(\E\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^r Z_{j,i}\,\middle|\, \mathcal W(\mathbf Z_j)\right]\right)\ge d+\eta. \] \end{enumerate} Then finite observations have arbitrarily large retained variance-to-cell-degree ratios, and Theorem~\ref{thm:main} follows.
[theorem] [Amplification from a mean cost advantage] \label{thm:mean-cost} Let $q\ge2$, let $\mathcal P:\R^q\to\mathcal L$ be a measurable map to a finite label set, and let $\ell:\mathcal L\to\{1,\ldots,q\}$. Suppose that, on every product of finite input alphabets, each cell indicator for $l$ is a finite linear combination of functions of at most $\ell(l)$ input coordinates. Suppose also that every cell has boundary of standard Gaussian measure zero in $\R^q$. For independent standard normals $G_1,\ldots,G_q$, put \[ S_G=\sum_{i=1}^qG_i,\qquad V=\Var(\E[S_G\mid\mathcal P(G)]),\qquad c=\E\ell(\mathcal P(G)). \] If $V>c$, then finite observations on sign cubes have arbitrarily large ratios $v(F)/D$, where $D>0$ is a cell degree bound.
