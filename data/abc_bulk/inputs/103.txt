ABSTRACT: We prove $`\mathsf L=\mathsf{RL}=\mathsf{BPL}`$, resolving the derandomization problem for polynomial-time randomized logarithmic space.
[theorem] [Exact logarithmic-space derandomization]\label{thm:main} \[ \Lclass=\RL=\BPL. \]
[theorem] [Acceptance-probability approximation]\label{thm:probability} Fix a randomized machine $\mathcal M$ with polynomial worst-case running time and $O(\log(n+2))$ work space. Let $p_{\mathcal M}(x)$ be its acceptance probability on an input $x$ of length $n$. There is a uniform deterministic algorithm which, on input $(x,1^q)$ with $q\ge1$, outputs an integer $0\le a\le2^{q+2}$ satisfying \[ \left|a2^{-(q+2)}-p_{\mathcal M}(x)\right|\le2^{-q}. \] It uses $O(\log(n+2)+q)$ work space and $(n+2)^{O(1)}2^{O(q)}$ time. The constants depend only on $\mathcal M$.
[proposition] [One-stage density and active-count reduction] \label{prop:hier-copy-step} Choose the detector rank $J$ large enough that \begin{equation} J\geq\frac{2C_\pi(D_0-1)k_*(k_*+1)}{\delta_0}, \label{eq:hier-active-J} \end{equation} and then choose $R_*$ as in Equation~\eqref{eq:hier-column-constants}. The matrix $C^+$ is strictly forward and substochastic, and \[ C^+(x_i,y_j)\leq f/q, \qquad |\mathcal A(C^+)|\leq2|\mathcal A(C)|. \] If $P^r$ is the matrix from $V^+$ to $V$ defined by \begin{equation} P^r(x_i,x)=r_i(x),\qquad P^r(x_i,z)=0\quad(z\ne x), \label{eq:hier-projection} \end{equation} then \begin{equation} C^+P^r=P^rD. \label{eq:hier-one-step-intertwining} \end{equation}
