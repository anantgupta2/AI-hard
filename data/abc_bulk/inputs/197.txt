FAMILY 197: A torsion-free group algebra that is not directly finite
SUBJECT: Algebra
SUMMARY: Constructs a finitely presented torsion-free nonsofic group whose group algebra over 𝔽2 is not directly finite, disproving Kaplansky's conjecture even without torsion. Companion examples give injective nonsurjective cellular automata on all configurations, refuting Gottschalk's surjunctivity conjecture. Another counterexample is an integral group-ring matrix, invertible over the rational group ring, with Fuglede–Kadison determinant strictly between zero and one, disproving the unrestricted Determinant Conjecture.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: A Counterexample to the Group-Ring Determinant Conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We disprove the unrestricted group-ring Determinant Conjecture. The logarithmic integral defining the determinant is finite.

Thus the unrestricted group-ring Determinant Conjecture has a negative resolution.
