--- MANUSCRIPT: A counterexample to the nearby Lagrangian conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We disprove the unrestricted nearby Lagrangian conjecture. For some sufficiently large even integer N, we construct a closed exact smoothly embedded Lagrangian in $`T^*(S^9\times S^{N-1})`$ that is diffeomorphic to the base but is not Hamiltonian isotopic to the zero section.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For some sufficiently large even integer $N$, the manifold $Q=S^9\times S^{N-1}$ admits a closed exact smoothly embedded Lagrangian $L\subset T^*Q$ such that $L$ is diffeomorphic to $Q$ and no compactly supported Hamiltonian isotopy carries the zero section to $L$.
[theorem] [Waldhausen's tube fibration]\label{thm:tube-fibration} For the stable smooth tube space \eqref{eq:stable-tubes} there is a homotopy fiber sequence \begin{equation}\label{eq:tube-fibration} \Hs(*)\longrightarrow\T\xrightarrow{\gamma}BG. \end{equation} The rigid tube map $\iota:BO\to\T$, defined by nondegenerate quadratic forms, satisfies $\gamma\circ\iota\simeq BJ$, where $BJ:BO\to BG$ is the stable spherical fibration map.
[proposition] \label{prop:kernel-class} There exists \begin{equation}\label{eq:kernel-class} 0\ne a\in\pi_9\T,\qquad \gamma_*a=0. \end{equation} Moreover, $(BJ)_*:\pi_9BO\to\pi_9BG$ is injective.
