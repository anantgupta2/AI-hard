FAMILY 053: A counterexample to Pixton completeness in Chow
SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
SUMMARY: Constructs a tautological relation on a moduli space of stable pointed curves that vanishes in rational Chow, hence in rational cohomology, but lies outside Pixton's original relation span. This disproves the Chow and rational-cohomological forms of his original completeness conjecture.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: A high-arity counterexample to Pixton completeness in Chow (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We disprove the Chow and rational-cohomological forms of Pixton's completeness conjecture for his original relation system. We construct a formal tautological class outside the original Pixton relation span whose image is zero in the Chow ring and in rational cohomology. The example has genus $`10^{60}`$ and $`3\binom{10^{60}}3`$ markings.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Set \[ g=10^{60},\qquad D_*=\binom g3,\qquad n=3D_*. \] The explicitly defined class \(Y\in \Salg_{g,n}^{2D_*}\) of Definition~\ref{def:candidate} satisfies \[ q_{g,n}(Y)=0,\qquad Y\notin\Pspan_{g,n}. \] Consequently Pixton's original relation span does not exhaust the tautological Chow relations: the equality \(\ker q_{g,n}=\Pspan_{g,n}\) fails for this stable pair.
[proposition] [Known validity and stability]\label{prop:known-relations} The span in Definition~\ref{def:pixton-span} is an ideal and is stable under tautological gluing and forgetting pushforwards and pullbacks. For gluing pullback to a product, the target relation space is the sum of the spaces with a relation in one factor. Moreover \[ \Pspan_{g,n}\subseteq\ker q_{g,n}. \]
[proposition] \label{prop:detector} There are a supercommutative \(\Q\)-algebra \(R\), a finite free supermodule \(V\) over \(R\), and \(\Q\)-linear maps \[ t_m:\Salg_{g,m}\longrightarrow V^{\otimes_R m} \qquad(m\geq 0) \] such that \(t_m(\Pspan_{g,m})=0\) for every \(m\), whereas \(t_n(Y)\neq0\). In particular \(Y\notin\Pspan_{g,n}\).
