--- MANUSCRIPT: A finite-time singularity of Calabi flow on projective space (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct a smooth Kähler metric in the Fubini–Study class of $`\mathbb{CP}^{10}`$ whose Calabi flow develops unbounded scalar curvature in finite time. This disproves Chen's smooth long-time existence conjecture, even in a class containing a constant-scalar-curvature metric.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is a smooth \(U(10)\)-invariant K\"ahler form \(\omega_0\) on \(\CP^{10}\), in the class \([\omega_{\mathrm{FS}}]\), whose maximal smooth Calabi flow has existence interval \([0,T_*)\) with \(0<T_*<\infty\). There are a point \(p\in\CP^{10}\) and a constant \(a>0\) such that \[ S(\omega(t))(p)\sim a(T_*-t)^{-1/2}\qquad\text{as }t\uparrow T_*. \]
[proposition] [Exact fixed-class reduction]\label{geo:reduction} Let \(I\) be an open time interval, and let \(H\in C^\infty(I\times[0,1])\) satisfy \begin{equation}\label{geo:positivity} b(t,x):=1+v_*(x)H(t,x)>0. \end{equation} Define \begin{equation}\label{geo:momentum} v=v_*+v_*^2H=v_*b,\qquad K(H)=w^{-1}(wv_*^2H)'',\qquad \A H=\big[w^{-1}(wv_*^2H)''\big]''. \end{equation} At each time these data, together with an affine choice in the Legendre potential, determine a smooth \(U(n)\)-invariant K\"ahler form in \([\omega_*]\). Its scalar curvature and average scalar curvature are \begin{equation}\label{geo:scalar} S(t,x)=n(n+1)-K(H)(t,x),\qquad \bar S=n(n+1). \end{equation} If, in addition, \begin{equation}\label{geo:equation} H_t+(1+v_*H)^2\A H=0, \end{equation} then for any \(t_0\in I\) and any initial affine choice there is a smooth family of these forms satisfying the Calabi flow exactly.
