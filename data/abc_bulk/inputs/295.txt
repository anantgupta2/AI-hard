FAMILY 295: The Kadison–Ringrose cohomology conjecture
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
SUMMARY: Proves that every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree at least two on a complex von Neumann algebra, with coefficients in the algebra itself, has a bounded primitive. Equivalently, all higher bounded Hochschild cohomology groups vanish, resolving the Kadison–Ringrose conjecture.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Vanishing of higher bounded Hochschild cohomology (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree at least two on a complex von Neumann algebra, with values in the algebra itself, has a bounded primitive. Together with the established degree-one inner-derivation theorem, this resolves the Kadison–Ringrose cohomology conjecture positively.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(M\) be a complex von Neumann algebra and let \(k\geq2\). Every \(f\in C_b^k(M,M)\) satisfying \(df=0\) has the form \(f=dg\) for some \(g\in C_b^{k-1}(M,M)\). Consequently \(H_b^k(M,M)=0\).
[proposition] [Classical reductions]\label{prop:classical} Let \(M\) be a von Neumann algebra and \(k\geq2\). \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item If \(f\in C_b^k(M,M)\) is a cocycle, there are a separately normal bounded cocycle \(f_0\) and a bounded \((k-1)\)-cochain \(b\) such that \(f=f_0+db\). \item If \(M\) has no type \(\mathrm{II}_1\) central summand, then \(H_b^k(M,M)=0\). \end{enumerate}
[theorem] \label{thm:walk} Let $M$ be a finite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ von Neumann algebra with separable predual and faithful normal tracial state $\tau$. There exist a symmetric, countably supported probability measure $\mu$ on $\U(M)$ and a strictly increasing sequence $n_\ell\to\infty$ with the following properties. The positive-mass atoms of $\mu$ contain a countable $2$-norm dense subset of $\U(M)$, and hence generate $M$ as a von Neumann algebra. For every $r\in\N$, every nonempty finite set $\mathcal W\subset\mathbb F_r\setminus\{e\}$, and every $\eta>0$, if $G_1^{(\ell)},\ldots,G_r^{(\ell)}$ are independent with law $\mu^{*n_\ell}$, then \[ \Pr\left( \max_{w\in\mathcal W} \left|\tau\bigl(w(G_1^{(\ell)},\ldots,G_r^{(\ell)})\bigr)\right| \geq\eta \right)\longrightarrow0. \] Thus these products have joint free Haar moments in probability.
