FAMILY 254: Classifying spaces and geometric obstructions for Artin groups
SUBJECT: Group theory
SUMMARY: The Salvetti complex of every finite-rank Artin group is aspherical, proving the Artin $`K(\pi,1)`$ conjecture. Arbitrary intersections of its parabolic subgroups are parabolic, proving the Parabolic Intersection Conjecture. An explicit Artin group admits no proper cocompact isometric action on any nonempty proper CAT$`(0)`$ space.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Harmonic heights and the Artin K(pi,1) conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the standard Salvetti complex of every Artin group with finitely many standard generators is aspherical. This resolves the Artin $`K(\pi,1)`$ conjecture in finite rank.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every Coxeter matrix on a finite set \(S\), the universal cover of \(X(W,S)\) is contractible. Equivalently, \[ \pi_n\bigl(X(W,S)\bigr)=0\qquad(n\ge2). \] The conclusion allows arbitrary finite labels, infinite labels, and connected or disconnected diagrams.
[theorem] [Standard parabolic embedding] \label{thm:topology-parabolic-embedding} For every $T\subseteq S$, the homomorphism from the Artin group defined by the submatrix on $T$ to $A$, sending its standard generators to the corresponding $\sigma_t$, is injective. Its image is $A_T$.
[theorem] [Finite-type positive monoids] \label{thm:topology-finite-monoid} Suppose that $T$ is spherical, and let $A_T^+$ be its positive Artin monoid. Then the following statements hold. \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}] \item The natural map $A_T^+\to A_T$ is injective. In particular, $A_T^+$ is cancellative on both sides. \item Every element of $A_T$ can be written both as $ab^{-1}$ and as $c^{-1}d$, with $a,b,c,d\in A_T^+$. \item Any two elements of $A_T^+$ have a greatest common right divisor, and also a greatest common left divisor. \item If $w_T$ is the longest element of $W_T$, then $\Delta_T=\iota(w_T)$ is the finite-type fundamental element. Its left divisors and its right divisors coincide and are exactly the elements $\iota(w)$, $w\in W_T$. \end{enumerate}

--- MANUSCRIPT: An Artin group with no geometric CAT(0) action (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct an Artin group on 116 generators that admits no proper, cocompact isometric action on a nonempty proper CAT(0) space. This refutes the CAT(0) conjecture for Artin groups.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is an Artin matrix \(M\) on 116 generators, with every off-diagonal label in \(\{2,3,\infty\}\), such that \(A_M\) admits no geometric action. One explicit matrix is given in Definition~\ref{def:matrix}.
[proposition] \label{prop:braid-angle} Suppose a group acting geometrically on a CAT(0) space contains elements $s_1,\ldots,s_{39}$ satisfying the $B_{40}$ relations, with $\ell(s_1^2)>0$. Then \begin{equation}\label{eq:braid-angle-bound} \left(\frac{\ell((s_1s_2)^3)}{3\ell(s_1^2)}\right)^2 \ge\frac{143}{513}>\frac14, \end{equation} and in particular \[ \frac{\ell((s_1s_2)^3)}{3\ell(s_1^2)} =\frac{\ell((s_1s_2)^3)}{3\ell(s_2^2)}>\frac12. \]
[proposition] \label{prop:triangle-obstruction} There is no geometric action of \(T\) such that, for every \((u,v)\in\mathcal E\), \begin{equation}\label{eq:strict-ratios} \frac{\ell(z_{uv})}{3\ell(u^2)} =\frac{\ell(z_{uv})}{3\ell(v^2)}>\frac12. \end{equation}
