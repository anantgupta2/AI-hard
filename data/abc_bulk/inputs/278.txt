FAMILY 278: Failure of Kohn–Sham ensemble representation
SUBJECT: Mathematical physics
SUMMARY: Constructs a three-electron Coulomb molecule with two equal positive-integer-charge nuclei whose absolute ground-state density has no noninteracting ground-state ensemble representation by a single real spin-independent local potential in $`L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^3)+L^\infty(\mathbb R^3)`$. This disproves Kohn–Sham ensemble representability for that potential class; the required nuclear charge is specified nonnumerically.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: A Coulomb ground-state density without Kohn-Sham ensemble representation (September-25-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct a finite three-electron Coulomb molecule whose spin-summed ground-state density cannot be reproduced by any ground-state ensemble of noninteracting electrons in a single real, spin-independent local potential in $`L^{3/2}(\mathbf R^3)+L^\infty(\mathbf R^3)`$. The molecule has two equal positive integer nuclear charges, specified by an exact finite nonnumerical formula.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Set \(D=10^{200}\) and \(e_z=(0,0,1)\). There is a finite positive integer \(Z\), specified by \Cref{eq:explicit-charge}, for which the Coulomb Hamiltonian \[ H_C=\sum_{i=1}^3 \left(-\frac12\Delta_i -\frac{Z}{|r_i-(D/Z)e_z|} -\frac{Z}{|r_i+(D/Z)e_z|}\right) +\sum_{1\le i<j\le3}\frac1{|r_i-r_j|} \] has a normalizable absolute ground state \(\Psi\) in the sector \(S_z=1/2\), normalized by \(\|\Psi\|_2=1\), but there is no \(v\in\mathcal V\) and no ground-state ensemble \(\Gamma\) of \(H_s(v)\) such that \[ n_\Gamma=n_\Psi\qquad\text{almost everywhere on }\R^3. \]
[proposition] [Dilation to integer nuclear charges] \label{prop:scaling} Suppose that the density of a ground state of \(H_{3,1/Z}\), for a positive integer \(Z\), has no noninteracting ground-state ensemble representation with a real spin-independent potential in \(L^{3/2}(\R^3)+L^\infty(\R^3)\). The physical Coulomb system with three electrons and charges \(Z,Z\) at \[ (0,0,D/Z),\qquad (0,0,-D/Z) \] then has a ground-state density with the same obstruction.
[proposition] [Two-centre spectrum] \label{prop:two-centre-spectrum} For \(D\ge100\), the two lowest eigenvalues of \(h_D\) are simple. They have normalized real, axisymmetric eigenfunctions \(b,e\), where \(b>0\) is even and \(e\) is odd and strictly positive on \(z>0\). Write \[ \epsilon_b=-\frac{\gamma^2}{2},\qquad \epsilon_e=-\frac{k_0^2}{2},\qquad d_*=\epsilon_e-\epsilon_b . \] Then \begin{equation} 0<d_*<\frac{100}{D},\qquad |\gamma-1|+|k_0-1|<\frac{200}{D}. \label{eq:two-centre-numerics} \end{equation} The spectrum on \(\{b,e\}^{\perp}\) lies more than \(0.2\) above \(\epsilon_e\). In particular, the spectral gap above \(b\) in the even subspace is greater than \(0.2\).
