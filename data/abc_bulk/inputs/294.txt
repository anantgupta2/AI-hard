--- MANUSCRIPT: A counterexample to Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture and failure of tensor-product stable finiteness (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We refute Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture by constructing a separable unital complex C∗-algebra that admits normalized 2-quasitraces but has no tracial state. As a consequence, we show that the minimal tensor product of two unital simple stably finite complex C∗-algebras can be properly infinite, even when one factor is the reduced free-group algebra $`C_r^*(\mathbb F_2)`$.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exist a separable unital complex \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\), admitting a normalized \(2\)-quasitrace, and positive contractions \(a,b\in A\) such that every normalized \(2\)-quasitrace \(\tau\) on \(A\) satisfies \[ \tau(a+b)-\tau(a)-\tau(b)\ge\frac1{144}. \]
[theorem] [Quasitrace existence] \label{thm:existence} A unital \(C^*\)-algebra \(C\) admits a normalized \(2\)-quasitrace if and only if \(M_m(C)\) is not properly infinite for every positive integer \(m\).
[proposition] \label{prop:bundle-test} For every pair of integers $m,R\geq 1$, set \begin{equation}\label{eq:test-ranks} d_0=64m4^R,\qquad d_j=4^j d_0\quad(0\leq j\leq R). \end{equation} There are a compact smooth manifold $Y$, Hermitian complex vector bundles $E_j\to Y$ for all $j\geq0$, and continuous bundle maps $X_i^{(j)}:E_j\to E_{j+1}$, for $1\leq i\leq24$ and $j\geq0$, such that $E_0$ is trivial of rank $d_0$, the rank of $E_j$ is $d_j$ for $0\leq j\leq R$, and \begin{equation}\label{bundle:scaled-identities} \sum_{i=1}^{24}(X_i^{(j)})^*X_i^{(j)}=I_{E_j}, \qquad \sum_{i=1}^{24}X_i^{(j)}(X_i^{(j)})^* \leq\frac23 I_{E_{j+1}}. \end{equation} Moreover, \begin{equation}\label{eq:chern-obstruction} \begin{gathered} \left(\bigoplus_{j=0}^R E_j\right)^{\oplus m}\\ \text{contains no trivial subbundle of rank }2md_0. \end{gathered} \end{equation}
