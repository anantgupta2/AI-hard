FAMILY 265: Area laws and tensor networks for two-dimensional gapped systems
SUBJECT: Mathematical physics
SUMMARY: Proves an entropy area law for unique ground states of finite-range Hamiltonians on arbitrary finite induced square-lattice domains, using only a uniform full-system spectral gap and bounds on the local interactions. On open $`L\times L`$ squares, uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor ground states also admit projected entangled-pair state approximations with polynomial bond dimension and global vector error at most L−1.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove an entropy area law for the unique ground state of a finite-range Hamiltonian on any finite induced subgraph of the square lattice. A lower bound on the spectral gap of the full Hamiltonian and fixed bounds on the local dimension, interaction range, and interaction strength suffice. For every set of sites, its entanglement entropy is bounded by a constant times the number of edges crossing its boundary, independently of the size and shape of the domain.
[theorem] [Area law]\label{thm:area} For every $q,R,J$ as above and every $\Delta>0$, there is a finite constant $C=C(q,R,J,\Delta)$ with the following property. Suppose that $\Lambda$ is a finite induced square-lattice domain and that the Hamiltonian \eqref{eq:hamiltonian} has a unique normalized ground vector $\Omega$, up to phase, with ground energy $E_0$, and satisfies \[ H-E_0\id\ \ge\ \Delta\bigl(\id-\ket{\Omega}\bra{\Omega}\bigr). \] Then for every $A\subseteq\Lambda$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:area-law} S_\Omega(A)\le C\,|\partial_\Lambda A|. \end{equation} The same constant applies to all such domains, sets, and Hamiltonians.
[proposition] [Initial safe-box estimate] \label{prop:initial-box} There are constants $C<\infty$ and $e_0\in(0,1)$, depending only on $q,R,J,\Delta$, such that \begin{equation} F_{\mathrm{box}}(r)\le Cr^{1+e_0}\qquad(r\ge1). \label{eq:initial-box-bound} \end{equation}
[proposition] [Positive replacement of the Hamiltonian] \label{prop:positive} Suppose $H\Omega=E_0\Omega$, $\|\Omega\|=1$, and \[ H-E_0I\ge\Delta(I-P_\Omega),\qquad P_\Omega=|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|. \] For each integer $p\ge1$ and $\alpha=p/(p+1)$, there are operators $k_i$, indexed by the original supports and retaining their anchors, and a constant $c_*>0$ such that \begin{equation} 0\le k_i\le I,\qquad k_i\Omega=0,\qquad H_F:=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I}k_i \ge c_*^{-1}(H-E_0I)\ge g(I-P_\Omega), \qquad g:=\Delta/c_*. \label{eq:quasilocal-positive} \end{equation} Thus $H_F$ has the unique ground vector $\Omega$, ground energy zero, and gap at least $g$. Furthermore, \begin{equation} k_{i,l}:=E_{i,l}(k_i)\in[0,I],\qquad \|k_i-k_{i,l}\|\le Ce^{-cl^\alpha} \quad(l\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}). \label{eq:quasilocal-positive-tail} \end{equation} Each $k_i$ is supported in the connected component containing $a_i$.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Polynomial PEPS approximation of gapped square-grid ground states (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the unique ground state of a uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian on an $`L\times L`$ square lattice admits a projected entangled-pair state approximation with bond dimension polynomial in L and global vector error at most L−1 after normalization. Only the gap of the full Hamiltonian is assumed. The result is an existence theorem, with constants uniform over Hamiltonians of fixed local dimension, interaction strength, and gap.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Fix an integer $q\ge2$ and constants $J,\Delta>0$. There are constants $C,c>0$, depending only on $q,J,\Delta$, with the following property. For every $L\ge2$, consider any Hermitian Hamiltonian \begin{equation}\label{eq:model} H=\sum_{v\in\Lambda_L}h_v+\sum_{e\in E(\Lambda_L)}h_e, \qquad \norm{h_v},\norm{h_e}\le J, \end{equation} where each summand is supported on its designated site or edge. Suppose that $H$ has a unique normalized ground vector $\Omega$, up to phase, with ground energy $E_0$, and \begin{equation}\label{eq:global-gap} H-E_0I\ge\Delta\bigl(I-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\bigr). \end{equation} Then there is a nonzero PEPS $\Phi$ on the same grid with maximum bond dimension at most $CL^c$ such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:target-error} \min_{\theta\in\mathbb R} \left\|\frac{\Phi}{\norm{\Phi}}-e^{i\theta}\Omega\right\| \le L^{-1}. \end{equation}
[proposition] [Entropy area law, {\cite[Theorem~1.1 and Corollary~1.2]{RepoAreaLaw}}] \label{prop:area-law} Under \eqref{eq:model}--\eqref{eq:global-gap}, there is $C_A=C_A(q,J,\Delta)$ such that every $A\subseteq\Lambda$ satisfies \[ S_\Omega(A)\le C_A|\partial_\Lambda A|, \qquad \partial_\Lambda A=\{\{x,y\}\in E(\Lambda):x\in A, \ y\notin A\}. \] The same constant works for every $L$ and every Hamiltonian in the class.
[proposition] [Coarse information outside a sublinear collar] \label{prop:coarse} Fix an integer $N_0\ge1$ and a constant $A_0\ge1$. There are $\beta<1$, $C<\infty$, and $u_0<\infty$ such that the following holds. Let $u\ge u_0$ and let $T$ be the full ambient lattice sample of a union of at most $N_0$ axis-parallel rectangles, with $\diam_\infty T\le A_0u$. For every physical set $E\subseteq\Lambda$ satisfying \[ \dist_\infty(E,T)>u^\beta, \] one has \[ I_\Omega(\Lambda\cap T:E)\le C. \] The same statement holds for any subset of $\Lambda\cap T$ in its first argument, for rectangles with consistently assigned open or closed sides, and after a common translation of the lattice coordinates.
