FAMILY 167: Planar distinct distances and unit-distance bounds
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
SUMMARY: Proves the weak pinned Erdős distance conjecture: for every fixed ε &gt; 0, all but $`o(n)`$ points of any n-point planar set determine at least $`n^{1-\varepsilon}`$ distinct nonzero distances. A complementary theorem bounds the number of unit-distance pairs by $`O(n^{4/3-\delta})`$ for an absolute δ &gt; 0.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: The weak pinned planar distance theorem (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the weak pinned Erdős distinct-distance conjecture. For every fixed ε &gt; 0, all but $`o(n)`$ points of any n-point planar set determine at least $`n^{1-\varepsilon}`$ distinct nonzero distances.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every fixed $s>0$, one has $F_n(s)\longrightarrow0$ as $n\longrightarrow\infty$.
[proposition] \label{prop:graph} Under the supposition above, there is a sequence of finite sets $P\subset\R^2$ of real algebraic points, with $n=|P|\to\infty$, and nonempty directed graphs $E$ on $P$, with the following properties. Their densities $d_n=|E|/[n(n-1)]$ tend to $\theta$, and every fiber has $k_e\ge n^{s-o(1)}$, uniformly in $e$. --- MANUSCRIPT: A power saving for planar unit distances (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove a power saving for the planar unit-distance problem: for some absolute $`\beta\lt 4/3`$, every set of n points in the Euclidean plane determines $`O(n^\beta)`$ unordered pairs at unit distance.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There are absolute constants \(0<C<\infty\) and \(1\le\beta<4/3\) such that \(u(n)\le Cn^\beta\) for every nonnegative integer \(n\).
[proposition] \label{inc:incidence} For \(m\) distinct points and \(l\) distinct unit circles in the plane, \begin{equation}\label{inc:incidence-bound} I\le C\log^2(2+m+l) \bigl(m^{2/3}l^{2/3}+m+l\bigr), \end{equation} where \(C\) is an absolute constant. In particular, when \(m,l\) are bounded by a fixed power of \(t\), the logarithmic factor is \((\log t)^{O(1)}\).
[proposition] \label{inc:piece} From a sequence of \(n=t^3\) real planar points with at least \(t^{4-o(1)}\) ordered unit pairs, one can obtain real algebraic point and center sets \(P,Q\), and a nonempty graph \(E\subseteq P\times Q\) consisting of unit incidences, with the following properties. There is a parameter \(a=a(t)>0\) such that \(a\to0\), \(a\log t\to\infty\), and \begin{equation}\label{inc:scales} |P|=t^{1+2a+o(a)},\qquad |Q|=t^{2+a+o(a)},\qquad |E|=t^{2+2a+o(a)}. \end{equation} Writing \(d(p),d(q)\) for graph degrees, uniformly over all vertices, \begin{equation}\label{inc:degrees} d(p)=t^{1+o(a)},\qquad d(q)=t^{a+o(a)}. \end{equation} The uniform law on \(E\) has marginals \(\mu(p)=d(p)/|E|\) and \(\kappa(q)=d(q)/|E|\) satisfying \begin{equation}\label{inc:marginal-atoms} \mu(p)\ge\frac{\gamma}{|P|},\qquad \kappa(q)\ge\frac{\gamma}{|Q|},\qquad \gamma=\frac{27}{50}=0.54. \end{equation} S
