SUMMARY: Proves Bloch's conjecture: for every smooth connected projective complex surface S with $`p_g(S)=0`$, the Albanese map $`\mathrm{CH}_0(S)^0\to\mathrm{Alb}(S)(\mathbb C)`$ on integral degree-zero zero-cycles is an isomorphism. --- MANUSCRIPT: Bloch’s conjecture for surfaces with p_g=0 (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Bloch's conjecture for smooth connected projective complex surfaces with $`p_g=0`$: the Albanese homomorphism on integral degree-zero zero-cycles is an isomorphism.
[theorem] \label{thm:bloch} Let \(S\) be a smooth connected projective surface over \(\mathbb C\) with \(p_g(S)=q(S)=0\). Then \[ \deg:\CH_0(S)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z \] is an isomorphism, where \(\CH_0(S)\) has integral coefficients.
[proposition] \label{prop:reduction} To prove Theorem~\ref{thm:bloch} with rational coefficients, it is enough to prove it for smooth minimal surfaces \(X_0\) of general type with \(p_g=q=0\). For such a surface, putting \(k_0=K_{X_0}^2\), one has \begin{equation}\label{eq:reduction-invariants} 1\le k_0\le9,\qquad \chi(\mathcal O_{X_0})=1,\qquad \int_{X_0}c_2(X_0)=12-k_0,\qquad b_2(X_0)=10-k_0. \end{equation} It is also enough to prove the assertion on any chosen sequence of point blowups of each such \(X_0\).
[proposition] [The determinant-fixed Quot theory]\label{prop:quot-pot} In either setting~\textup{(i)} or~\textup{(ii)}, the enhancement $\mathfrak B$ induces a perfect obstruction theory $E_B\to L_B$, with virtual tangent \begin{equation}\label{eq:quot-tangent} E_B^\vee\simeq \fib\left\{ \RHom(S',Q)\xrightarrow{d\det^o} \RGamma(\mathcal I_o)[1]\otimes\mathcal O_B \right\}. \end{equation} It is $G$-equivariant. Its virtual dimension is \begin{equation}\label{eq:vdim} v=2n+(A-K)D. \end{equation}
