--- MANUSCRIPT: An isomorphism of the free group factors (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We solve the free group factor isomorphism problem affirmatively by proving that $`L(\mathbb F_2)`$ and $`L(\mathbb F_3)`$ are isomorphic as tracial von Neumann algebras. The classical free group factor alternative then implies that all interpolated free group factors, including $`L(\mathbb F_\infty)`$, are isomorphic and have fundamental group $`\mathbb R_{\gt 0}`$.
[theorem] [Isomorphism of consecutive ranks] \label{thm:rank-step} For every integer $n\geq3$ there is a unital trace-preserving normal $*$-isomorphism \[ L(\F_n)\cong L(\F_{n+1}). \]
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exists a unital normal trace-preserving $*$-isomorphism \[ \Phi:L(\F_2)\longrightarrow L(\F_3). \]
