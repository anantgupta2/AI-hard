SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
SUMMARY: Proves that the Thorp shuffle randomizes $`N=2^d`$ labeled cards in $`\Theta(\log N)`$ physical shuffles, settling its optimal mixing order for power-of-two deck sizes.
