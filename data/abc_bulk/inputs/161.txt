FAMILY 161: Counterexamples to Sidorenko’s conjecture and the forcing conjecture
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
SUMMARY: Disproves Sidorenko's conjecture with a connected bipartite pattern on 35 vertices and 66 edges that occurs less frequently than in a random graph of the same edge density. The same pattern disproves the forcing conjecture of Skokan and Thoma: matching its density and the edge density of a constant graphon need not force quasirandomness.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: A counterexample to Sidorenko's conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We disprove Sidorenko's conjecture with a bipartite graph on 35 vertices and 66 edges: its homomorphism density in some finite simple graph is smaller than the conjectured lower bound. The same connected graph also disproves the forcing conjecture: at one fixed density, asymptotically matching the edge and pattern densities does not imply quasirandomness.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(H\) be the incidence graph of the \(22\) triples on \(13\) points in Table~\ref{tab:complex}. This bipartite graph has \(35\) vertices and \(66\) edges. There is a finite simple undirected graph \(G\) with at least one edge such that \[ t(H,G)<p(G)^{66}. \]
[proposition] \label{prop:complex} The complex has the following properties. \begin{enumerate} \item Every pair in $E$ lies in exactly two faces, $|E|=33$, and the face-neighbor graph is connected. \item For each bit position, each of its two classes consists of $11$ faces covering all $13$ points. Each class has an ordering in which every face after the first shares a pair with an earlier face and introduces exactly one new point. \item Every $a_e$ is at least $1/3$, and some $a_e$ is greater than $1/3$. \item Color refinement of $H$, starting with a separate color for each part, eventually gives distinct colors to all vertices within either part. \end{enumerate}
[proposition] \label{prop:negative-kernel} Assuming Lemma~\ref{lem:rank-layer}, there is a symmetric kernel $U:\Omega\times\Omega\to[0,1]$ on a finite probability space with mean $\mu>0$ such that \begin{equation} t(H,U)<\mu^{66}. \label{eq:kernel-symmetric-gap} \end{equation}
