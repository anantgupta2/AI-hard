--- MANUSCRIPT: Global Uniqueness for the Smooth Isotropic Elasticity Inverse Problem (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the full static displacement-to-traction map uniquely determines both real smooth Lamé moduli on every bounded connected smooth domain in ℝ3, provided μ &gt; 0 and $`3\lambda+2\mu\gt 0`$ on the closure. This resolves the smooth three-dimensional isotropic elastic Calderón uniqueness problem under these positivity assumptions.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $\Omega\subset\R^3$ be any bounded connected domain with $C^\infty$ boundary. For $j=1,2$, let $\lambda_j,\mu_j\in C^\infty(\overline\Omega;\R)$ satisfy $\mu_j>0$ and $3\lambda_j+2\mu_j>0$ on $\overline\Omega$. If \[ \Lambda_{\lambda_1,\mu_1} =\Lambda_{\lambda_2,\mu_2} \colon H^{1/2}(\partial\Omega;\R^3) \longrightarrow H^{-1/2}(\partial\Omega;\R^3), \] then $\lambda_1=\lambda_2$ and $\mu_1=\mu_2$ throughout $\Omega$.
[proposition] [Transfer of physical solutions]\label{prop:physical-transfer} Let $B$ be a ball containing $\overline B_r$. Every smooth solution $u_1$ of $L_1u_1=0$ on $B$ has a smooth counterpart $u_2$ satisfying $L_2u_2=0$ on $B$ and \[ u_2=u_1\quad\text{on }B\setminus\overline\Omega. \] In particular, $u_2-u_1$ and $\diverg u_2-\diverg u_1$ are smooth and supported in $\overline\Omega$.
[proposition] [Supported Carleman estimate] \label{prop:carleman} Let $B$ be a bounded ball. For $U\in C_c^\infty(B;\C^4)$ and all sufficiently small $h>0$, \begin{equation} \label{eq:augmented-carleman} \norm{E_\tau^{-1}U}_{H_h^1} \le Ch\norm{E_\tau^{-1}\Lcal U}_{H_h^{-1}}. \end{equation}
