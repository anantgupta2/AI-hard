FAMILY 271: Bloch's law, its lattice correction, and the spherical magnetization law
SUBJECT: Mathematical physics
SUMMARY: Proves Bloch's T3/2 law with its exact coefficient for three-dimensional quantum Heisenberg ferromagnets at every positive quantum spin, allowing nonnegative symmetric finite-range couplings whose support generates ℤ3. The thermodynamic limit precedes the zero-field derivative and low-temperature limit. The family also proves spontaneous magnetization for nearest-neighbor models in every dimension d ≥ 3 and determines the first lattice correction for three-dimensional nearest-neighbor couplings.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Bloch's Law for Finite-Range Heisenberg Ferromagnets in Three Dimensions (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Bloch's T3/2 law for the spontaneous magnetization of three-dimensional quantum Heisenberg ferromagnets at every fixed positive quantum spin. The result holds for every nonnegative symmetric finite-range interaction whose support generates ℤ3, including spatially anisotropic couplings. The leading magnetization deficit has the exact coefficient determined by the determinant of the quadratic one-magnon dispersion. The thermodynamic limit is taken before the right field derivative at zero, and the low-temperature limit is taken last.
[theorem] [Bloch's law] \label{thm:main} For every fixed $S\in\{\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,\ldots\}$ and every interaction satisfying \eqref{eq:interaction}, \begin{equation} \lim_{\beta\to\infty}\beta^{3/2}\bigl(S-m_{S,J}(\beta)\bigr) =\frac{\zeta(3/2)}{8\pi^{3/2}\sqrt{\det D_{S,J}}}. \label{eq:bloch-law} \end{equation}
[proposition] [Loop representation]\label{prop:loop-representation} For every finite cube, every $\beta>0$, and every real $h$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:loop-partition} Z_\Lambda(h):=\Tr e^{-\beta(H^J_{S,N}-hM_{S,N})} =e^{\beta w_\Lambda/4}\, \mathbb E_{\rm b}\sum_\sigma e^{\beta hM(\sigma)}. \end{equation} At zero field the Gibbs spectral law of total magnetization is obtained by weighting each picture by $2^{\#\{\text{cycles}\}}$, coloring its cycles independently and fairly, and evaluating $M(\sigma)$. More precisely, for every function $\Phi$ on the finite spectrum of $M_{S,N}$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:loop-spectral-law} \frac{\Tr\bigl(e^{-\beta H^J_{S,N}}\Phi(M_{S,N})\bigr)}{Z_\Lambda(0)} =\frac{\mathbb E_{\rm b}\sum_\sigma\Phi(M(\sigma))} {\mathbb E_{\rm b}2^{\#\{\text{cycles}\}}}. \end{equation}

--- MANUSCRIPT: The spherical magnetization law for the three-dimensional quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the spherical magnetization law for the three-dimensional nearest-neighbor isotropic quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet at every fixed positive quantum spin and every sufficiently low fixed positive temperature. As the even periodic cubes grow, the symmetric zero-field magnetization converges in moments to a uniform direction with a deterministic positive magnitude. This magnitude equals the right derivative at zero field of the infinite-volume pressure. The law is expressed through self-adjoint linear combinations of the spin components and requires no joint measurement of noncommuting observables.

In addition, \begin{equation}\label{eq:second-moment-law} \lim_{\substack{L\to\infty\\L\text{ even}}} \frac1{V^2}\sum_{x,y\in\Lambda_L} \langle S_x\cdot S_y\rangle_{\beta,L}=m^2. \end{equation}
