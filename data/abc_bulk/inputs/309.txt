--- MANUSCRIPT: The Kervaire invariant problem at the prime three (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We solve the Kervaire invariant problem at the prime three for the standard Kervaire classes in the mod-three Adams spectral sequence. These classes survive precisely at indices 0, 2, and 3, and each surviving detection coset contains an element of additive order three. In particular, there is an order-three Kervaire element in stem 322.
[theorem] \label{thm:classification} At the prime three, \[ K=K_3=\{0,2,3\}. \] Thus the surviving classes occur in stems $10$, $106$, and $322$, and each admits an additive-order-three representative. The class $b_1$ and every $b_j$ with $j\geq4$ fail to survive.
[theorem] [Detection of actual sphere classes] \label{det:actual-detector} Let \(j\geq2\), and let \(\alpha\in\pi_{4\cdot3^{j+1}-2}S_{(3)}\) be detected by \(b_j\) in the mod-\(3\) Adams spectral sequence. Its image under the unit \[ \pi_{4\cdot3^{j+1}-2}S_{(3)} \longrightarrow \pi_{4\cdot3^{j+1}-2}(E^{hG}) \] is nonzero and has finite additive order. Its filtration-two symbol is the nonzero image of the Adams--Novikov representative in Lemma~\ref{det:filtration-comparison}.
[proposition] \label{det:full-gap} For every \(n\in\Z\), the group \(\pi_{-2n}(E^{hG})\) contains an element of infinite additive order. In particular, \(\pi_{-2}(E^{hG})\ne0\).
