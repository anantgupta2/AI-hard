FAMILY 182: Power savings for intersective polynomial differences and prime arguments
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
SUMMARY: For every fixed intersective integer polynomial h of degree k ≥ 2 with positive leading coefficient, proves that a subset of $`\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ avoiding nonzero values $`h(1),h(2),\ldots`$ as differences has size $`O_h(N^{1-c_k})`$, with $`c_k\gt 0`$ depending only on degree. Here intersective means having a root modulo every modulus. For prime arguments, a power saving also holds when h has a unit root modulo every modulus, with exponent allowed to depend on h.
LEAN: yes
