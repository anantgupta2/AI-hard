FAMILY 292: Kirchberg's $`\mathcal O_2`$ norm-ultrapower embedding problem
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
SUMMARY: Constructs an explicit separable unital full group C∗-algebra that cannot embed unitally into the norm ultrapower of any fixed nonzero unital nuclear C∗-algebra, for any free ultrafilter on the natural numbers. Taking the target to be $`\mathcal O_2`$ answers Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem negatively.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: An explicit obstruction to nuclear norm-ultrapower embeddings (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We give a negative answer to Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem. We construct an explicit separable unital full group C∗-algebra that admits no unital embedding into Bω, for any nonzero unital nuclear C∗-algebra B and any free ultrafilter ω on ℕ. In particular, it does not embed unitally into $`\mathcal O_2^\omega`$.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \[ G=\Z[1/2]^3\rtimes\bigl(\SL_3(\Z)\times\Z\bigr), \qquad (M,k)\cdot v=2^kMv, \] and let $A=C^*(G)$ be its full group $C^*$-algebra. Then $A$ is separable and unital. For every nonzero unital nuclear $C^*$-algebra $B$ and every free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\N$, there is no unital embedding \[ A\longrightarrow B^\omega. \] In particular, $A$ does not embed unitally into $\Otwo^\omega$.
