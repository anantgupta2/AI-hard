FAMILY 209: Integral counterexamples to Gersten’s conjecture
SUBJECT: Algebra
SUMMARY: Disproves unrestricted integral Gersten injectivity in degrees 3 and 5. Two explicit two-dimensional ramified regular local rings of mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ have nonzero integral K-theory classes that vanish over their fraction fields.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: An integral counterexample to Gersten's conjecture (September-25-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct a two-dimensional ramified regular local ring A of mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ for which $`K_5(A)\to K_5(\mathop{\mathrm{Frac}}\nolimits A)`$ has a nonzero kernel. This disproves Gersten's conjecture in its unrestricted integral form.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $V$ be the ring of integers in the unramified extension of $\Q_5$ of degree eight, and put \[ A=\bigl(V[x,y]/(5+x^4+y^4)\bigr)_{(5,x,y)}. \] Then $A$ is a Noetherian regular local domain of dimension two and \[ \ker\bigl(K_5(A)\longrightarrow K_5(\Frac A)\bigr)\ne0. \] Consequently the unrestricted integral Gersten conjecture has a negative answer.

Restriction of these equalities gives the corresponding identities in $K_5(H;5)$. In the second identity this means \[ \operatorname{res}_\iota(\delta c)\bmod5 =\chi(\delta)^2 b_\iota^2[\iota(\delta u)]_5, \] where $b_\iota$ is multiplication by $\operatorname{res}_\iota(\beta)$; no action of $\delta$ on $H$ is required.

--- MANUSCRIPT: An integral degree-three Gersten counterexample (September-26-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct a two-dimensional ramified regular local ring A in mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ for which the integral map $`K_3(A)\to K_3(\mathop{\mathrm{Frac}}\nolimits A)`$ has nonzero kernel. This gives a negative answer to the unrestricted integral Gersten conjecture.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} The ring $A$ is a two-dimensional Noetherian regular local domain of mixed characteristic $(0,5)$, with $\pi\in\mathfrak m_A^2$. There is an integral class $c\in K_3(A)$ such that \[ c|_{\Frac A}=0, \qquad c|_{A[1/\pi]}\bmod5\ne0 \quad\text{in }G_3(A[1/\pi];\Z/5). \] In particular, $K_3(A)\to K_3(\Frac A)$ is not injective.
[proposition] \label{prop:integral} There exists $b\in K_3(S)$ whose restriction to $D$, followed by coefficient reduction, is $a_D$. If $j:\Spec S\hookrightarrow\Spec A$ and $c=j_*b\in G_3(A)=K_3(A)$, then $c|_{\Frac A}=0$. Moreover, for $T=\Spec A[1/\pi]$ and $i:\Spec D\hookrightarrow T$, \[ c|_T\bmod5=i_*a_D\quad\text{in }G_3(T;\Z/5). \]
