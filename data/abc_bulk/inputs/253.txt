SUBJECT: Group theory
SUMMARY: Constructs an infinite finitely presented simple amenable group, answering the longstanding question of whether these properties can occur simultaneously.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: An infinite finitely presented simple amenable group (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct an infinite finitely presented simple amenable group, giving a positive answer to the finite-presentation existence question for simple amenable groups.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exists an infinite finitely presented simple amenable group.
[theorem] \label{thm:simplicity} For every positive integer $m$, the group $A_m$ is infinite, perfect, and simple, and \[ [F_m,F_m]=A_m. \]
[theorem] \label{thm:amenability} For every positive integer $m$, the discrete group $F_m$ is amenable. Every subgroup of $F_m$, in particular $A_m$, satisfies the finite-set F\o lner condition.
