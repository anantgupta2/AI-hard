FAMILY 093: Dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for subgaussian log-concave measures
SUBJECT: Convex and metric geometry
SUMMARY: Proves a dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for centered log-concave densities with uniformly subgaussian linear marginals, with constant bounded by a universal multiple of the squared linear subgaussian parameter.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: A dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for subgaussian log-concave measures (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every centered log-concave probability measure with a Lebesgue density on ℝn and linear subgaussian parameter a satisfies $`\mathop{\mathrm{Ent}}\nolimits _\mu(f^2)\le Ca^2\int|Df|^2\,d\mu`$ for compactly supported smooth f, with one universal constant C. This resolves positively the dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev conjecture for subgaussian log-concave measures.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is an absolute constant \(C<\infty\) with the following property. For every \(n\ge1\), every centered log-concave probability measure \(\mu\) with a Lebesgue density on \(\R^n\), and every \(a>0\) satisfying \eqref{eq:intro-subgaussian} for \(X\sim\mu\), \[ \Ent_\mu(f^2)\le C a^2\int_{\R^n}|Df|^2\,d\mu \qquad\text{for every }f\in C_c^\infty(\R^n). \] The same constant works in every dimension.
[proposition] [Limit of the regularized constants] \label{prop:regularized-constants} For the sequence in Lemma~\ref{lem:critical-normalization}, \begin{equation}\label{eq:critical-R-limit} R_s\longrightarrow1\qquad\text{for every }0<s<1/2. \end{equation} At every differentiability point of $R_s$, the fields of Lemma~\ref{lem:critical-two-alternatives} satisfy the contact identity \begin{equation}\label{eq:critical-contact-derivative} R_s'=R_s\,\frac{\E_\eta\langle w,K_sw\rangle}{I}. \end{equation} In particular $0\le R_s'\le C_J\sqrt a$ almost everywhere on each fixed compact $J\subset(0,1/2)$.
