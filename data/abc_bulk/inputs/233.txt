--- MANUSCRIPT: The joint scaling limit of critical Ashkin-Teller currents (October-6-2026)
ABSTRACT: For each fixed point on the critical Ashkin–Teller line, including the four-state Potts endpoint, we prove the conjectured joint scaling limit of the height and both current-cluster collections in every bounded Jordan domain, for every admissible polygonal approximation. The height converges to a Gaussian free field with the predicted coupling constant, and the clusters converge to canonical recursive two-valued local sets of that same field. The joint limit includes all nesting depths and the distinguished wired boundary cluster.
[theorem] [Joint critical-line limit]\label{thm:main} For every fixed pair $J>0$, $U\le J$ with $\sinh(2J)=e^{-2U}$, every bounded Jordan domain $D$, and every admissible polygonal approximation as defined in \cref{sec:lattice-domains}, \[ \bigl(H_\delta,\cC_\delta^{\rm w},\cC_\delta^{\rm f}, C_{\partial,\delta}\bigr) \ \xrightarrow[\delta\downarrow0]{\mathrm{law}}\ \left(\frac{h}{\pi\sqrt g},\cC^{\rm w},\cC^{\rm f}, \TVS{a}{a}(h)\right). \] The height converges in \(H^s_{\rm loc}(D)\) for every \(s<-1\). Each cluster collection converges in the matching topology just defined, and the distinguished component converges in Hausdorff topology. The continuum collections are locally finite above every positive diameter. All convergences are joint, and the same GFF determines both continuum collections.
[proposition] [Comparison with pins and wires] \label{prop:comparison} The finite $(s,W)$ law is positively associated in \eqref{eq:spatial-order}, as is every conditional law obtained by fixing vertex spins and compatible edge states with both endpoints fixed. Increasing additional vertex and compatible edge prescriptions, or changing retained connections according to the ordered boundary rule, increases the residual law. The analogous statement holds with the two colors reversed. These comparisons permit monotone circuit couplings: search from the changed data toward the region of interest, and stop at an open plus circuit in the lower configuration, or an open minus circuit in the upper configuration. Beyond such a common cut, the two configurations, including complementary cluster colors, can be sampled identically.
