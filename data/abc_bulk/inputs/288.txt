FAMILY 288: Kadison's similarity conjecture
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
SUMMARY: Proves Kadison's similarity conjecture: every bounded complex-linear unital algebra homomorphism from a unital complex C∗-algebra to operators on a Hilbert space becomes a $`*`$-homomorphism after conjugation by a bounded invertible operator.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Kadison's similarity theorem through uniform derivation estimates (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every bounded complex-linear unital algebra homomorphism from a unital complex C∗-algebra into the bounded operators on an arbitrary Hilbert space is similar to a $`*`$-homomorphism. This resolves Kadison's similarity conjecture positively. We also obtain one universal hyperreflexivity constant for all unital von Neumann algebras on arbitrary complex Hilbert spaces.
[theorem] [Similarity]\label{thm:similarity} Let \(A\) be any unital complex \(C^*\)-algebra, let \(H\) be any complex Hilbert space, and let \(\pi:A\to\mathcal B(H)\) be a bounded, complex-linear, unital algebra homomorphism. There exists an operator \(S\in\mathcal B(H)\), invertible with bounded inverse, such that \[ \rho(a)=S\pi(a)S^{-1}\qquad(a\in A) \] is a \(*\)-homomorphism. In particular, \(\rho(a^*)=\rho(a)^*\) for every \(a\in A\).
[theorem] [Uniform commutator estimate]\label{thm:universal} There is an absolute constant \(C<\infty\) such that, for every complex Hilbert space \(K\), every unital von Neumann algebra \(P\subset\mathcal B(K)\), every \(Y\in\mathcal B(K)\), every integer \(h\ge1\), and every \(X\in M_h(P)\), \begin{equation}\label{eq:universal} \|[Y^{(h)},X]\|\le C\,g_P(Y)\,\|X\|. \end{equation} Here \(M_h(P)\) acts naturally on \(K^h\), and \([T,U]=TU-UT\).
[theorem] [Kirchberg's similarity--derivation equivalence] \label{thm:kirchberg} For a unital \(C^*\)-algebra \(A\), the following properties are equivalent. \begin{enumerate} \item Every bounded unital homomorphism \(\pi:A\to\mathcal B(H)\), on any Hilbert space \(H\), is similar by a bounded invertible operator to a \(*\)-homomorphism. \item For every faithful unital \(*\)-representation \(\sigma:A\to\mathcal B(K)\), on any Hilbert space \(K\), every bounded linear map \(\Delta:A\to\mathcal B(K)\) satisfying \[ \Delta(ab)=\Delta(a)\sigma(b)+\sigma(a)\Delta(b) \] has the form \(\Delta(a)=V\sigma(a)-\sigma(a)V\) for some \(V\in\mathcal B(K)\). \end{enumerate}
