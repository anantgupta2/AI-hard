--- MANUSCRIPT: A uniformly discrete counterexample to bounded approximation in Lipschitz-free spaces (September-26-2026)
ABSTRACT: We construct a countable uniformly discrete metric space whose real Lipschitz-free space has the approximation property but fails the bounded approximation property, answering Kalton's question negatively. The identity can be approximated on every compact set by finite-rank operators, but no uniform bound on their norms is possible.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is a countable, unbounded, nonproper pointed metric space $(M,d,o)$ such that $d(x,y)\ge1$ for distinct points and $\F(M)$ has AP but fails $\lambda$-BAP for every finite $\lambda\ge1$.
[theorem] \label{thm:blocks} For each integer $p\ge1$, there are a bounded countably infinite pointed metric space $(M_p,d_p,o)$ and a finite set $A_p\subseteq M_p$ containing $o$ such that \[ d_p(x,y)\ge1\quad(x\ne y),\qquad \operatorname{diam}M_p\le B_p:=300p^2+150p+5, \] and every bounded finite-rank linear operator $T_0:\F(M_p)\to\F(M_p)$ with $\norm{T_0}\le p$ satisfies \[ \max_{a\in A_p}\norm{T_0\delta_a-\delta_a}\ge\frac12. \]
[proposition] \label{prop:coefficients} Let $(N,d,o)$ be a countable pointed metric space such that $d(x,y)\ge1$ for distinct points and $\operatorname{diam}N\le B<\infty$. The map \[ J:\ell_1(N\setminus\{o\})\longrightarrow\F(N), \qquad Jc=\sum_{x\ne o}c_x\delta_x, \] is a linear isomorphism onto $\F(N)$ and satisfies \begin{equation}\label{eq:coefficient-bounds} \tfrac12\norm{c}_1\le\norm{Jc}\le B\norm{c}_1. \end{equation} If $N\ne\{o\}$, then $\F(N)$ has $2B$-BAP. If $N=\{o\}$, both spaces are zero.
