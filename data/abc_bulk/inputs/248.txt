FAMILY 248: Thompson's group F is nonamenable
SUBJECT: Group theory
SUMMARY: Proves that Thompson's group F, the group of dyadic piecewise linear homeomorphisms of the interval, is nonamenable, resolving its longstanding amenability problem.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Thompson's group F is nonamenable (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that Thompson's group F is nonamenable. This confirms Geoghegan's conjecture and resolves the amenability problem for F.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Thompson's group \(F\) is not amenable.
[proposition] \label{avg:boundary} For every nonempty finite set $A\subset F$, \begin{equation}\label{avg:boundary-bound} \max_{h\in S}\frac{|hA\mathbin{\triangle}A|}{|A|} \geq \frac{\delta^2/L^2-4/D}{4(1-1/D)}>0. \end{equation}
[proposition] \label{aux:map} Let $H=L^2([0,1];\R^2)$ be a real Hilbert space and let $B=\{x\in H:\norm{x}\le1\}$. There is a globally Lipschitz map $f:B\to B$ such that \[ \norm{f(x)}=1, \qquad \norm{f(x)-x}\ge\frac12 \quad(x\in B). \]
