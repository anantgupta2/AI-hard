FAMILY 314: Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss
SUBJECT: Topology
SUMMARY: Determines the optimal chromatic loss from geometric H-fixed points to geometric G-fixed points for every subgroup H of a finite p-group G. At every nonnegative height, the loss equals the shortest subnormal-chain length from H to G with cyclic quotients. Each quotient counts once regardless of order, and finite spectra witness sharpness.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: For a finite p-group G and a subgroup H, we prove that the optimal chromatic fixed-point loss equals the shortest length of a subnormal chain from H to G with cyclic quotients. The equality holds at every prime and every nonnegative height, resolving positively the equality proposed by Kuhn and Lloyd.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every prime \(p\), finite \(p\)-group \(G\), subgroup \(H\le G\), and integer \(n\ge0\), \[ \boxed{r_n(G,H)=\ell(G,H).} \]
[proposition] [Cyclic upper bound]\label{prop:cyclic-upper} For every finite \(p\)-group \(G\), subgroup \(H\), and \(n\ge0\), \(r_n(G,H)\le\ell(G,H)\).
[proposition] [Uniform finite localization]\label{prop:finite-local} Let \(\mathcal C_m^G\) be the category of genuine \(G\)-spectra localized by the inflated sphere \(L_m^fS\). It is rigidly compactly generated. Its compact tensor-triangular spectrum is the finite subspace \[ \{(H,i):H\le G\text{ up to conjugacy},\ 0\le i\le m\} \] of the equivariant spectrum, with \((H,i)\) representing \(\mathcal P_i^G(H)\). The image of a finite genuine spectrum is compact and dualizable. An object \(Z\in\mathcal C_m^G\) is zero if and only if \[ T(i)\wedge\Phi^H Z=0 \qquad(H\le G,\ 0\le i\le m). \] For the image of a finite object, its support is tested equally by \(T(i)\) and \(K(i)\) at these heights. For every compact object of \(\mathcal C_m^G\), membership in the support at \((H,i)\) is equivalent to nonvanishing of \(K(i)\wedge\Phi^H(-)\).
