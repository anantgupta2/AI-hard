--- MANUSCRIPT: Uniform Interior $`C^{1,\alpha}`$ Estimates for Infinity-Harmonic Functions (October-4-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove a uniform interior $`C^{1,\alpha_d}`$ estimate for bounded infinity-harmonic functions in every dimension d ≥ 3. For some $`\alpha_d\in(0,1/3]`$ depending only on the dimension, the gradient's supremum norm and αd-Hölder seminorm on B1/2 are bounded by a dimension-dependent constant times the oscillation on B1. The exponent is not explicit, and no endpoint or boundary regularity claim is made. In particular, infinity-harmonic functions are locally C1 on every open subset of ℝd, for d ≥ 3.
[theorem] [Uniform interior estimate]\label{thm:main} For every integer $d\ge3$ there are constants $\alpha_d\in(0,1/3]$ and $C_d<\infty$ such that every bounded infinity-harmonic function $u\in C(B_1)$, where $B_1\subset\R^d$, belongs to $C^{1,\alpha_d}(B_{1/2})$ and satisfies \[ \|\nabla u\|_{L^\infty(B_{1/2})} +[\nabla u]_{C^{0,\alpha_d}(B_{1/2})} \le C_d\osc_{B_1}u. \]
[proposition] [Affine blow-ups at a fixed center]\label{prop:affine-blowups} Let $w$ be infinity-harmonic in $D$ and let $z\in D$. Every locally uniform limit, along a sequence $r_i\downarrow0$, of \[ V_i(Y)=\frac{w(z+r_iY)-w(z)}{r_i} \] is a linear function on $\R^n$. Such limits exist after passage to subsequences.
[proposition] [Three-point flatness]\label{prop:three-point} Let $U_i$ be infinity-harmonic on $B_1\subset\R^n$ with a common Lipschitz bound, and let $\ell_i$ be affine functions such that \[ \|U_i-\ell_i\|_{L^\infty(B_1)}\longrightarrow0. \] Then \begin{equation}\label{eq:three-point} \sup_{\substack{x,y\in B_{1/4}\\x\ne y}} \frac{|U_i(x)+U_i(y)-2U_i((x+y)/2)|}{|x-y|} \longrightarrow0. \end{equation}
