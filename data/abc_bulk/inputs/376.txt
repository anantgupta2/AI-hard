FAMILY 376: Universal computation in forced Navier–Stokes flows
SUBJECT: Partial differential equations
SUMMARY: Constructs viscous incompressible flows starting from rest on a fixed flat three-dimensional domain that perform universal computation under smooth external forcing. A terminating compiler turns a Turing machine and input into a finite program for the force, so a designated particle reaches a fixed region exactly when the machine halts. The viscosity is fixed, positive and computable.
