--- MANUSCRIPT: Continuous Phase Foliations Create Analytic Caustic Collars (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: For a smooth strictly convex planar billiard of positive curvature, a continuous foliation of a full grazing annulus by individually invariant essential curves forces an analytic boundary and a jointly analytic collar of smooth strictly convex caustics. The physical-collar rigidity theorem proved in the companion article then implies that the table is an ellipse.
[theorem] [Phase-foliation rigidity]\label{thm:main} Suppose a \(T\)-invariant open annulus \(U\subset M\) contains \((\R/L\mathbb Z)\times(0,\eta)\) for some \(\eta>0\). Suppose there is a homeomorphism \[ H:\mathbb S^1\times(0,1)\longrightarrow U \] whose leaves are essential simple closed curves, each individually invariant under \(T\). Then \(\Omega\) is an ellipse: there are \(c\in\R^2\) and a real symmetric positive-definite matrix \(Q\) such that \[ \Omega=\{x\in\R^2:(x-c)^TQ(x-c)<1\}. \] Circles are included.
[theorem] [Physical-collar rigidity, {\citep[Definition~1.1 and Theorem~1.2]{NB}}] \label{thm:nb-rigidity} A bounded strictly convex planar domain with \(C^\infty\) regular boundary of positive curvature and a continuous physical caustic collar is an ellipse.
[theorem] [Regularity of a phase-foliated collar] \label{thm:collar-regularity} Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, the boundary is real analytic. There are \(\lambda_0>0\) and a jointly real-analytic map \[ \Gamma:\mathbb T\times[0,\lambda_0]\longrightarrow\R^2 \] such that \(\Gamma(\cdot,0)\) parametrizes \(\partial\Omega\), every curve \(\Gamma(\cdot,\lambda)\) for \(0<\lambda\le\lambda_0\) is a regular strictly convex caustic, and the restriction to \(\mathbb T\times[0,\lambda_0)\) is a homeomorphism onto a relatively open collar of \(\partial\Omega\) in \(\overline\Omega\). Here real analyticity at the parameter endpoints means extension to a neighborhood.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Rigidity of Smooth Billiards with a Continuous Caustic Collar (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that a smooth strictly convex planar billiard with positive curvature is an ellipse whenever a full neighborhood of its boundary is continuously foliated by smooth closed convex caustics. No differentiability across the leaves is assumed. This resolves the near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture in the stated smooth class.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} If \(\Omega\) has a continuous caustic collar, then \(\Omega\) is an ellipse. Equivalently, there are \(c\in\R^2\) and a real symmetric positive-definite matrix \(Q\) such that \[ \Omega=\{x\in\R^2:(x-c)^TQ(x-c)<1\}. \] Circles are included.
[theorem] [Analytic regularity]\label{thm:analytic} Under the hypotheses of \Cref{thm:main}, the support function of the table and its boundary are real analytic.
[proposition] \label{prop:high-frequency-estimate} For each integer \(m\ge2\) and \(M<\infty\), take \(r_0=m+15\). There are constants \(K,N\), independent of \(0\le\sigma\le1\), such that, whenever \(\|u\|_{r_0,\sigma}\le M\) and \(|n|\ge N\), the derivative of \Cref{ana:constraint-definition} has the form \begin{equation}\label{ana:constraint-derivative} (\dd C_n)_u(v)=[b_n(u)v]_n. \end{equation} Here \begin{equation}\label{ana:coefficient-estimate} \|b_n(u)-1\|_{m,(1-K/n^2)\sigma}\le\frac{K}{|n|}, \end{equation} and, for two such inputs on the same strip, \begin{equation}\label{ana:coefficient-lipschitz} \|b_n(u)-b_n(\widetilde u)\|_{m,(1-K/n^2)\sigma} \le\frac{K}{|n|}\|u-\widetilde u\|_{r_0,\sigma}. \end{equation} The derivative identity holds for smooth real variations, and for real-symmetric holomorphic variations with the indicated finite norms.
