FAMILY 312: The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis
SUBJECT: Topology
SUMMARY: Proves the Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis for ∞-groupoids associated with every Grothendieck coherator in the Ara–Henry convention: these algebraic objects recover the homotopy theory of spaces.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis via elementary expansions (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis for every Grothendieck coherator in the Ara–Henry convention: its weak globular infinity-groupoids recover the homotopy theory of spaces. We also resolve Henry's pushout conjecture, showing that elementary expansions preserve components and all homotopy groups of cellular infinity-groupoids.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Fix any Grothendieck coherator \(\mathcal C\). Let \(X\) be a cellular \(\mathcal C\)-infinity-groupoid, let \(n\ge0\), and let \(a:D_n\to X\) be any cell. In the pushout square \[ \begin{tikzpicture}[baseline=(current bounding box.center), node distance=1.25cm and 2.2cm,>=Stealth] \node (a) {\(D_n\)}; \node (b) [right=of a] {\(D_{n+1}\)}; \node (c) [below=of a] {\(X\)}; \node (d) at (b |- c) {\(X^+\)}; \draw[->] (a) -- node[above] {\(s_n\)} (b); \draw[->] (a) -- node[left] {\(a\)} (c); \draw[->] (b) -- (d); \draw[->] (c) -- node[below] {\(i\)} (d); \end{tikzpicture} \] where \(s_n\) is the source inclusion, the map \(i:X\to X^+\) is a weak equivalence.
[proposition] [Weak-equivalence test]\label{prop:weak-test} For a model map $f:X\to Y$, the following are equivalent. \begin{enumerate} \item $f\in\mathcal W$. \item It induces a bijection on $\pi_0$ and a bijection \[ \pi_r(X;u,v)\longrightarrow\pi_r(Y;fu,fv) \] for every $r\ge1$ and every parallel pair $u,v$ in $X$. \item For every $r\ge0$, every specified boundary $b:\partial D_r\to X$, and every $r$-cell $y$ of $Y$ with boundary $fb$, there are an $r$-cell $x$ with boundary \emph{exactly} $b$ and an $(r+1)$-cell $fx\to y$ in $Y$. \end{enumerate} In particular, a weak equivalence reflects homotopy between parallel cells, including equality of components for vertices.
[proposition] [Contractibility of free shapes] \label{prop:shape-contractible} For every nonempty finite globular-sum shape $S$, the models $F(S)$ and $F_q(S)$ are contractible, for every $q\geq0$.
