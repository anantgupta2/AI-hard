FAMILY 041: Hyperkähler SYZ and projective-space bases
SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
SUMMARY: Proves the strong hyperkähler SYZ conjecture: every holomorphic line bundle with nonzero nef isotropic first Chern class on a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold is semiample. It also proves that every projective Lagrangian fibration with normal projective base has projective space as its base, in every dimension and deformation type.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Projective-space bases of Lagrangian fibrations (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the normal projective base of a projective Lagrangian fibration from a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold is projective space. This resolves the projective-space base conjecture for such fibrations in every dimension and deformation type.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(n\geq1\), and let \(X\) be a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold of dimension \(2n\), with symplectic form \(\sigma\). Suppose that \[ f:X\longrightarrow B \] is a projective surjective holomorphic morphism with connected fibers, where \(B\) is a normal projective variety of dimension \(n\). Assume that every irreducible component of every fiber has dimension \(n\) and that the restriction of \(\sigma\) to its smooth locus is zero. Then \(B\) is isomorphic to \(\PP^n_{\C}\).

[theorem] [Strong hyperkähler SYZ, companion input] \label{thm:strong-syz} Let $Y$ be a compact IHS Kähler manifold of dimension $2m$. If a holomorphic line bundle $L$ satisfies \[ c_1(L)\neq0,\qquad c_1(L)\in\overline{\mathcal K_Y},\qquad q_Y(c_1(L))=0, \] then a positive tensor power of $L$ is generated by its global sections. Its associated map with connected fibers has a normal projective base of dimension $m$. Every component of every fiber has dimension $m$ and is Lagrangian.

--- MANUSCRIPT: The strong hyperkähler SYZ conjecture (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the strong hyperkähler SYZ conjecture: every holomorphic line bundle with nonzero nef isotropic first Chern class on a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold is semiample.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $X$ be a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic K\"ahler manifold of complex dimension $2n$, where $n\ge1$. If a holomorphic line bundle $L$ satisfies \[ c_1(L)\ne0,\qquad c_1(L)\in\overline{\mathcal K_X}, \qquad q_X(c_1(L))=0, \] then $L$ is semiample. More precisely, there exist a positive integer $m$, a normal projective variety $B$, an ample line bundle $A$ on $B$, and a surjective holomorphic map with connected fibers \[ f:X\longrightarrow B,\qquad L^m\simeq f^*A, \] such that $\dim_{\C}B=n$. Every irreducible component of every fiber has complex dimension $n$, and $\sigma$ vanishes on its smooth locus.
