FAMILY 237: The three-quarter exponent for honeycomb self-avoiding walk
SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
SUMMARY: Proves the diameter form of Nienhuis's predicted three-quarter exponent: a uniformly chosen n-step self-avoiding walk on the honeycomb lattice has diameter $`n^{3/4+o(1)}`$. Its local mass and covering numbers have exponent 4/3. These estimates hold at every sufficiently large fixed length, simultaneously across scales, with arbitrarily high polynomial probability.
