--- MANUSCRIPT: The second Kahn–Kalai conjecture (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the second Kahn–Kalai conjecture. For every finite simple graph H with h ≥ 1 edges and at most n vertices, the threshold for $`G(n,p)`$ to contain an ordinary copy of H is at most $`C p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)`$, where C is universal. Here $`p_{\mathrm E}(n,H)`$ is the least density at which every subgraph of H has expected copy count at least one half.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every $n\ge2$ and every finite simple graph $H$ with $h=e(H)\ge1$ edges and at most $n$ vertices, \begin{equation}\label{eq:stronger} \pc(n,H)\le \min\{1,\;2048\ee^{50}\pE(n,H)(1+\log_2 h)\}. \end{equation} In particular, \[ \pc(n,H)\le6144\ee^{50}\pE(n,H)\log_2 n. \]
[theorem] [Tree covering]\label{thm:tree} Let $\TT$ be a disjoint probability tree on a finite set $X$, with $k\ge1$ levels. Suppose that every level is $\sigma$-spread, where $\sigma>0$, and that its positive integer capacities satisfy \[ \ell_{i+1}\ge16\ell_i\qquad(1\le i<k). \] Set \[ r=\min\{1,\;16\ee^{50}\sigma(1+\log_2\ell_k)\}. \] Then \[ \PP_{W\sim\mu_r} \bigl(\UU(P)\subseteq W\text{ for some path }P\text{ of }\TT\bigr) \ge\tfrac23. \]
