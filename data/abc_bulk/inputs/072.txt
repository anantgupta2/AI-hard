FAMILY 072: Brennan's conjecture and the integral-means spectrum
SUBJECT: Real and complex analysis
SUMMARY: Proves Brennan's conjecture: for every conformal bijection ϕ from a simply connected plane domain onto the disk, $`|\phi'|^s`$ is area-integrable for $`4/3\lt s\lt 4`$. The sharp universal integral-means identity is $`B_{\mathcal S}(t)=|t|-1`$ for t ≤ −2. A strict bound $`B_b(-1)\lt 1/4`$ for bounded univalent functions disproves Kraetzer's prediction at that parameter.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Brennan's conjecture and sharp inverse-square integral means (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Brennan's conjecture: if a simply connected plane domain admits a conformal bijection φ onto the unit disk, then $`|\varphi'|^s`$ is area-integrable for every $`4/3\lt s\lt 4`$. We also prove the sharp inverse-square integral-means exponent $`B_{\mathcal S}(-2)=1`$ for the normalized schlicht class $`\mathcal S`$.
[theorem] [Brennan's conjecture and inverse-square means] \label{thm:main} For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $C_\varepsilon<\infty$ such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:main-uniform} M_{-2}[f'](r)\le C_\varepsilon(1-r)^{-1-\varepsilon} \qquad\left(f\in\SSS,\ \frac12\le r<1\right). \end{equation} Moreover, $B_{\SSS}(-2)=1$. Let $W\subset\CC$ be any simply connected domain whose boundary in the Riemann sphere contains at least two points. Every conformal bijection $\varphi:W\to\DD$ satisfies \[ \int_W|\varphi'(z)|^s\dd A(z)<\infty \qquad\left(\frac43<s<4\right). \] Equivalently, every univalent map $f:\DD\to\CC$ satisfies \[ \int_{\DD}|f'(w)|^t\dd A(w)<\infty \qquad\left(-2<t<\frac23\right). \]
[proposition] [Uniform disk comparison] \label{prop:disk-transfer} If $\rho\le2$, then for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $C_\varepsilon<\infty$ such that \begin{equation} M_{-2}[f'](r)\le C_\varepsilon(1-r)^{-1-\varepsilon} \quad\bigl(f\in\SSS,\ 1/2\le r<1\bigr). \label{prelim:uniform-disk} \end{equation} The constant depends only on $\varepsilon$.
[proposition] [Compact positive superlevels] \label{prop:proper-superlevels} For $P$-almost every $F\in\KK$, for planar area-almost every $\xi\in\CC$, the set \[ \{z\in\HH:V_\xi^F(z)\ge\delta\} \] is compact in $\HH$ for every $\delta>0$.

--- MANUSCRIPT: A strict inverse-first-power bound for univalent functions (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove a uniform upper bound for inverse-first-power integral means of normalized univalent disk maps with exponent strictly below 1/4. Consequently, the bounded universal integral-means spectrum satisfies $`B_b(-1)\lt 1/4`$, disproving Kraetzer's conjectured spectrum at p = −1.
[theorem] [Uniform inverse-first-power bound]\label{thm:main} There exist constants \(0<\varepsilon<1/4\) and \(C<\infty\) such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:uniform} M_{-1}[f'](r)\le C(1-r)^{-1/4+\varepsilon} \qquad\text{for every }f\in\SSS\text{ and every }1/2\le r<1. \end{equation} Consequently \(B_b(-1)<1/4\), and Kraetzer's conjectured identity \eqref{eq:kraetzer} is false.
[proposition] [From dyadic samples to disk means]\label{prop:disk} There is a universal constant \(C_0\) such that, for \(f\in\SSS\), \[ M_{-1}[f'](r)\le C_0\|L^n\| \quad\text{if}\quad 1/2\le r<1,\quad v=2^{-n}\le1-r<2v. \] Consequently \(B_b(-1)\le\beta\).
[proposition] [Exact-parameter affine law]\label{prop:law} Assume \(\beta=\log_2\rho\ge1/4\), where \(\rho\) is defined in \eqref{eq:rho}. There is a Borel probability \(P\) on \(\KK\) such that, writing \(\E\) for expectation under \(P\), \begin{equation}\label{eq:law} \E\bigl[|Q_F(x+iy)|\,\Phi(T_{x+iy}F)\bigr] =y^{-\beta}\E\Phi(F) \end{equation} for every \(x+iy\in\HH\) and every nonnegative Borel test \(\Phi:\KK\to[0,\infty]\).
