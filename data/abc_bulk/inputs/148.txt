SUMMARY: For every self-similar measure on the line generated by finitely many contracting similarities, proves $`\dim_{\mathrm H}\mu=\min\{1,h_{\mathrm{RW}}/\chi\}`$, where $`h_{\mathrm{RW}}`$ is the entropy rate of random composed maps and χ the average logarithmic contraction. --- MANUSCRIPT: The entropy-rate dimension formula for self-similar measures on the line (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the Hausdorff dimension of every finite real self-similar measure equals the minimum of one and its random-walk entropy rate divided by its Lyapunov exponent. Exact overlaps are allowed, and the contraction ratios may be unequal and negative. This resolves the entropy-rate dimension conjecture.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every finite nonempty indexed family $\varphi_i(x)=r_i x+t_i$ on $\R$ with $0<|r_i|<1$, and every strictly positive probability vector $p$, its self-similar measure satisfies \[ \dim_H\mu_{\Phi,p} =\min\left\{1,\frac{h_{\mathrm{RW}}(\Phi,p)}{\chi(\Phi,p)}\right\}. \] No separation assumption is required; exact overlaps and repeated generators are allowed.
[proposition] [Pair mass in finite scale bands]\label{prop:pair-bands} Under the assumptions and notation of Lemma~\ref{lem:type-deficit}, there exist $c>0$ and an integer $n_0$ such that, for each $n\ge n_0$, there is a deterministic finite integer $B_n\ge a_n$ satisfying \begin{equation}\label{eq:types-pair-bands} \E\!\left[\mathbf1_{E_n} \sum_{\ell=a_n}^{B_n}c_\ell(\nu_{\omega,n})\right]\ge cn. \end{equation} Here $c_\ell(\nu)$ is the maximum total weight of fair two-point laws with separations in $[2^{-\ell},2^{1-\ell})$ whose weighted sum is a submeasure of $\nu$, as in Lemma~\ref{lem:pair-bound}. The constants $b,A,A',c,n_0$ are independent of $n$ and $\omega$; no growth bound on $B_n$ is asserted.
