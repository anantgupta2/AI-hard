FAMILY 006: Goldfeld’s conjecture: densities and mean analytic rank
SUBJECT: Number theory
SUMMARY: Proves Goldfeld's conjecture for quadratic twists of every elliptic curve over ℚ: analytic ranks zero and one each have density 1/2, and the mean analytic rank tends to 1/2. Both statements order signed squarefree twist parameters by absolute value.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: Goldfeld's analytic density conjecture and the 2-converse for elliptic curves (October-7-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Goldfeld's analytic density conjecture: for every elliptic curve E over ℚ, the quadratic twists of E with analytic rank zero and one each have density 1/2 among signed squarefree twist parameters ordered by absolute value. We also prove the low-corank 2-converse: if the $`2^\infty`$-Selmer corank of E is zero or one, then it equals the analytic and Mordell–Weil ranks, and the Tate–Shafarevich group is finite.
[theorem] [The $2$-converse]\label{thm:main-converse} For every elliptic curve $E/\Q$ with $c_2(E)\in\{0,1\}$, \[ a(E)=r(E)=c_2(E),\qquad \Sha(E/\Q)\text{ is finite}. \]
[theorem] [Goldfeld's analytic density conjecture]\label{thm:main-density} For every elliptic curve $E/\Q$ and each $j\in\{0,1\}$, \[ \lim_{X\to\infty} \frac{\#\{d\in\mathcal D(X):a(E^{(d)})=j\}}{\#\mathcal D(X)} =\frac12. \] Consequently the twists of analytic rank at least two have density zero. For a density-one set of signed squarefree parameters $d$, the analytic and Mordell--Weil ranks of $E^{(d)}$ agree and its whole Tate--Shafarevich group is finite.

--- MANUSCRIPT: The mean analytic rank of quadratic twists of elliptic curves (October-6-2026)
ABSTRACT: For every elliptic curve over ℚ, we prove that the average analytic rank of its quadratic twists tends to 1/2 when signed squarefree twist parameters are ordered by absolute value. This resolves Goldfeld's mean analytic-rank conjecture in this counting convention.
[theorem] \label{thm:mean} For every elliptic curve \(E/\Q\), \[ \lim_{Y\to\infty}\frac{1}{\#\mathcal D(Y)} \sum_{d\in\mathcal D(Y)}a(E^{(d)})=\frac12. \]
[theorem] \label{thm:tail} For every elliptic curve \(E/\Q\), there are constants \(C_E>0\) and an integer \(R_E\ge1\) such that, for every integer \(R\ge R_E\), \[ \limsup_{Y\to\infty}\frac1Y \sum_{\substack{d\in\mathcal D(Y)\\a(E^{(d)})>R}}a(E^{(d)}) \le \frac{C_E}{R}. \]
[proposition] [Logarithmic second moments]\label{analytic:moment} There are fixed positive integers $A,B$ such that, for every dyadic $M\geq1$, every $F\in\mathcal F$, every $t\in\mathbb R$, and $1/2\leq\sigma\leq2$, \begin{equation}\label{analytic:L-moment} \frac1M\sum_{\substack{d\ \mathrm{admissible}\\M\leq|d|<2M}} |L_{F^{(d)}}(\sigma+it)|^2 \ll_{\mathcal F}\log^B(3M)(1+|t|)^A. \end{equation} For arbitrary signed squarefree twisting parameters and $H\geq1$, \begin{equation}\label{analytic:prefix-moment} \sum_{\substack{0<|h|\leq H\\h\ \mathrm{squarefree}}} |L_{F^{(h)}}(\sigma+it)|^2 \ll_{\mathcal F}H\log^B(3H)(1+|t|)^A. \end{equation}
