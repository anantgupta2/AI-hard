--- MANUSCRIPT: Exact Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer Formula from Low Selmer Corank (October-7-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over ℚ whose full q-power Selmer group has corank zero or one at some prime q. The analytic and Mordell–Weil ranks equal that corank, and the Tate–Shafarevich group is finite. The formula includes all prime factors and requires no additional hypotheses on reduction, rational torsion, isogenies, complex multiplication, or residual Galois representations.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $E/\Q$ be an elliptic curve and let $q$ be any prime. If $s_q(E)\in\{0,1\}$, then \[ r(E)=a(E)=s_q(E),\qquad \#\Sha(E/\Q)<\infty, \] and, with $r=r(E)$ and the preceding normalizations, \begin{equation}\label{eq:bsd-main} \frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)}{r!} =\frac{\Omega_E\Reg_E\,\#\Sha(E/\Q) \prod_{\ell\text{ finite}}c_\ell(E)} {\#E(\Q)_{\rm tors}^{2}}. \end{equation} There are no additional hypotheses on reduction, rational torsion, isogenies, complex multiplication, or residual Galois representations.
[theorem] [Unrestricted low-corank converse]\label{classical:low-corank} For every elliptic curve $A/\Q$, every prime $q$, and $u\in\{0,1\}$, the equality $s_q(A)=u$ implies \[ a(A)=r(A)=u,\qquad \#\Sha(A/\Q)<\infty. \]
[theorem] [Two-primary leading term]\label{classical:two-primary} For every elliptic curve $A/\Q$ with $s_2(A)\leq1$, the quotient $Q_A$ of \eqref{eq:normalized-quotient}, with the total real period and the full-lattice regulator of \eqref{eq:height-convention}, is a positive rational number, and \[ v_2(Q_A)=v_2(\#\Sha(A/\Q)). \]

--- MANUSCRIPT: The Selmer converse for elliptic curves at every prime (October-7-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the Selmer converse in coranks zero and one for every elliptic curve over ℚ and every prime p: if the full p-power Selmer group has ℤp-corank $`r\in\{0,1\}`$, then the analytic and Mordell–Weil ranks both equal r, and the entire Tate–Shafarevich group is finite. As an application at the additive prime 3, we prove that for every prime $`\ell\equiv4,7,8\pmod9`$, the cubic $`X^3+Y^3=\ell Z^3`$ has analytic and Mordell–Weil rank one and finite Tate–Shafarevich group. In particular, every such ℓ is a sum of two rational cubes.
[theorem] [The unrestricted low-corank Selmer converse] \label{thm:main} For every elliptic curve $A_0/\Q$, every prime $p$, and $r\in\{0,1\}$, \[ s_p(A_0)=r \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a(A_0)=\rank_{\Z}A_0(\Q)=r, \qquad \#\Sha(A_0/\Q)<\infty. \] There is no restriction on reduction type, complex multiplication, the residual representation, rational torsion, or rational isogenies.
[theorem] [Established converse at $2$] \label{thm:two-converse-input} If $E/\Q$ is any elliptic curve and $\corank_{\Z_2}\Sel_{2^\infty}(E/\Q)=r\in\{0,1\}$, then \[ \ord_{s=1}L(E,s)=\rank E(\Q)=r, \qquad \Sha(E/\Q)\text{ is finite}. \]
[theorem] [Established analytic twist-density theorem] \label{thm:twist-density-input} For any elliptic curve $E/\Q$, among nonzero squarefree integers $d$ ordered by $|d|$, the quadratic twists $E^{(d)}$ of analytic rank zero and one each have density $1/2$. In particular the exceptional set of twists of analytic rank at least two has density zero.

--- MANUSCRIPT: The two-primary Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer formula in Selmer corank at most one (October-6-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the two-primary Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over the rationals whose two-power Selmer group has corank at most one. In this range, the algebraic rank, analytic rank, and Selmer corank are equal, and the Tate–Shafarevich group is finite. Combined with the quadratic-twist Selmer distribution, this gives the exact two-primary formula for a density-one set of signed squarefree twists of each fixed curve, ordered by absolute value; the common rank is zero or one, with each value having density one half.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(E/\mathbb Q\) be any elliptic curve with \(s_2(E)\leq1\). Then \[ r:=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbb Q) =\operatorname{ord}_{s=1}L(E,s)=s_2(E), \qquad \#\Sha(E/\mathbb Q)<\infty. \] Moreover, \[ Q_E=\frac{L^{(r)}(E,1)\,(\#T_E)^2} {r!\,\Omega_E\Reg_E\prod_{\ell\ \mathrm{finite}}c_\ell(E)} \in\mathbb Q_{>0}, \qquad v_2(Q_E)=v_2\bigl(\#\Sha(E/\mathbb Q)\bigr). \] There is no restriction on \(E[2]\) or on the reduction of \(E\) at two.
[proposition] [Positive comparison]\label{prop:positive} Let \(A/\mathbb Q\) be non-CM. If \(\an(A)\leq1\), then \(X(A)\geq0\). Suppose a positive fundamental discriminant \(a\) prime to \(2N_A\), or \(a=1\), satisfies \(\an(A^a)\leq1\) and \(X(A^a)=0\). Then \(s_2(A)\leq1\) implies \(\an(A)=s_2(A)\) and \(X(A)=0\).
[proposition] [Split-pair anchor]\label{prop:anchor} Let \(A/\mathbb Q\) be non-CM. There exist a fundamental discriminant \(h\) prime to \(2N_A\), allowing \(h=1\), and an imaginary quadratic field of discriminant \(k\) prime to \(2hN_A\), in which all primes dividing \(2hN_A\) split, such that \[ \an(A^h)+\an(A^{hk})=1, \qquad X(A^h)+X(A^{hk})=0. \]
