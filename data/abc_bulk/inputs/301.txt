FAMILY 301: Trace cones and Razak–Jacelon stabilization
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
SUMMARY: Classifies separable nuclear complex C∗-algebras after tensoring with the Razak–Jacelon algebra and the compact operators, using their full topological cones of extended lower-semicontinuous tracial weights. This answers Robert’s trace-cone question, including algebras with arbitrary ideal structure and both finite and infinite subquotients.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations (September-25-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the canonical topological cone of all extended lower-semicontinuous tracial weights determines a separable nuclear C∗-algebra after tensoring with the Razak–Jacelon algebra and the compact operators, answering Robert's trace-cone classification question positively. The isomorphism realizes the prescribed cone map, with arbitrary ideal structure and without a density assumption on the finite domains of the weights.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(A\) and \(B\) be separable nuclear complex \(C^*\)-algebras. If \(T(A)\) and \(T(B)\) are isomorphic as topological cones, then \[ A\otimes\W\otimes\K\ \cong\ B\otimes\W\otimes\K, \] where \(\K=\K(\ell^2(\N))\) and all tensor products are spatial.
[theorem] [Trace realification]\label{st:realification} For every \(C^*\)-algebra \(C\), the trace cone with topology \eqref{intro:trace-topology} is compact Hausdorff, and it is second countable when \(C\) is separable. If \(C\) is exact, the assignment \(\tau\mapsto d_\tau\) identifies this cone with the topological cone of functionals on \(\Cu(C)\). For every \(C^*\)-algebra \(C\), \[ \Cu(C\otimes\W)\cong\Cu(C)_{\R}. \] Realification is determined functorially by the topological functional cone. [proposition] [Transport of ideals]\label{st:ideals} The maps \(F,G\) identify the complete ideal lattices of \(P\) and \(Q\), and these identifications agree. They identify the finite and zero ideals of corresponding weights. The resulting homeomorphism of primitive ideal spaces will be used to regard \(\Prim(P)=\Prim(Q)=X\). For an open \(U\subseteq X\), write \(P(U)\) and \(Q(U)\) for the corresponding ideals. Then \[ G(\Cu(P(U)))=\Cu(Q(U)), \] and \[ \Fin(\tau)=Q(U)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Fin(F\tau)=P(U), \qquad \Ker(\tau)=Q(U)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Ker(F\tau)=P(U). \]
