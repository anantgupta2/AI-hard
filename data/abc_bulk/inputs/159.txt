FAMILY 159: Erdős’s reciprocal-sum conjecture and quasipolynomial Szemerédi bounds
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
SUMMARY: Proves Erdős's conjecture that every set of positive integers with divergent reciprocal sum contains arithmetic progressions of every finite length. Quantitatively, for each fixed k ≥ 3, every subset of $`\{1,\ldots,N\}`$ with no nonconstant k-term progression has size at most $`C_kN\exp[-c_k(\log N)^{\varepsilon_k}]`$, with positive constants depending only on k.
LEAN: yes
