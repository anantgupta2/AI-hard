FAMILY 214: The Benjamini–Schramm nonuniqueness conjecture
SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
SUMMARY: Proves $`p_c\lt p_u`$ for Bernoulli bond percolation on every infinite connected locally finite nonamenable quasi-transitive graph, resolving the Benjamini–Schramm nonuniqueness conjecture. Thus there is a nonempty range of probabilities with infinitely many infinite clusters. A stronger operator bound also establishes the critical triangle condition.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Nonuniqueness of percolation on nonamenable quasi-transitive graphs (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the bond-percolation nonuniqueness conjecture of Benjamini and Schramm: every infinite connected, locally finite, nonamenable quasi-transitive graph has a nonempty interval of parameters for which Bernoulli bond percolation almost surely has infinitely many infinite clusters. We also prove Hutchcroft's stronger operator-threshold conjecture, establishing $`p_c\lt p_{2\to2}\le p_u`$.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $G$ be an infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with $h_V(G)>0$. For Bernoulli bond percolation on $G$, \[ \sup_{0\le p<\pc}\norm{T_p}_{2\to2} =\norm{T_{\pc}}_{2\to2}<\infty, \qquad \pc(G)<\ptwo(G)\le\pu(G). \] There exist $\pc<p_1<p_2<1$ such that, in the coupling obtained from independent uniform edge labels, almost surely there are infinitely many infinite open clusters simultaneously for every $p\in[p_1,p_2]$.
[proposition] [Reduction to a unimodular action] \label{pre:unimodular-reduction} If $\Aut(G)$ is nonunimodular, then $\pc<\ptwo\le\pu$ and $T_\pc$ is bounded. For the proof of the operator gap it therefore remains to treat the case in which $\Gamma=\Aut(G)$ is unimodular.
[theorem] [Critical boundedness for a unimodular action] \label{thm:critical-operator} Let $G$ be an infinite connected locally finite simple quasi-transitive graph with $h_V(G)>0$. If $\Aut(G)$ is unimodular, then $\norm{T_{\pc}}_{2\to2}<\infty$.
