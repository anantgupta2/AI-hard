--- MANUSCRIPT: The equivariant cohomological Hikita conjecture for arbitrary quivers (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the equivariant cohomological Hikita conjecture for arbitrary finite quivers, including loops and multiple arrows. For any dimension and framing vectors, any commuting flavor torus, and a stability character whose semistable locus is stable and has free gauge action, the equivariant cohomology of the Nakajima variety is canonically isomorphic to the coordinate ring of the scheme-theoretic fixed locus of the stability cocharacter on the flavor-deformed Coulomb branch. This is an isomorphism of graded algebras over the common coefficient ring, retaining nilpotents and including the empty case.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every datum \eqref{eq:gauge-data}, every commuting flavor torus $F$, and every regular stability character $\nu$, both coefficient maps are surjective and have the same kernel. They therefore induce a canonical isomorphism of graded $R$-algebras \[ H_F^*(X;\C)\ \cong\ \C[Y_{\mathfrak f}^{\nu}]. \] The gradings are ordinary cohomological degree and BFN homological degree, respectively. The assertion includes the empty case, whose ring is zero.
