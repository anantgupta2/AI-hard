FAMILY 241: Rigidity of the Turing degrees
SUBJECT: Mathematical logic
SUMMARY: Every order automorphism of the Turing degrees is the identity, resolving their rigidity problem. Thus no nontrivial relabeling of degrees preserves the ordering by relative computability.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Rigidity of the Turing degrees (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every order automorphism of the full partial order of Turing degrees is the identity, resolving the rigidity conjecture for the Turing degrees positively.
[theorem] \label{thm:rigidity} Every order automorphism of the full Turing-degree structure is the identity: \[ \forall\pi\in\Aut(\DT,\leT)\ \forall\mathbf a\in\DT \qquad \pi(\mathbf a)=\mathbf a. \]
[theorem] [Slaman--Woodin]\label{thm:representation} For every $\pi\in\Aut(\DT,\leT)$ there is an arithmetic function $\widehat F:2^\N\to2^\N$ such that \[ \degT{\widehat F(A)}=\pi(\degT A) \qquad\text{for every }A\subseteq\N. \]
[proposition] [Recovery from four values]\label{prop:recovery} Let $F:\R\to2^\N$ be Borel with countable fibers, and suppose \[ F(x+y)\leT F(x)\oplus F(y)\qquad(x,y\in\R). \] For every irrational $t\in(0,1)$ there is a comeager set $G_t\subseteq\R^2$ such that for each $(u,v)\in G_t$ there is a rational $\delta\in(0,1)$ for which \begin{equation}\label{eq:four-values} C(t)\leT F(u)\oplus F(u-v)\oplus F(v-u+\delta t)\oplus F(v-u+\delta(t-1)). \end{equation}
