FAMILY 290: Relative bicentralizers and modular spectral recovery
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
SUMMARY: Proves Connes' bicentralizer conjecture for every type III1 factor with separable predual and every faithful normal state. More generally, for every inclusion $`N\subset M`$ of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals admitting a faithful normal conditional expectation, constructs an amenable expected subalgebra $`P\subset N`$ with $`P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)`$, resolving the relative bicentralizer conjecture.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Expected amenable subalgebras preserving core commutants (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that every inclusion $`N\subset M`$ of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals and a faithful normal conditional expectation contains an expected amenable subalgebra $`P\subset N`$ such that $`P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)`$, where $`c(M)`$ denotes the continuous core of M. This resolves the relative bicentralizer conjecture in this setting.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $N\subset M$ be a unital inclusion of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals, and suppose that there is a faithful normal conditional expectation $E_N:M\to N$. There exist a unital amenable von Neumann subalgebra $P\subset N$ and a faithful normal conditional expectation $F:N\to P$ such that \[ P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M). \] Here amenable means injective.
[proposition] [Binormal states and spectral shifts]\label{prop:inputs} In this setting the following hold. \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)] \item If a state $\Phi$ on $\mathbf B(\HH)$ satisfies \[ \Phi\circ\lambda=\Phi\circ\rho=\varphi,\qquad \Phi(e_N)=1,\qquad \Phi(f(X_M))=f(0)\quad(f\in C_0(\R)), \] then, for $a,b\in B$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:binormal} \Phi(\lambda(a)\rho(b))=\langle a\Omega b,\Omega\rangle. \end{equation} \item For each $s\in\R$ there is a unital completely positive map $\theta_s:\mathbf B(\HH)\to\mathbf B(\HH)$ such that \begin{align} \theta_s(e_N)&=e_N,& \theta_s\circ\rho&=\rho,\label{eq:shift1}\\ \theta_s\circ\lambda&=\lambda\circ\gamma_s\circ E_B,& \theta_s(f(X_M))&=f(X_M-s)\quad(f\in C_0(\R)).\label{eq:shift2} \end{align} These maps need not be normal. \end{enumerate}
[theorem] \label{thm:absolute} For every type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor $N$ with separable predual and every faithful normal state $\varphi$ on $N$, one has $\B(N,\varphi)=\C1$.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Bounded recovery for modular spectral averages (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove a bounded spectral recovery theorem for a von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal state whose centralizer consists only of scalars. A positive averaged squared norm for an arbitrary bounded Hilbert-space operator on shrinking modular spectral bands can be recovered on uniformly bounded algebra elements with shrinking spectral support. We apply this theorem to spectral intertwining rigidity and obtain an alternative proof of bicentralizer triviality for type III1 factors with separable predual.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $M$ be a type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor with separable predual and let $\phi$ be any faithful normal state on $M$. Then \[ \BC(M,\phi)=\C1. \]
[theorem] [Spectral intertwining rigidity]\label{thm:rigidity} Let $M$ be a von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal state $\phi$ such that $M_\phi=\C1$. Let $b:\R\curvearrowright M$ be a continuous $\phi$-preserving action. Suppose that, for every $s\in\R$, every uniformly bounded sequence $(w_n)$ in $M$, and every $a\in M$, \begin{equation}\label{eq:flow-property} \norm{\phi w_n-e^s w_n\phi}_{M_*}\longrightarrow0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad w_na-b_s(a)w_n\longrightarrow0 \quad\text{strongly}. \end{equation} Then $b_s=\id_M$ for every $s\in\R$.
[proposition] [Established bicentralizer results]\label{bc:inputs} Let $M$ be a type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor with separable predual and $\phi$ a faithful normal state. \begin{enumerate} \item\label{bc:reduction} If $B=\BC(M,\phi)$ is nonscalar, then $B$ is a type $\mathrm{III}_1$ subfactor with a normal $\phi$-preserving conditional expectation, and \[ \BC(B,\phi|_B)=B. \] \item\label{bc:flow} There is a continuous $\phi$-preserving action $\beta^\phi:\R_+^*\curvearrowright\BC(M,\phi)$ characterized by \[ \norm{w_n\phi-\lambda\phi w_n}_{M_*}\longrightarrow0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad w_na-\beta^\phi_\lambda(a)w_n\longrightarrow0 \quad *\text{-strongly} \] for every $\lambda>0$, every uniformly bounded sequence $(w_n)$ in $M$, and every $a\in\BC(M,\phi)$. \item\label{bc:ergodicity} The fixed-point algebra of $\beta^\phi$ is $\C1$. \end{enumerate}
