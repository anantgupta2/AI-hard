FAMILY 256: Nonsingular systems of equations over arbitrary groups
SUBJECT: Group theory
SUMMARY: Proves that every finite system of equations over an arbitrary group whose exponent-sum matrix has full row rank over ℚ has a simultaneous solution in an overgroup, resolving Howie's conjecture. The coefficient group embeds in the presented quotient. A companion proves Kervaire's conjecture: adjoining one generator and one relation cannot trivialize a nontrivial group.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Nonsingular systems of equations over arbitrary groups (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: Every finite nonsingular system of equations over an arbitrary group has a simultaneous solution in an overgroup. This proves Howie's conjecture on nonsingular systems.
[theorem] [Nonsingular systems]\label{thm:main} Let $G$ be any group, let $1\le m\le n$ be integers, and let $w_1,\ldots,w_m\in G*F_n$. If their exponent-sum matrix has row rank $m$ over $\mathbb Q$, then the canonical homomorphism \[ G\longrightarrow (G*F_n)/\langle\!\langle w_1,\ldots,w_m\rangle\!\rangle \] is injective. Equivalently, the system has a simultaneous solution in an overgroup of $G$.

Let $\Sigma$ be a connected oriented surface of genus zero obtained from a nonempty finite collection of signed word disks for these words by attaching bands that pair every slot exactly once, always with the same variable index and opposite traversal signs.
