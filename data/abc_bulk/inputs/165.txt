FAMILY 165: The Harary–Hill and Zarankiewicz crossing-number formulas
SUBJECT: Combinatorics
SUMMARY: Resolves the Harary–Hill conjecture and Turán's brickyard problem in the Zarankiewicz formulation, determining the crossing numbers of every complete and complete bipartite graph. The result proves the optimality of the classical drawings among all plane drawings with continuous edge arcs.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: The crossing number of complete graphs (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the Harary–Hill conjecture: for every positive integer n, the ordinary crossing number of the complete graph Kn is
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every integer \(n\ge3\), \[ \crn(K_n)=Z(n):= \frac14\left\lfloor\frac n2\right\rfloor \left\lfloor\frac{n-1}2\right\rfloor \left\lfloor\frac{n-2}2\right\rfloor \left\lfloor\frac{n-3}2\right\rfloor. \]
[proposition] [Odd lower bound]\label{prop:odd} Every normalized minimum drawing of \(K_{2s+1}\), \(s\ge2\), has at least \(\binom{s}{2}^{2}\) crossings.
[proposition] \label{prop:upper} This drawing has exactly \(Z(n)\) crossings for every \(n\ge3\).

--- MANUSCRIPT: The crossing number of complete bipartite graphs (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the Zarankiewicz crossing-number conjecture, resolving Turán's brickyard problem. For all positive integers m, n, the ordinary crossing number of the complete bipartite graph $`K_{m,n}`$ is
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For all positive integers $m,n$, \[ \crn(K_{m,n})=d_m d_n. \]
