--- MANUSCRIPT: Spectral scalar curvature and Urysohn width in dimension three (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: Every connected complete smooth Riemannian three-manifold without boundary satisfying $`-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge\lambda\gt 0`$ as a quadratic-form inequality admits a continuous map to a graph whose entire fibers have diameter at most $`500/\sqrt\lambda`$ in the original metric. No orientability, spin, compactness, or bounded-geometry assumption is required.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $(M^3,g)$ be a connected complete smooth Riemannian three-manifold without boundary. Suppose that $\lambda>0$ and \begin{equation}\label{eq:main-spectral} \int_M\bigl(4|\nabla_g\phi|_g^2+\Scal_g\phi^2\bigr)\,d\vol_g \ge \lambda\int_M\phi^2\,d\vol_g \qquad\bigl(\phi\in C_c^\infty(M;\R)\bigr). \end{equation} Then there exist a simplicial complex $K$ of dimension at most one and a continuous map $f:M\to K$ such that \[ \diam_g f^{-1}(y)\le \frac{500}{\sqrt\lambda} \qquad(y\in K). \] The complex has its usual polyhedral topology. No orientability, spin, compactness, or bounded-geometry assumption is imposed on $M$.
[proposition] [Closed stable surfaces]\label{prop:stable-surface} Let $\Sigma$ be a smooth closed connected two-sided surface in a Riemannian three-manifold $(X,h)$. Let $T,\mu$ be smooth ambient functions. Suppose that $\Sigma$ satisfies $H-\nu T=\mu$, is stable for weighted area with pressure $\mu$, and satisfies \eqref{eq:pressure-curvature-condition} for a constant $c>0$. Then, in the induced metric $k=h|_\Sigma$, \[ \diam_k\Sigma\le\frac{2\pi}{\sqrt c}. \] Moreover, $\Sigma$ is diffeomorphic to $S^2$ or $\R P^2$.
[proposition] \label{prop:separating-cut} There is a locally finite family $\mathcal S$ of pairwise disjoint closed connected embedded two-sided stable weighted minimal surfaces in $M$ such that the following statements hold. Let $N$ be the manifold obtained by cutting $M$ along $\mathcal S$, and let $q:N\to M$ be the natural gluing map. \begin{enumerate} \item The map $q$ is proper and is a quotient map. Each connected component $P$ of $N$, with the intrinsic length metric of the pulled-back metric $g$, is proper. \item Each boundary component of $P$ is a sphere or a real projective plane, is stable weighted minimal, and has intrinsic $g$-diameter at most $2\pi$. --- MANUSCRIPT: Positive scalar curvature and uniform codimension-two width (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the quantitative continuous form of Gromov's scalar-curvature conjecture in every dimension n ≥ 4. Every complete connected smooth boundaryless n-manifold with scalar curvature at least one admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most $`n-2`$ whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only in terms of n. We also obtain the continuous macroscopic-dimension conclusion for universal covers of closed manifolds with positive scalar curvature in every dimension n ≥ 2.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every integer \(n\ge4\), there is a finite constant \(C_n\) such that every connected complete smooth Riemannian \(n\)-manifold \((M,g)\), without boundary and with \(\Scal_g\ge1\), admits a continuous map \(f:M\to K\) to a simplicial complex of dimension at most \(n-2\) satisfying \[ \diam_g f^{-1}(y)\le C_n\qquad(y\in K). \] In particular, \(\UW_{n-2}(M,g)\le C_n\).
[theorem] [Pressure law]\label{pressure:law} For every $A_0,P_0,\eps,b_0>0$ there exist $0<S_0\le1$, $T\ge1$, and a smooth function \[ F:(-S_0,S_0)\times[T,\infty)\longrightarrow\R \] which is odd in $x$, has $F_x>0$, and satisfies \begin{equation}\label{pressure:estimates} \begin{gathered} F_x\ge A_0t,\qquad |F_t|\le\eps|F|,\qquad FF_t\le\eps F_x,\\ F_x\le b_0t^2\quad\text{or}\quad -FF_t\ge P_0F_x,\\ F_x\le b_0t^2+\eps F^2. \end{gathered} \end{equation} For each fixed $t\ge T$, \[ \lim_{x\to-S_0}F(x,t)=-\infty, \qquad \lim_{x\to S_0}F(x,t)=+\infty. \] These limits are uniform when $t$ ranges over a compact subinterval of $[T,\infty)$. The choices depend only on $A_0,P_0,\eps,b_0$ and fixed numerical constants. In particular, they are independent of any manifold, metric, local cutoff, or subsequent deformation parameter. The lower bound $T$ may be increased after the construction.
[theorem] [Local stretching]\label{step:main} Fix $n\ge2$ and $0<\delta<1/10$, and put $L_*=1.004$. There are constants $W,T\ge1$, depending only on $n,\delta$, with the following property. Let $h_0$ be a complete smooth metric on a manifold $M^n$ without boundary, let $h\ge h_0$ be smooth, and let $t\ge T$ be smooth. Let $A$ be a nonempty compact set and $B$ a nonempty closed set such that \[ \dist_{h_0}(A,B)\ge W. \] For every $0<d<\delta$ there are smooth $h'\ge h$ and $t'\ge t$ such that the changes have compact support in $\{\dist_{h_0}(A,\cdot)<W\}$, \begin{equation}\label{step:conclusion} \mathcal D(h',t')\ge \mathcal D(h,t)+(t'^2-t^2) -\delta\bigl(\tr_h h_0-\tr_{h'}h_0\bigr)t'^2-dt'^2, \end{equation} and \begin{equation}\label{step:distance} \dist_{h'}(A,B)\ge L_*W. \end{equation} In particular, $h'$ is complete. Neither $W$ nor $T$ depends on $d$, the geometry of $M$, or the sets $A,B$.
