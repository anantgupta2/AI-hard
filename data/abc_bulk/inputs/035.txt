FAMILY 035: Log-canonical threefold abundance in numerical dimension one
SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
SUMMARY: Proves log abundance for projective log canonical threefold pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p &gt; 3 when the effective boundary is rational and the ℚ-Cartier adjoint is nef of numerical dimension one. The adjoint is semiample, without requiring the original variety to be terminal or ℚ-factorial.
LEAN: no

--- MANUSCRIPT: Log abundance in numerical dimension one for threefolds in positive characteristic (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of log abundance for threefolds over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p &gt; 3. If $`(X,B)`$ is a projective log canonical threefold pair with effective rational boundary, and $`K_X+B`$ is ℚ-Cartier, nef, and of numerical dimension one, then $`K_X+B`$ is semiample. Neither terminality nor ℚ-factoriality of X is required.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>3$. Let $(X,B)$ be a projective log canonical threefold pair over $k$, where $X$ is normal, $B$ is an effective $\Q$-divisor, and $K_X+B$ is $\Q$-Cartier. If $K_X+B$ is nef and has numerical dimension one, then $K_X+B$ is semiample.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Abundance in numerical dimension one for terminal threefolds in positive characteristic (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: Let X be a projective ℚ-factorial terminal threefold over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p &gt; 3. We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of abundance: if KX is nef with $`\nu(K_X)=1`$, then KX is semiample and $`\kappa(X,K_X)=1`$.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $X$ be a normal projective $\Q$-factorial threefold over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p>3$. Suppose that $X$ has terminal singularities and that $K_X$ is nef with $\nu(K_X)=1$. Then some positive Cartier multiple of $K_X$ has at least two independent global sections. In particular, $\kappa(X,K_X)=1$ and $K_X$ is semiample.
