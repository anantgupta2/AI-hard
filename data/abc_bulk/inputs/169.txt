--- MANUSCRIPT: Elementary positivity of chromatic quasisymmetric functions (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the chromatic quasisymmetric function of every natural unit interval graph is elementary-positive over $`\mathbb N[q]`$. This resolves the elementary-positivity part of the Shareshian–Wachs conjecture.
[theorem] [Elementary positivity and a permutation witness] \label{main:theorem} There is a deterministic terminating procedure which, given a natural unit interval graph $G$ on $[n]$ and $\sigma\in D_G^0$, returns a partition $\theta_G(\sigma)\vdash n$ such that \begin{equation} \chi_G(X;q)=\sum_{\sigma\in D_G^0} q^{\ginv_G(\sigma)}e_{\theta_G(\sigma)}(X). \label{main:witness} \end{equation} In particular every elementary coefficient of $\chi_G(X;q)$ belongs to $\N[q]$. The assertion holds as a formal symmetric-function identity, uniformly in $n$ and $G$.
[proposition] [Commuting independent-set sums] \label{graph:commutation} For every natural unit interval graph $\Gamma$ and all $j,k\ge0$, $E_j(\Gamma)E_k(\Gamma)=E_k(\Gamma)E_j(\Gamma)$.
[proposition] [Coloring kernel] \label{graph:kernel} If $\alpha(\Gamma)\le D$, then $X_G(X;q)$ is symmetric and \begin{equation} X_G(X;v^2)=\sum_{\substack{\lambda\vdash n\\\ell(\lambda)\le D}} v^M[X_u]m_\lambda(Y)\,e_\lambda(X). \label{graph:coefficient} \end{equation} The equality is independent of the extra vertices of $\Gamma$.
