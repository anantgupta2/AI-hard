--- MANUSCRIPT: Existential–universal real sentences in the counting hierarchy (October-4-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove that the existential theory of the reals lies in the counting hierarchy. More generally, we show that the truth of existential–universal sentences over the reals can be decided in a fixed level of the counting hierarchy, even when the integer polynomials are given by arithmetic circuits.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There is an absolute integer $j$ such that \[ \EATR\in\Clevel j. \] The level $j$ is independent of the input length, the numbers of variables, the degrees of the circuit polynomials, and their coefficient magnitudes.
[proposition] [Closure of controlled families]\label{prop:controlled-closure} Controlled families are preserved by each of the following operations, applied a fixed number of times: \begin{enumerate} \item sums and products over short index sets, with uniform bounds on the summands or factors; \item powers with short nonnegative exponents computable in polynomial time, and substitution of controlled polynomials for indeterminates; \item extraction of indexed coefficients in any subset of the indeterminates, and differentiation in one variable to a short indexed nonnegative order. \end{enumerate} In the first operation the index domain is specified in polynomial time; more generally, an available predicate $A(w,i)$ may restrict the indices when polynomial-time uniform bounds for the unrestricted domain are retained. [proposition] [Sign from residues]\label{prop:crt-sign} Let $X(w)\in\Z$ and suppose that nonnegative short integers $m(w)$ and $\lambda(w)$ are computable in polynomial time, with $|X(w)|\le2^{m(w)}$. Define \begin{equation}\label{eq:crt-bounds} Z=\max(16,m,\lambda),\qquad Y=2Z^4. \end{equation} If $X(w)\bmod p$, represented in $\{0,\ldots,p-1\}$, is uniformly computable in $\Flevel{a}$ for primes $\lambda<p\le Y$, then $\sgn X(w)$ is in $\Flevel{\max(a,5)+3}$.
