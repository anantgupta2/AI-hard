FAMILY 196: A counterexample to Kaplansky’s zero-divisor conjecture
SUBJECT: Algebra
SUMMARY: Constructs a finitely presented torsion-free group G whose group algebra $`\mathbb F_2[G]`$ has nonzero zero divisors, disproving Kaplansky's zero-divisor conjecture. The group has a finite two-dimensional classifying space.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: A Torsion-Free Group Algebra with Zero Divisors (September-23-2026)
ABSTRACT: We disprove Kaplansky's zero-divisor conjecture by constructing a finitely presented torsion-free group G for which $`\mathbb F_2[G]`$ has nonzero zero divisors. The group admits a finite two-dimensional classifying space.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} There exist a finitely presented torsion-free group \(G\) and nonzero elements \(\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F_2[G]\) such that \(\alpha\beta=0\). Moreover, \(G\) admits a finite two-dimensional classifying space.
[proposition] [Bounded-pattern estimate] \label{prop:bounded-pattern} There is a constant \(\varepsilon>0\), depending only on the fixed types and turn weights, with the following property. For every fixed positive integer \(K\), fixed real \(C\ge1\), and fixed nonnegative integer \(I\), the conditional probability tends to zero that there exists a system as above having at most \(K\) paths, total length \(H\) with \[ L\le H\le CL, \] at most \(I\) interval pairs, and \(b\) unpaired occurrences, where \(b\le\varepsilon H\). In particular, \(\varepsilon\) is chosen before \(K,C,I\), and these three bounds are held fixed as \(n\) tends to infinity through its admissible values.
[proposition] [Absence of reduced spherical arrangements] \label{prop:no-arrangements} With probability $1-o(1)$, $\Gamma$ admits no reduced spherical arrangement. In particular, for all sufficiently large admissible $n$ there exist graphs of the prescribed types, with girth at least $L$ and the diameter bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:diameter}, admitting no such arrangement.
