FAMILY 267: Positive-temperature Bose–Einstein condensation and exact quantum depletion
SUBJECT: Mathematical physics
SUMMARY: Proves Bose–Einstein condensation for the exact canonical Gibbs state of the three-dimensional hard-sphere gas: each fixed exclusion distance and sufficiently small fixed density admit a strictly positive temperature, independent of volume, with positive condensate fraction in the thermodynamic limit. At zero temperature, proves the Bogoliubov leading quantum-depletion law for hard spheres and fixed bounded nonnegative radial finite-range potentials of positive scattering length, taking the thermodynamic limit before the dilute limit.
LEAN: yes

--- MANUSCRIPT: Bose–Einstein condensation at positive temperature in the dilute hard-sphere gas (October-5-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Bose–Einstein condensation at positive temperature in the three-dimensional dilute hard-sphere gas. For each fixed exclusion distance and every sufficiently small fixed density, there is a strictly positive temperature, independent of the volume, at which the exact canonical Gibbs state has a positive condensate fraction in the thermodynamic limit. The condensate occupies the constant orbital.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every $a>0$, there is $\rho_*(a)>0$ such that for each fixed $0<\rho<\rho_*(a)$ there is a fixed temperature $T=T(a,\rho)>0$ for which \[ \liminf_{\substack{L\to\infty\\N/L^3\to\rho}} \frac{\langle u_{0,L},\gamma^{(1)}_{N,L,T}u_{0,L}\rangle}{N}>0. \] The exclusion distance, density, and temperature are held fixed in this limit.
[theorem] [Scaled occupation bound]\label{thm:scaled} There are constants $\alpha_0\ge2$, $D_*<\infty$, and $c_*>0$ such that $B_{\rm occ}\ge c_*$ whenever the parameters satisfy \eqref{eq:scaled-parameters}, $D\ge D_*$, and $K$ is sufficiently large. The lower threshold for $K$ can be chosen locally uniformly for $D$ in $[D_*,\infty)$, uniformly in $\alpha\in[\alpha_0/2,2\alpha_0]$ and $0<T\le D^{-1}$.
[proposition] [Particle-number free-energy bounds]\label{prop:increments} For sufficiently large $D$, uniformly in $K\ge100$, $0<T\le D^{-1}$, and the fixed $\alpha$ window, \begin{align} F_N&\ge c\alpha N,\label{eq:free-energy-lower}\\ F_{N+k}&\ge F_N+c\alpha k \qquad(0\le k\le N), \label{eq:free-energy-increase}\\ F_j^{(-W)}-F_{j-1}^{(-W)}&\le C\alpha \qquad(1\le j\le2N).\label{eq:free-energy-insertion} \end{align} The last estimate holds for every bounded measurable $W\ge0$, with a constant independent of $W$. All form domains through particle number $2N$ are nonempty. The constants $c,C$ can be numerical; only the large-$D$ threshold depends on the fixed window for $\alpha$.

[proposition] [Removal energies]\label{set:removal} For all sufficiently large $D$, uniformly in \eqref{set:scales}, \begin{equation}\label{set:removal-bound} E_N-E_{N-m}\le C\alpha m\qquad(0\le m\le N). \end{equation} At fixed $K,r$, the ratio $E_n/n$ is nondecreasing among positive particle numbers having nonempty form domain.
[proposition] [Joint low-count bound]\label{set:low-counts} One can fix $\alpha_0$ sufficiently large and then $\ell_*>0$ sufficiently small so that every normalized nonnegative bosonic ground function satisfies, for every $A\subset\mathcal L$, \begin{equation}\label{set:low-count-bound} \mu\{n_{B_v}<\ell_*D\text{ for every }v\in A\} \le (C/D^2)^{|A|}. \end{equation} The estimate holds for all sufficiently large $D$, uniformly in $K$ and the ground function.

--- MANUSCRIPT: A density-uniform condensate bound for dilute Bose gases (September-27-2026)
ABSTRACT: For each fixed bounded measurable, nonnegative, radial interaction of finite range in three dimensions that is not zero almost everywhere, we prove a positive lower bound on the constant-orbital condensate fraction that is uniform over all sufficiently small densities and all temperatures between zero and the square of the density. The interaction and density remain fixed in the thermodynamic limit.
[theorem] \label{thm:main} For every potential $v$ as above there are constants $\rho_*(v)>0$ and $c_*(v)>0$ with the following property. For every $0<\rho<\rho_*(v)$ there is $L_0(\rho,v)<\infty$ such that, for every $L\ge L_0(\rho,v)$, every integer $N\ge1$ satisfying $\rho/2\le N/L^3\le2\rho$, and every $0\le T\le\rho^2$, \[ \frac{\langle u_0,\gamma^{(1)}_{N,L,T}u_0\rangle}{N} \ge c_*(v). \] Consequently, for each such fixed density $\rho$ and fixed $0\le T\le\rho^2$, every sequence with $L\to\infty$ and $N/L^3\to\rho$ has lower limiting condensate fraction at least $c_*(v)$.

--- MANUSCRIPT: Ground-state condensation in the dilute hard-sphere gas (September-24-2026)
ABSTRACT: We prove Bose–Einstein condensation in every ground state of a dilute three-dimensional hard-sphere Bose gas. At every sufficiently small fixed gas parameter, the constant orbital contains a positive fraction of the particles in the thermodynamic limit. The fraction can be chosen independently of the gas parameter, and the conclusion holds for arbitrary complex ground states.
[theorem] [Condensation in every ground state]\label{main:theorem} There are absolute constants $\varepsilon_0,c_0>0$ with the following property. Fix $a,\rho>0$ satisfying $\rho a^3<\varepsilon_0$. For every sequence of particle numbers $N_k$ and lengths $L_k$ such that \[ N_k\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad L_k\longrightarrow\infty, \qquad \frac{N_k}{L_k^3}\longrightarrow\rho, \] and every choice of normalized, possibly complex, bosonic ground eigenvectors $\Psi_k$ of the hard-sphere Hamiltonians, \begin{equation}\label{main:limit} \liminf_{k\to\infty} B(\Psi_k)\ge c_0. \end{equation}
[proposition] [Joint low-count bound]\label{sp:low-counts} One can fix $\alpha_0$ sufficiently large and then $\ell>0$ sufficiently small so that, for every $A\subset\mathcal L$ and all sufficiently large $D$, \begin{equation}\label{sp:low-count-bound} \mu\{n_v<\ell D\text{ for every }v\in A\} \le (C/D^2)^{|A|}. \end{equation} These choices and the constant $C$ are independent of $K$ and of the ground function.
[proposition] [Joint spatial bounds]\label{sp:joint-tails} For every $A\subset\mathcal L$, \begin{equation}\label{sp:count-mgf} \log\int e^{zf_A}\dd\mu\le CzD|A|\qquad(0\le z\le1), \end{equation} where $f_\varnothing=0$. Fix any $\theta>0$. One can choose $b>0$ sufficiently small depending on $\theta$, then take $D$ sufficiently large, so that the random site set \begin{equation}\label{sp:close-bad-set} \mathcal Q(X)=\left\{v\in\mathcal L: \#\{i:d(x_i,v)\le5,\ d_i\le s_0\}\ge\theta D\right\} \end{equation} satisfies \begin{equation}\label{sp:close-tail} \mu\{A\subset\mathcal Q\}\le p_D^{|A|},\qquad p_D=\left(C_{\theta}b^{-2}D^{-4/3}\right)^{1/Q}\longrightarrow0. \end{equation} Here $Q$ is an absolute integer; $D$ is taken large enough that the displayed base is at most one. All statements are uniform for $\alpha\in[\alpha_0/2,2\alpha_0]$, $K\ge100$, and the ground function.
