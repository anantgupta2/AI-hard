FAMILY 035: Log-canonical threefold abundance in numerical dimension one
SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
OPENAI SUMMARY: Proves log abundance for projective log canonical threefold pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p &gt; 3 when the effective boundary is rational and the ℚ-Cartier adjoint is nef of numerical dimension one. The adjoint is semiample, without requiring the original variety to be terminal or ℚ-factorial.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Log abundance in numerical dimension one for threefolds in positive characteristic (October-5-2026) [preprints/Log-abundance-in-numerical-dimension-one-for-threefolds-in-positive-characteristic-October-5-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of log abundance for threefolds over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p &gt; 3. If $`(X,B)`$ is a projective log canonical threefold pair with effective rational boundary, and $`K_X+B`$ is ℚ-Cartier, nef, and of numerical dimension one, then $`K_X+B`$ is semiample. Neither terminality nor ℚ-factoriality of X is required.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} The abundance conjecture predicts that a nef log canonical divisor $K_X+B$ on a projective log canonical pair is semiample: some positive Cartier multiple is generated by its global sections. Semiampleness turns the numerical positivity of the divisor into a morphism. We consider threefolds in positive characteristic and the case of numerical dimension one. For a nef $\Q$-Cartier divisor $L$ on a threefold, this condition means that, for an ample Cartier divisor $H$, \[ LH^2>0\qquad\text{and}\qquad L^2H=0. \] [statement] The boundary coefficients are arbitrary rational numbers allowed by log canonicity, and neither terminality nor $\Q$-factoriality is assumed for the original variety. The theorem proves the numerical-dimension-one case of threefold log abundance in this characteristic range. Over the complex numbers, Miyaoka proved abundance for minimal threefolds of numerical dimension one \cite{Miyaoka}; Kawamata completed abundance for minimal threefolds and gave another proof of this case \cite[Section~4]{Kawamata}. Keel, Matsuki, and McKernan established log abundance for log canonical threefold pairs \cite{KMM,KMMCorrection}. In positive characteristic, Keel's semiampleness criterion \cite{Keel} and the basepoint-freeness methods of Cascini, Tanaka, and Xu \cite{CTX} provided foundations. The threefold minimal model program developed through the work of Hacon--Xu, Birkar, and Birkar--Waldron in characteristic greater than five \cite{HX,Birkar,BW}, the log canonical and relative extensions of Waldron and Hashizume--Nakamura--Tanaka \cite{Waldron,HNT}, and Hacon--Witaszek's treatment of characteristic five \cite{HW}. For abundance, Xu--Zhang proved nonvanishing for terminal minimal threefolds in characteristic greater than five \cite{XZ}. Witaszek obtained klt nonvanishing and abundance in nef dimension at most two in that characteristic range \cite{Witaszek}. The nef dimension $n(L)$ is the dimension of the nef reduction of $L$. On a threefold over an uncountable algebraically closed field, $n(L)=3$ means that no curve of $L$-degree zero passes through a very general point. Zheng Xu established nonvanishing for pseudoeffective log canonical divisors on projective lc threefold pairs in characteristic greater than three, together with abundance in nef dimension at most two \cite[Theorems~1.4 and~1.8]{XuNonvanishing}; he subsequently proved abundance in numerical dimension two \cite[Theorem~1.1]{XuNuTwo}. For the present problem, the remaining case has numerical dimension one and nef dimension three. The companion paper \cite{Companion} treats terminal threefolds with nef canonical divisor of numerical dimension one. Its proof isolates a stronger structural result: certain connected nef Cartier divisors on smooth threefolds cannot exist. We state this cycle obstruction in Theorem~\ref{thm:obstruction}, with all its hypotheses. The present paper constructs such a divisor from a log canonical pair with arbitrary rational boundary. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>3$. Let $(X,B)$ be a projective log canonical threefold pair over $k$, where $X$ is normal, $B$ is an effective $\Q$-divisor, and $K_X+B$ is $\Q$-Cartier. If $K_X+B$ is nef and has numerical dimension one, then $K_X+B$ is semiample.

ABSTRACT: Let X be a projective ℚ-factorial terminal threefold over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p &gt; 3. We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of abundance: if KX is nef with $`\nu(K_X)=1`$, then KX is semiample and $`\kappa(X,K_X)=1`$.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} The abundance conjecture predicts that a nef canonical divisor on a projective variety with mild singularities is semiample: some positive Cartier multiple is generated by its global sections. Such sections turn numerical information about the canonical divisor into a morphism that records the canonical geometry of the variety. For a nef divisor $L$ on a threefold, numerical dimension one means $L\cdot H^2>0$ and $L^2\cdot H=0$ for an ample Cartier divisor $H$. We prove this case of abundance for terminal threefolds in characteristic greater than three. [statement] No assumption is made on the first Betti number, irregularity, or Albanese variety. Thus Theorem~\ref{thm:main} resolves the numerical-dimension-one case of the abundance conjecture under the stated terminal and characteristic hypotheses. Geometrically, the theorem gives a morphism $f:X\to C$ with connected fibers to a normal projective curve and an ample Cartier divisor $A$ on $C$ such that $mK_X\sim f^*A$ for some $m>0$. Indeed, the image of a morphism defined by a globally generated pluricanonical multiple is a curve, since $\nu(K_X)=1$. Its Stein factorization gives $f$; the finite pullback to $C$ of a hyperplane divisor on the image gives $A$. \subsection{Context} Over the complex numbers, Miyaoka proved the numerical-dimension-one case of threefold abundance by studying effective pluricanonical divisors, primitive connected cycles, and their infinitesimal neighborhoods \cite{miyaoka1988}. Kawamata's abundance theorem for minimal threefolds includes another proof of this case using the log minimal model program and formal thickenings \cite[Section~4]{kawamata1992}. The present argument develops this canonical-cycle framework in positive characteristic. More recently, Liu--Xu developed higher-dimensional numerical-dimension-one abundance over $\mathbb C$ under nonvanishing hypotheses \cite{liuxu2025}. The ground field in the present theorem requires the positive-characteristic inputs described next. Birkar proved flips for $\Q$-factorial dlt threefold pairs and log minimal models for projective klt threefold pairs with pseudoeffective log canonical divisor \cite[Theorems~1.1 and~1.2]{birkar2016}. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $X$ be a normal projective $\Q$-factorial threefold over an algebraically closed field $k$ of characteristic $p>3$. Suppose that $X$ has terminal singularities and that $K_X$ is nef with $\nu(K_X)=1$. Then some positive Cartier multiple of $K_X$ has at least two independent global sections. In particular, $\kappa(X,K_X)=1$ and $K_X$ is semiample.
