FAMILY 352: A finite-time singularity of Calabi flow
SUBJECT: Differential geometry
OPENAI SUMMARY: Disproves Chen's smooth long-time existence conjecture for Calabi flow by constructing a smooth $`U(10)`$-invariant Kähler metric on $`\mathbb{CP}^{10}`$ whose flow develops a finite-time singularity. The metric lies in the Fubini–Study class, so the failure occurs even in a class containing a constant-scalar-curvature metric.

=== MANUSCRIPT: A finite-time singularity of Calabi flow on projective space (September-24-2026) [preprints/A-finite-time-singularity-of-Calabi-flow-on-projective-space-September-24-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We construct a smooth Kähler metric in the Fubini–Study class of $`\mathbb{CP}^{10}`$ whose Calabi flow develops unbounded scalar curvature in finite time. This disproves Chen's smooth long-time existence conjecture, even in a class containing a constant-scalar-curvature metric.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} Let \(\omega_0\) be a smooth K\"ahler form on a compact complex manifold \(M\). We use the scalar-curvature convention \(S(\omega)=\operatorname{tr}_\omega\operatorname{Ric}(\omega)\). The Calabi flow is \begin{equation}\label{eq:calabi-flow} \omega(t)=\omega_0+i\partial\bar\partial\phi(t)>0, \qquad \phi(0)=0,\qquad \partial_t\phi=S(\omega(t))-\bar S, \end{equation} where \(\bar S\) is the average scalar curvature, determined by the K\"ahler class. Calabi introduced this flow to seek canonical metrics within a K\"ahler class~\cite{Calabi1982}. Chen's smooth long-time existence conjecture asserts that the flow exists for all positive times from arbitrary smooth K\"ahler initial data; see \cite[Conjecture~1.1]{HeZeng2021}. We give a counterexample on projective space. Write \(\omega_{\mathrm{FS}}\) for the Fubini--Study form whose potential on the standard affine chart is \(\log(1+|Z|^2)\). [statement] The Fubini--Study metric has constant scalar curvature. Thus the example also answers negatively the smooth global-existence question for classes containing a constant-scalar-curvature K\"ahler metric, emphasized by Chen and Cheng~\cite[p.~912, following Conjecture~1.3]{ChenCheng2021}. In particular, no assertion of smooth global existence and subsequent smooth convergence modulo holomorphic automorphisms can hold for arbitrary smooth initial metrics in such a class: the obstruction already occurs at a finite time. The same singularity persists on products with a stationary factor. [statement] The corollary is proved after Theorem~\ref{thm:main} in Section~\ref{sec:compact}. \subsection{Background and significance} On compact Riemann surfaces, Chru\'sciel proved global forward existence and convergence to a constant-curvature metric~\cite[Theorem~2.1]{Chrusciel1991}. Chen gave a different proof using decreasing functionals and concentration compactness~\cite{Chen2001}, and Struwe developed a unified treatment of Calabi and normalized Ricci flows on closed surfaces~\cite{Struwe2002}. In higher dimensions, Chen and He proved local existence and uniqueness and established stability near a constant-scalar-curvature metric~\cite[Theorems~1.2 and~3.2]{ChenHe2008}. He and Zeng developed short-time theory for rougher initial metrics~\cite{HeZeng2021}. These higher-dimensional results concern either short times or restricted initial data. Curvature estimates give continuation criteria for the smooth flow. Chen and He proved extension under a uniform Ricci-curvature bound~\cite[Corollary~5.3]{ChenHe2008}, and Chen and Cheng established extension under a uniform scalar-curvature bound~\cite[Theorem~1.8]{ChenCheng2021}. Li, Zhang and Zheng state that a uniform spatial $L^p$ scalar-curvature bound, measured with respect to the evolving metric, suffices when $p>\dim_{\C}M$~\cite[Theorem~1.2]{LiZhangZheng2024}; Li and Zhang state a further criterion involving spacetime scalar and scalar-Laplacian integrability~\cite[Theorem~1.1]{LiZhang2025}. On a compact almost homogeneous manifold with two hypersurface ends, Guan proved exponential smooth convergence of the modified Calabi flow for initial metrics invariant under a maximal compact subgroup of the automorphism group~\cite[Theorem~A]{Guan2007}. The two special orbits of the action used here are instead a point and the hyperplane at infinity. The point has complex codimension ten, so the two-hypersurface hypothesis fails. Streets constructed global weak Calabi flows by minimizing movements~\cite{Streets2014}. Berman, Darvas and Lu identify these flows with trajectories in a space of finite-energy K\"ahler potentials and show that, when the class contains a constant-scalar-curvature metric, every weak trajectory converges to a smooth constant-scalar-curvature potential in the $d_1$ distance, the extension of the $L^1$ path-length distance on smooth K\"ahler potentials~\cite[Section~6 and Theorem~1.11]{BermanDarvasLu2017}. [theorem] \label{thm:main} There is a smooth \(U(10)\)-invariant K\"ahler form \(\omega_0\) on \(\CP^{10}\), in the class \([\omega_{\mathrm{FS}}]\), whose maximal smooth Calabi flow has existence interval \([0,T_*)\) with \(0<T_*<\infty\). There are a point \(p\in\CP^{10}\) and a constant \(a>0\) such that \[ S(\omega(t))(p)\sim a(T_*-t)^{-1/2}\qquad\text{as }t\uparrow T_*. \]
[corollary] [Products with stationary factors]\label{cor:product-singularities} Let \(\omega(t)\), \(T_*\), \(p\), and \(a\) be as in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, and let \((Y,\eta)\) be a compact connected K\"ahler manifold with \(\eta\) of constant scalar curvature. With \(\pi_X\) and \(\pi_Y\) the projections from \(\CP^{10}\times Y\), the maximal smooth Calabi flow from \(\Omega_0=\pi_X^*\omega_0+\pi_Y^*\eta\) is \[ \Omega(t)=\pi_X^*\omega(t)+\pi_Y^*\eta, \qquad 0\leq t<T_*. \] For every \(y\in Y\), \(S(\Omega(t))(p,y)\sim a(T_*-t)^{-1/2}\). In particular, finite-time smooth Calabi-flow singularities occur in every complex dimension \(n\geq10\), on \(\CP^{10}\times\CP^{n-10}\), in a class containing a K\"ahler--Einstein metric.
