[theorem] \label{thm:main} For some sufficiently large even integer $N$, the manifold $Q=S^9\times S^{N-1}$ admits a closed exact smoothly embedded Lagrangian $L\subset T^*Q$ such that $L$ is diffeomorphic to $Q$ and no compactly supported Hamiltonian isotopy carries the zero section to $L$.
[theorem] [Waldhausen's tube fibration]\label{thm:tube-fibration} For the stable smooth tube space \eqref{eq:stable-tubes} there is a homotopy fiber sequence \begin{equation}\label{eq:tube-fibration} \Hs(*)\longrightarrow\T\xrightarrow{\gamma}BG. \end{equation} The rigid tube map $\iota:BO\to\T$, defined by nondegenerate quadratic forms, satisfies $\gamma\circ\iota\simeq BJ$, where $BJ:BO\to BG$ is the stable spherical fibration map.
[proposition] \label{prop:kernel-class} There exists \begin{equation}\label{eq:kernel-class} 0\ne a\in\pi_9\T,\qquad \gamma_*a=0. \end{equation} Moreover, $(BJ)_*:\pi_9BO\to\pi_9BG$ is injective.
[proposition] \label{prop:one-critical} Let $B=S^9$ and let $p\geq20$. Suppose that $f_b:\R^{2p}\to\R$, $b\in B$, is a smooth family satisfying the following conditions: \begin{enumerate} \item it is homogeneous at infinity, with smooth homogeneous data in $T_{p,p}$; \item each $f_b$ has exactly one critical point, which is nondegenerate and varies smoothly with $b$; \item the stable spherical class of the family at infinity is zero in $[B,BG]$. \end{enumerate} Then its stable tube class is zero in $[B,\T]$.
[proposition] \label{prop:hcob-injectivity} Let $k,l\geq20$, and put \[ Y=S^{k+l-1},\qquad \Sigma=\{q_{k,l}=0\}\cap Y\cong S^{k-1}\times S^{l-1}. \] For a closed collar embedding $e:\Sigma\times J\hookrightarrow Y$, the composite \begin{equation}\label{eq:hcob-injectivity-map} H(\Sigma)\xrightarrow{\sigma}H(\Sigma\times J) \xrightarrow{e_!}H(Y)\longrightarrow\Hs(Y) \end{equation} induces an isomorphism on $\pi_9$ in the product component. Consequently, if a sphere family in $H(\Sigma)$ has null image under this composite, the family is itself null.
[proposition] \label{prop:embedded-realization} On $Q=B\times S^{N-1}$ there is a smooth Morse family $F:Q\times\R^N\to\R$ which equals $g_b(w)$ outside a uniform vertical ball and whose generating map is a diffeomorphism from its critical locus onto a closed exact embedded Lagrangian $L\subset T^*Q$. Its critical locus, and hence $L$, is diffeomorphic to $Q$. The infinity class on each slice $B\times\{v_0\}$ is the chosen class $a$.
[proposition] [One transport step with controlled infinity data] \label{prop:transport-step} Suppose $F_o:U\times\R^r\to\R$ is a smooth Morse family, with $\Gamma_{F_o}$ a diffeomorphism onto its generated Lagrangian $\mathcal L_o\subset T^*U$. Suppose that it is smooth up to $\overline U$, with uniform bounds on compact vertical sets, and equals a homogeneous function $g_o(x,w)$ for $\norm{w}\ge R_o$, where $g_o$ is smooth for $w\ne0$ and \begin{equation} \norm{\nabla_wg_o(x,w)}\ge c_o\norm{w}, \qquad \norm{d_xg_o(x,w)}\le C_o\norm{w}^2, \qquad c_o>0. \label{eq:transport-old-bounds} \end{equation} For a small step $\psi$ as in Lemma~\ref{lem:transport-mixed-generator}, there is a smooth Morse family on $U\times\R^{r+2d}$ generating $\psi(\mathcal L_o)$ with the same properties. Its critical locus corresponds diffeomorphically to $\Crit(F_o)$. On every fixed compact parameter slice in $U$, its homogeneous data represent the split quadratic stabilization of the preceding tube class.
