FAMILY 062: Projective contact classification and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture
SUBJECT: Algebraic and complex geometry
OPENAI SUMMARY: Proves the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture: every closed connected positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension at least eight is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space. It also proves contact-Fano homogeneity and classifies smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds of complex dimension at least three: those with $`b_2=1`$ are adjoint varieties with their canonical contact structures, while those with $`b_2\ge2`$ have underlying manifold $`\mathbb P(T^*Z)`$ for a smooth projective variety Z.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Contact Fano manifolds and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture (September-23-2026) [preprints/Contact-Fano-manifolds-and-the-LeBrun-Salamon-conjecture-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We resolve the contact-Fano homogeneity conjecture and the Riemannian LeBrun–Salamon conjecture positively. Every smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of complex dimension at least three, with its given contact distribution, is contact-isomorphic to the adjoint variety of a simple complex Lie algebra. Consequently, every closed connected smooth positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension $`4m\geq8`$ is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} Let $X$ be a smooth connected complex projective variety of dimension $2n+1$, where $n\geq1$. A \emph{contact structure} on $X$ is an exact sequence of algebraic vector bundles \begin{equation}\label{intro:contact-sequence} 0\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow T_X \xrightarrow{\ \theta\ }L\longrightarrow0, \end{equation} with $L$ a line bundle, such that the Levi pairing \[ F\otimes F\longrightarrow L, \qquad (v,w)\longmapsto\theta([v,w]) \] is everywhere nondegenerate. In a local frame of $L$, write $\alpha$ for the scalar one-form representing $\theta$. The condition is equivalent to $\alpha\wedge(d\alpha)^n$ being nowhere zero. We call $(X,F)$ a \emph{contact Fano manifold} if $-K_X$ is ample. For a simple complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, let $\mathcal O_{\min}(\mathfrak g)$ be its nonzero nilpotent adjoint orbit of smallest dimension, and put \[ Y_{\mathfrak g}=\PP(\mathcal O_{\min}(\mathfrak g)). \] This is the unique closed adjoint orbit in $\PP(\mathfrak g)$. Its canonical contact structure is obtained by projectivizing the Kirillov--Kostant--Souriau symplectic cone. [statement] Throughout the paper, $\PP(E)$ parametrizes lines in $E$, with tautological subbundle $\OO_{\PP(E)}(-1)$. The two models separated by the Picard reduction are \[ \begin{array}{c|c|c} X&L&\mathfrak g\\ \hline \PP^{2n+1}&\OO(2)&\mathfrak{sp}_{2n+2}(\CC)\\[2pt] \PP(T^*\PP^{n+1})&\OO(1,1)|_X&\mathfrak{sl}_{n+2}(\CC). \end{array} \] In the second row, $X$ is the incidence divisor of points and hyperplanes in $\PP^{n+1}\times(\PP^{n+1})^*$. In the first, the adjoint embedding is the quadratic Veronese embedding, so its hyperplane bundle is $\OO(2)$. The proof then treats the remaining case $\Pic(X)=\ZZ[L]$. Two consequences will be proved after the classification in Section~\ref{hom:section}. [statement] For projective contact manifolds without a Fano hypothesis, the results of Demailly and Kebekus--Peternell--Sommese--Wi\'sniewski provide a reduction to which Theorem~\ref{thm:main} applies. [statement] The Riemannian application concerns a smooth manifold of real dimension $4m$, $m\geq2$, whose metric has holonomy contained in $\Sp(m)\Sp(1)$ in its standard representation. Such a metric is called \emph{quaternionic-K\"ahler}; \emph{positive} here means that its scalar curvature is positive. The compact symmetric positive quaternionic-K\"ahler spaces are the \emph{Wolf spaces}. [statement] Theorem~\ref{thm:main} resolves the smooth contact-Fano homogeneity conjecture positively. Corollary~\ref{thm:quaternionic} gives the positive resolution of the Riemannian LeBrun--Salamon conjecture in every real dimension $4m$ with $m\geq2$. The link is the positive twistor construction: the twistor space is contact Fano, so the general algebraic theorem applies to it. Simple connectivity of the contact Fano twistor space and its $S^2$-bundle structure show that the original manifold is simply connected. The metric is recovered from the resulting complex twistor space in Section~\ref{tw:section}. \subsection*{History and prior work} The conjectural classification has two related settings. Wolf's work relates compact quaternionic symmetric spaces to homogeneous complex contact spaces~\cite{Wolf1965}; the adjoint-variety description of their twistor fibration is recalled in~\cite[Section~3.9, pp.~105--106]{LandsbergManivel2004}. The twistor construction of Salamon, independently due to B\'erard-Bergery, associates a contact Fano manifold to a closed connected positive quaternionic-K\"ahler manifold~\cite{Salamon1982,LeBrun1995}. LeBrun's converse characterizes the contact Fano manifolds arising in this way by the existence of a K\"ahler--Einstein metric~\cite[Theorem~A]{LeBrun1995}. The finiteness and rigidity program of LeBrun and Salamon~\cite{LeBrun1993,LeBrunSalamon1994} provides the historical setting for the homogeneity conjecture. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $(X,F)$ be a smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of dimension $2n+1$, with $n\geq1$, and let $L=T_X/F$. There exist a finite-dimensional simple complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ and an algebraic isomorphism \[ \varphi:X\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }Y_{\mathfrak g} \] whose differential carries the given distribution $F$ to the canonical contact distribution on $Y_{\mathfrak g}$. Moreover, \[ L\simeq\varphi^*\bigl(\OO_{\PP(\mathfrak g)}(1)|_{Y_{\mathfrak g}}\bigr). \]
[corollary] [K\"ahler--Einstein existence]\label{thm:contact-fano-ke} Let $(X,F)$ be a smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of complex dimension $2n+1$, where $n\geq1$. Then $X$ admits a K\"ahler--Einstein metric of positive scalar curvature for its given complex structure.
[corollary] [Projective contact manifolds]\label{thm:projective-contact} Let $(X,F)$ be a smooth connected complex projective contact manifold of dimension $2n+1$, where $n\geq1$. Its second Betti number determines the following alternatives. \begin{enumerate} \item If $b_2(X)=1$, then $(X,F)$ is contact-isomorphic to the adjoint variety $Y_{\mathfrak g}$, with its canonical contact distribution, for a simple complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$. \item If $b_2(X)\geq2$, then there is a smooth connected complex projective manifold $Z$ of dimension $n+1$ and a biholomorphism \[ X\simeq\PP(T^*Z). \] Here $\PP(T^*Z)$ parametrizes lines in cotangent spaces, equivalently hyperplanes in tangent spaces. This assertion identifies the underlying manifolds, without imposing compatibility with the given distribution $F$. \end{enumerate} Conversely, every adjoint variety of complex dimension $2n+1$ and every projectivized cotangent bundle in the second alternative admits a contact structure. Thus these are precisely the underlying manifolds of smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds in these dimensions.
[corollary] \label{thm:quaternionic} Let $(M^{4m},g)$ be a closed connected smooth Riemannian manifold, where $m\geq2$, with holonomy contained in $\Sp(m)\Sp(1)$ in the standard representation and with positive scalar curvature. Then \[ \nabla\Rm_g=0. \] Moreover, $(M,g)$ is homothetic to a compact symmetric positive quaternionic-K\"ahler Wolf space.
