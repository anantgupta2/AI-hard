OPENAI SUMMARY: For the square-lattice nearest-neighbor cosine XY model, proves critical axis correlations $`C_{\beta_c}(r)\sim Ar^{-1/4}(\log r)^{1/8}`$ and the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless essential singularity $`\sqrt{\beta_c-\beta}\log\xi(\beta)\to B`$, with $`A,B\gt 0`$ after the free-box thermodynamic limit.
