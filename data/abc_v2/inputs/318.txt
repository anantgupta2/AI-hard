FAMILY 318: Chromatic splitting: filtrations and counterexamples
SUBJECT: Topology
OPENAI SUMMARY: Disproves strong chromatic splitting at height three for primes p ≥ 5, and weak splitting for the derived p-completed sphere at heights p (p ≥ 5) and $`p+1`$ (p ≥ 7). Nevertheless, for n ≥ 1 and $`p\gt n+1`$, the overlap $`L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedge`$ admits a $`2^n`$-stage filtration by the predicted localized-sphere pieces. At height three and prime three, even finite assembly from such pieces fails in the category of $`E(2)`$-local modules over the derived completed sphere.

[theorem] [Incompatible descent exponents]\label{thm:exponents} For every prime $p\geq5$, with $m=p^2+2$, \[ \expn_H(E_{p-1})>m, \qquad \expn_H\bigl(T(B_p)\bigr)\leq m. \] A homotopy retraction of $i_{p,X}$ would make $E_{p-1}$ an $H$-equivariant retract of $T(B_p)$.
