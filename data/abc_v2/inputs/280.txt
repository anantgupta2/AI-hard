ABSTRACT: Every simple unitary strongly rational vertex operator algebra generates a completely rational conformal net. All its simple grading-restricted modules are unitarizable, its canonical fusion forms are positive, and the Carpi–Weiner–Xu functor gives a braided unitary tensor equivalence from its grading-restricted finite-length module category onto the finite-index sectors of the net. Gui's extension theorems then identify normalized irreducible finite-index local extensions of these nets with simple CFT-type conformal extensions of the vertex operator algebras.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} Vertex operator algebras and conformal nets are two mathematical formulations of chiral conformal field theory. A vertex operator algebra records field modes and their algebraic identities. A conformal net assigns von Neumann algebras of bounded observables to intervals of the circle. Their expected correspondence has both an analytic and a representation-theoretic part. Formal commutation of fields must lead to commuting local algebras, and algebraic modules must correspond to Hilbert-space sectors with the same fusion and braiding. We establish this correspondence for every simple unitary strongly rational complex vertex operator algebra. The functor in the result is the Carpi--Weiner--Xu construction: it completes a unitary module and integrates its actual closed smeared fields. The tensor product on the net side is Connes fusion, with the Doplicher--Haag--Roberts braiding. \subsection{The statement} Let $V$ be a complex vertex operator algebra, with vacuum $\Omega$, conformal vector $\nu$ and conformal energy $L_0$. It is of \emph{CFT type} if \[ V=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}V_n,\qquad V_0=\mathbb C\Omega. \] Write $C_2(V)=\operatorname{span}\{a_{(-2)}b:a,b\in V\}$. We call $V$ \emph{strongly rational} if it is of CFT type, is isomorphic to its graded contragredient, is rational, and has finite-dimensional quotient $V/C_2(V)$. Here rationality means complete reducibility of admissible modules. Unitarity means that $V$ has a normalized positive invariant Hermitian form and its corresponding antilinear PCT involution $\Theta$. We use grading-restricted finite-length modules: their conformal-energy spaces are finite dimensional, and their weights are bounded below. Their algebraic braided tensor category is denoted by $\Rep(V)$. Its tensor product is the Huang--Lepowsky fusion product. The category $\Rep^u(V)$ consists of such modules with positive invariant Hermitian forms, and module homomorphisms. We call $V$ \emph{strongly unitary} when every simple module is unitarizable, and \emph{completely unitary} when, in addition, the canonical invariant Hermitian fusion forms are positive definite. In the latter case the module category is a unitary modular tensor category. For a homogeneous $a\in V$ of weight $d_a$, write \[ Y(a,z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}a_{(n)}z^{-n-1}. \] Polynomial energy bounds mean that, for each $a$, there are $C>0$ and nonnegative integers $p,k$ such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-energy} \|a_{(n)}b\|\leq C(1+|n|)^p\|(1+L_0)^k b\|, \qquad n\in\mathbb Z,\quad b\in V. \end{equation} These bounds define closable smeared fields \begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-smear} Y(a,f)=\oint_{S^1}Y(a,z)f(z)z^{d_a}\frac{dz}{2\pi i z}, \qquad f\in C^\infty(S^1), \end{equation} initially on $V$. For a nonempty nondense open interval $I\subset S^1$, set \begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-net} A_V(I)=W^*\!\left\{\overline{Y(a,f)}: a\in V\text{ homogeneous},\ \supp f\subset I\right\}. \end{equation} Here the von Neumann algebra generated by closed operators is the algebra generated by their bounded graph or polar data. The VOA is \emph{CKLW-strongly local} if it is energy-bounded and $A_V(I)\subset A_V(I')'$, where $I'$ is the interior of the complementary arc. A conformal net $A$ is \emph{completely rational} if it is irreducible, split, strongly additive and has finite $\mu$-index. Split means that separated interval algebras generate their spatial tensor product. Strong additivity means that removing one interior point from an interval does not change the algebra generated by its two components. For two disjoint intervals $E=I_1\cup I_3$, the $\mu$-index is the index of $A(E)\subset A(E')'$. We write $\Rep^f(A)$ for the additive category of finite-index, equivalently dualizable, locally normal DHR representations, including finite sums of the tensor unit. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $V$ be a simple unitary strongly rational vertex operator algebra over $\mathbb C$. Then: \begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}] \item $V$ has polynomial energy bounds and is CKLW-strongly local. The algebras \eqref{eq:intro-net} form an irreducible conformal net $A_V$. \item $V$ is completely unitary: every simple grading-restricted module is unitarizable, and all canonical invariant Hermitian fusion forms are positive definite. \item Every unitary grading-restricted finite-length module is strongly integrable, with the closed-field equality \eqref{eq:intro-cwx}. \item The net $A_V$ is completely rational. \item After choosing invariant positive forms on the simple modules, the Carpi--Weiner--Xu functor gives a braided unitary tensor equivalence \[ \Rep(V)\simeq\Rep^u(V) \xrightarrow{\ F_{\mathrm{CWX}}^V\ }\Rep^f(A_V). \] The tensor structure and braiding on the right are Connes fusion and DHR braiding, with a common orientation convention for the circle. \end{enumerate}

structure CFTTypeVOA (V : Type u) [AddCommGroup V] [Module ℂ V]
    extends OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.VertexAlgebra V where
  conformalVector : V
  centralCharge : ℂ
  grade : ℕ → Submodule ℂ V
  grading_internal : DirectSum.IsInternal grade
  grade_finite : ∀ d, FiniteDimensional ℂ (grade d)
  vacuum_ne_zero : vacuum ≠ 0
  grade_zero : grade 0 = ℂ ∙ vacuum
  conformal_mem : conformalVector ∈ grade 2
  conformal_energy : ∀ d, ∀ a ∈ grade d,
    OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector 1 a = (d : ℂ) • a
  conformal_translation : OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector 0 = toVertexAlgebra.translation
  translation_covariance : ∀ a : V, ∀ n : ℤ,
    OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields (toVertexAlgebra.translation a) n =
      (-(n : ℂ)) • OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields a (n - 1)
  virasoro : ∀ m n : ℤ,
    (OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector (m + 1)).comp (OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector (n + 1)) -
      (OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector (n + 1)).comp (OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector (m + 1)) =
    ((m - n : ℤ) : ℂ) • OAI.StronglyRationalVOA.mode fields conformalVector (m + n + 1) +
      (if m + n = 0 then (centralCharge / 12) * ((m : ℂ)^3 - m) else 0) •
        (1 : Module.End ℂ V)
end OAI.StronglyRationalVOA
