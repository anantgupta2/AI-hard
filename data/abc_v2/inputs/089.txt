FAMILY 089: Bounded-distortion L1 embeddings of planar and bounded-treewidth graphs
SUBJECT: Convex and metric geometry
OPENAI SUMMARY: Resolves the planar and bounded-treewidth cases of the Gupta–Newman–Rabinovich–Sinclair conjecture. Shortest-path metrics of finite connected graphs with arbitrary positive edge lengths embed into real L1 with universal distortion for planar graphs, and distortion depending only on treewidth for bounded-treewidth graphs. The corresponding multicommodity flow–cut gaps are uniformly bounded.

=== MANUSCRIPT: Planar Graph Metrics Embed into L1 with Constant Distortion (September-23-2026) [preprints/Planar-Graph-Metrics-Embed-into-L1-with-Constant-Distortion-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that every finite connected planar graph with arbitrary positive real edge lengths embeds into real L1 with a universal distortion bound. This resolves the planar embedding conjecture positively.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{intro} Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite connected undirected graph with positive real edge lengths. Its graph metric $d_G$ is the shortest-path distance on $V$. An embedding of a finite metric space $(V,d)$ into real $L_1$ has \emph{distortion at most $C$} if, after multiplication by a positive scalar, its distances lie between $d$ and $Cd$. The planar embedding conjecture asks whether one constant $C$ works for every planar graph metric, independently of the number of vertices and the edge lengths. We prove that it does. [statement] This proves the planar case of the conjecture of Gupta, Newman, Rabinovich, and Sinclair, which predicts uniformly bounded $L_1$ distortion in every proper minor-closed family~\cite{GNRS04}. In particular, the bound is independent of the number of distance scales in the graph metric. \subsection{Cuts, multicommodity flow, and the embedding problem} \label{intro:flow} The relationship with cuts explains why $L_1$ is a natural target. For $S\subseteq V$, put $\delta_S(x,y)=|\1_S(x)-\1_S(y)|$. A nonnegative combination \begin{equation}\label{intro:cut-metric} D(x,y)=\sum_{S\subseteq V}w_S\delta_S(x,y),\qquad w_S\ge0, \end{equation} is the $\ell_1$ distance between the vectors $(w_S\1_S(x))_{S\subseteq V}$ and $(w_S\1_S(y))_{S\subseteq V}$. Conversely, threshold sweeps through real coordinates give such a representation for every $L_1$ metric on a finite set. Thus we seek weighted cuts that separate each pair in proportion to its graph distance while charging each edge at most a constant times its length. The edge bound then extends to every pair along a shortest path. Linial, London, and Rabinovich established the connection between metric embeddings and multicommodity flow~\cite{LLR95}; Aumann and Rabani independently obtained a general approximate max-flow/min-cut theorem~\cite{AR98}. To state the corresponding planar consequence, give the edges nonnegative capacities and the unordered pairs of distinct vertices nonnegative demands, not all zero. Let $\lambda_*$ be the largest common multiplier of the demands that can be routed fractionally. Let $\phi_*$ be the minimum ratio of crossing capacity to separated demand over cuts with positive separated demand. For $\lambda_*>0$, the ratio $\phi_*/\lambda_*$ is the \emph{flow--cut gap}. Gupta et al. showed that its worst value on a fixed graph equals the worst $L_1$ distortion over all weighted shortest-path metrics on that graph~\cite[Section~3, Theorem~3.2]{GNRS04}. Hence Theorem~\ref{thm:main} also gives a universal bound for the undirected fractional flow--cut gap on planar graphs, with arbitrary demands. \subsection{Earlier results and the remaining obstacle} The general preceding bound for $n$-vertex planar metrics is $O(\sqrt{\log n})$, due to Rao~\cite{Rao99}. His construction embeds first into Euclidean space and then into $L_1$; it builds on the decomposition methods of Klein, Plotkin, and Rao for graphs excluding a fixed minor~\cite{KPR93}. Newman and Rabinovich showed that even series-parallel metrics can require $\Omega(\sqrt{\log n})$ distortion into Euclidean space~\cite{NR03}. Thus a constant planar $L_1$ bound requires a different route. Abraham, Filtser, Gupta, and Neiman later recovered Rao's planar bound through recursive shortest-path decompositions~\cite{AFGN22}. Constant distortion was known under several topological restrictions. Gupta et al. proved it for series-parallel graphs~\cite{GNRS04}. Chakrabarti, Jaffe, Lee, and Vincent obtained the sharp upper bound $2$~\cite{CJLV08}, with a matching lower bound due to Lee and Raghavendra~\cite{LR10}. Chekuri, Gupta, Newman, Rabinovich, and Sinclair gave a bound exponential in the outerplanarity parameter~\cite{CGNRS06}: the number of rounds needed to delete all vertices by successive outer-face removals in a planar drawing. [theorem] \label{thm:main} There is a finite universal constant $C\ge1$ with the following property. For every finite connected planar graph $G=(V,E)$ with strictly positive real edge lengths, there exist a measure space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mu)$ and a map $f:V\to L_1(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mu;\R)$ such that \[ d_G(x,y)\le\|f(x)-f(y)\|_1\le C d_G(x,y) \qquad(x,y\in V). \]

=== MANUSCRIPT: L1 Embeddings of Graphs of Bounded Treewidth (September-23-2026) [preprints/L1-Embeddings-of-Graphs-of-Bounded-Treewidth-September-23-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: For every fixed treewidth bound, the shortest-path metrics of finite connected graphs with arbitrary positive real edge lengths embed into real L1 with uniformly bounded distortion. This resolves the bounded-treewidth case of the Gupta–Newman–Rabinovich–Sinclair conjecture positively.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} A graph metric is the shortest-path metric $d_{G,\ell}$ of a finite connected undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ with positive real edge lengths $\ell:E\to(0,\infty)$. An embedding into real $L_1$ has \emph{distortion at most $C$} if it can be rescaled so that every image distance is between the original distance and $C$ times that distance. We establish a bound depending only on the treewidth. A \emph{tree decomposition} consists of a finite tree $T$ and subsets $B_t\subseteq V$, called bags, indexed by $t\in V(T)$: the bags cover $V$, each graph edge has both endpoints in a bag, and the bags containing any fixed vertex induce a connected subtree of $T$. Its width is $\max_t|B_t|-1$; the treewidth of $G$ is the minimum width of a tree decomposition. Throughout this paper $k$ bounds \emph{bag cardinality}, so the corresponding treewidth bound is $k-1$. [statement] The target can be taken to be a finite-dimensional real $\ell_1$ space. The proof gives the existence of $C_k$, without an explicit estimate for its dependence on $k$. The bounded-treewidth family is one of the cases of the conjecture of Gupta, Newman, Rabinovich, and Sinclair, which predicts a uniform $L_1$ distortion bound for every proper minor-closed graph family~\cite{GNRS}. By the excluded-planar-minor theorem of Robertson and Seymour~\cite{RS86Planar}, our result also gives a uniform bound for graphs excluding any fixed planar graph as a minor. \subsection{Context and prior work} Finite $L_1$ pseudometrics are exactly the nonnegative combinations of cut pseudometrics. If $S\subseteq V$ and $\delta_S(u,v)=|\mathbf1_S(u)-\mathbf1_S(v)|$, then \begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-cut-representation} D(u,v)=\sum_{S\subseteq V}w_S\delta_S(u,v),\qquad w_S\ge0, \end{equation} is realized by the vectors $(w_S\mathbf1_S(v))_{S\subseteq V}$. Conversely, threshold sweeps through real coordinates give this representation for any finite set of $L_1$ points. We therefore seek cuts that charge graph edges by at most a constant times their lengths and separate every vertex pair by a comparable amount. The edge bound extends along shortest paths to all pairs. Linial, London, and Rabinovich related low-distortion embeddings to approximate multicommodity flow--cut bounds~\cite{LLR95}; Aumann and Rabani independently obtained a general approximate max-flow/min-cut theorem~\cite{AR98}. Given nonnegative edge capacities and nonnegative demands on unordered pairs of distinct vertices, not all zero, let $\lambda_*$ be the largest common multiplier of the demands that can be routed fractionally, and let $\phi_*$ be the minimum ratio of crossing capacity to separated demand over cuts with positive separated demand. For instances with $\lambda_*>0$, the ratio $\phi_*/\lambda_*$ is the flow--cut gap. Minimizing the cut ratio that defines $\phi_*$ is the nonuniform sparsest-cut problem. The worst such gap on a fixed graph equals the worst $L_1$ distortion of its weighted shortest-path metrics~\cite[Section~3, Theorem~3.2]{GNRS}. For graphs with bags of size at most $k$, Theorem~\ref{thm:main} therefore gives \[ \lambda_*\leq\phi_*\leq C_k\lambda_*. \] Gupta et al. proved the treewidth-two case~\cite{GNRS}. The optimal distortion is $2$, by the upper bound of Chakrabarti, Jaffe, Lee, and Vincent~\cite{CJLV08} and the matching lower bound of Lee and Raghavendra~\cite{LR10}. Other structural restrictions also yielded uniform bounds: a fixed number of successive outer-face removals in a planar graph~\cite{CGNRS06}, and graphs formed by identifying edges of pieces from a fixed finite family~\cite{LP13}. A path decomposition is a tree decomposition whose indexing tree is a path; its minimum width is the graph's \emph{pathwidth}. Lee and Sidiropoulos established the bounded-pathwidth case through stochastic embeddings into trees: each sampled tree metric dominates the graph metric, while its expected expansion is bounded by a function of the pathwidth~\cite{LeeSidiropoulos}. [theorem] \label{thm:main} For every integer $k\ge2$ there is a finite constant $C_k\ge1$ with the following property. If a finite connected graph $G=(V,E)$ admits a tree decomposition with bags of size at most $k$, then, for every edge-length function $\ell:E\to(0,\infty)$, there is a map $F$ from $V$ into a real $L_1$ space such that \begin{equation}\label{eq:main-embedding} d_{G,\ell}(u,v) \le \|F(u)-F(v)\|_1 \le C_k\,d_{G,\ell}(u,v) \qquad(u,v\in V). \end{equation} The constant is independent of the number of vertices, the depth of the decomposition, and all ratios of edge lengths.

import Mathlib
namespace OAI
namespace PlanarL1
def IsPlanar {V : Type} (G : SimpleGraph V) : Prop :=
  ∃ (point : V → ℝ × ℝ) (arc : ∀ u v : V, G.Adj u v → C(unitInterval, ℝ × ℝ)),
    Function.Injective point ∧
    (∀ u v h, (arc u v h) 0 = point u ∧ (arc u v h) 1 = point v) ∧
    (∀ u v h, Function.Injective (arc u v h)) ∧
    (∀ u v h (t : unitInterval) w, 0 < (t : ℝ) → (t : ℝ) < 1 →
      (arc u v h) t ≠ point w) ∧
    (∀ u v h x y k (s t : unitInterval),
      0 < (s : ℝ) → (s : ℝ) < 1 → 0 < (t : ℝ) → (t : ℝ) < 1 →
      (arc u v h) s = (arc x y k) t →
      (u = x ∧ v = y) ∨ (u = y ∧ v = x))
def walkLength {V : Type} {G : SimpleGraph V} (length : V → V → ℝ)
    {u v : V} : G.Walk u v → ℝ
  | .nil => 0
  | @SimpleGraph.Walk.cons _ _ u w _ _ p => length u w + walkLength length p
noncomputable def graphDistance {V : Type} (G : SimpleGraph V)
    (length : V → V → ℝ) (u v : V) : ℝ :=
  sInf (Set.range (fun p : G.Walk u v => walkLength length p))
def MainStatement : Prop :=
  ∃ C : ℝ, 1 ≤ C ∧
    ∀ (n : ℕ) (G : SimpleGraph (Fin n)) (length : Fin n → Fin n → ℝ),
      G.Connected → IsPlanar G →
      (∀ u v, length u v = length v u) →
      (∀ u v, G.Adj u v → 0 < length u v) →
      ∃ (Ω : Type) (measurable : MeasurableSpace Ω)
          (μ : @MeasureTheory.Measure Ω measurable)
          (f : Fin n → @MeasureTheory.Lp Ω ℝ measurable _ 1 μ),
        ∀ x y, graphDistance G length x y ≤ ‖f x - f y‖ ∧
          ‖f x - f y‖ ≤ C * graphDistance G length x y

import Mathlib
namespace OAI
noncomputable section
namespace BoundedTreewidthL1
universe u_1
variable {V : Type u_1} (G : SimpleGraph V)
def walkLength (ℓ : G.edgeSet → ℝ) : {u v : V} → G.Walk u v → ℝ
  | _, _, .nil => 0
  | u, _, .cons (v := v) h p => ℓ ⟨s(u, v), h⟩ + walkLength ℓ p
noncomputable def shortestPathDistance (ℓ : G.edgeSet → ℝ) (u v : V) : ℝ :=
  sInf {r : ℝ | ∃ p : G.Walk u v, p.IsPath ∧ walkLength G ℓ p = r}
def HasTreeDecomposition [DecidableEq V] (k : ℕ) : Prop :=
  ∃ n : ℕ, ∃ T : SimpleGraph (Fin n), ∃ bag : Fin n → Finset V,
    T.IsTree ∧
    (∀ v : V, ∃ t, v ∈ bag t) ∧
    (∀ u v : V, G.Adj u v → ∃ t, u ∈ bag t ∧ v ∈ bag t) ∧
    (∀ v : V, (T.induce {t | v ∈ bag t}).Connected) ∧
    (∀ t, (bag t).card ≤ k)
def HasL1Embedding (ℓ : G.edgeSet → ℝ) (C : ℝ) : Prop :=
  ∃ m : ℕ, ∃ F : V → PiLp (1 : ENNReal) (fun _ : Fin m => ℝ),
    ∀ u v : V,
      shortestPathDistance G ℓ u v ≤ ‖F u - F v‖ ∧
      ‖F u - F v‖ ≤ C * shortestPathDistance G ℓ u v
