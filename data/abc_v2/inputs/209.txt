FAMILY 209: Integral counterexamples to Gersten’s conjecture
SUBJECT: Algebra
OPENAI SUMMARY: Disproves unrestricted integral Gersten injectivity in degrees 3 and 5. Two explicit two-dimensional ramified regular local rings of mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ have nonzero integral K-theory classes that vanish over their fraction fields.

=== MANUSCRIPT: An integral counterexample to Gersten's conjecture (September-25-2026) [preprints/An-Integral-Counterexample-to-Gerstens-Conjecture-September-25-2026/build/source/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We construct a two-dimensional ramified regular local ring A of mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ for which $`K_5(A)\to K_5(\mathop{\mathrm{Frac}}\nolimits A)`$ has a nonzero kernel. This disproves Gersten's conjecture in its unrestricted integral form.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} For a Noetherian regular local domain $A$ with fraction field $F$, Gersten's conjecture asserts exactness of the canonical augmented residue complex \begin{equation}\label{eq:gersten} 0\longrightarrow K_n(A)\longrightarrow K_n(F) \longrightarrow\bigoplus_{\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p=1} K_{n-1}(\kappa(\mathfrak p))\longrightarrow\cdots . \end{equation} Here $n\geq0$, the groups are integral Quillen $K$-groups, and the residues come from localization and the codimension filtration. The first assertion is generic injectivity: a class that vanishes after passage to the fraction field must already vanish on $A$. We construct a counterexample to this assertion. [statement] The maximal ideal of $A$ is $(x,y)$, and $5\in(x,y)^4$. Its special fiber has dimension one and embedding dimension two at the closed point. Thus the regular total ring is ramified and is not smooth over $V$. This distinction is central both to the example and to its relation with positive results on Gersten's conjecture. \paragraph{History and related results.} Gersten's questions about transfer and local higher $K$-theory \cite{Gersten1973Problems}, together with his work on exact sequences \cite{Gersten1973Exact}, place the conjecture in the study of localization and residue maps. Quillen formulated it for arbitrary regular local rings and proved it for semilocalizations of finite-type algebras over a field at regular primes \cite[\S7, Conjecture~5.10 and Theorem~5.11]{Quillen1973Higher}. Panin established the full equicharacteristic case \cite[Theorem~A]{Panin2003}. In mixed characteristic, Gillet and Levine's relative theorem reduces the smooth local case to the associated discrete valuation ring case \cite{GilletLevine1987}. Geisser and Levine prove finite-coefficient Gersten exactness for semilocal rings essentially smooth over a discrete valuation ring, for every finite coefficient order, including powers of the residue characteristic \cite[Theorem~8.2]{GeisserLevine2000}; their text credits B.~Kahn for pointing out this consequence. Skalit proved finite-coefficient Gersten exactness for arbitrary unramified regular local rings, again for every finite coefficient order \cite[Corollary~5.7]{Skalit2017}. In mixed characteristic $(0,p)$, unramified means $p\notin\mathfrak m^2$; our ring is excluded since $5\in\mathfrak m^4$. Version~1 of Druzhinin's preprint states a result for essentially smooth local Henselian schemes over a discrete valuation ring \cite[Theorem~4.5]{Druzhinin2025}. The smoothness hypotheses exclude the structure map $V\to A$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. Feld's finite-coefficient example is a close methodological predecessor \cite[Theorem~1.1 and Corollary~5.1]{Feld2026}. It gives a degree-two augmentation kernel modulo three at a ramified regular local ring whose integral $K_2$ map is injective. Its supported pushforward, character averaging, cyclotomic scalar extension, conjugate splitting and divisor-class detection also organize our construction. In that example, an integral lift of the coefficient kernel has nonzero but three-divisible generic image, as follows from integral injectivity and the coefficient exact sequence. An integral generic kernel therefore requires an additional argument. Here a vanishing result for integral $K_4$ permits a cyclotomic coefficient class to lift to integral $K_5$. Two Bott factors contribute the square of the cyclotomic character, whose signs are realized by an elliptic quotient of the Fermat quartic. Our companion paper, \emph{An integral degree-three Gersten counterexample} \cite[Theorem~1.1]{OpenAIDegreeThree2026}, constructs an integral $K_3$ kernel at a different ramified ring: the local ring at the special point $[0:0:1]$ of the cyclotomic quintic model $(X-Z)^5-XY^4+Y^5+\zeta Z^5=0$ over the integers of $\Q_5(\zeta)$, where $\zeta$ is a primitive fifth root of unity. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let $V$ be the ring of integers in the unramified extension of $\Q_5$ of degree eight, and put \[ A=\bigl(V[x,y]/(5+x^4+y^4)\bigr)_{(5,x,y)}. \] Then $A$ is a Noetherian regular local domain of dimension two and \[ \ker\bigl(K_5(A)\longrightarrow K_5(\Frac A)\bigr)\ne0. \] Consequently the unrestricted integral Gersten conjecture has a negative answer.

=== MANUSCRIPT: An integral degree-three Gersten counterexample (September-26-2026) [preprints/An-Integral-Degree-Three-Gersten-Counterexample-September-26-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We construct a two-dimensional ramified regular local ring A in mixed characteristic $`(0,5)`$ for which the integral map $`K_3(A)\to K_3(\mathop{\mathrm{Frac}}\nolimits A)`$ has nonzero kernel. This gives a negative answer to the unrestricted integral Gersten conjecture.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} For a regular local domain $B$ with fraction field $F_B$, the integral Gersten conjecture asks whether, for each $n\geq0$, the augmented complex of Quillen groups \[ 0\longrightarrow K_n(B)\longrightarrow K_n(F_B) \longrightarrow \bigoplus_{\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p=1} K_{n-1}(\kappa(\mathfrak p))\longrightarrow\cdots \] is exact. Here $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ is the residue field at a prime, and the later maps are the codimension-residue maps. In particular, exactness requires the first map $K_n(B)\to K_n(F_B)$ to be injective. We construct a ring for which this requirement fails in degree three. Fix a primitive fifth root of unity $\zeta$, and define \[ L=\Q_5(\zeta),\qquad V=\OO_L,\qquad \pi=1-\zeta. \] Let $\X\subset\mathbb P^2_V$ be the projective hypersurface \[ (X-Z)^5-XY^4+Y^5+\zeta Z^5=0. \] On its special fiber take $m=[0:0:1]$, and set $A=\OO_{\X,m}$. We use $G_i$ for coherent $K$-theory and $G_i(-;\Z/5)$ for its mod-five coefficient groups. [statement] Thus the unrestricted integral Gersten complex can fail already at its initial injection. The class $c$ is not in $5K_3(A)$: any such multiple would have zero coefficient reduction, and hence zero reduction after restriction to $A[1/\pi]$. This conclusion does not determine the order of $c$ or its image in $K_3(A)\otimes\Q$. \subsection*{Context and earlier results} Gersten asked, among other questions, whether the transfer from the residue field to a discrete valuation ring vanishes \cite[Problem 9]{GerstenProblems}. Localization connects this question to injectivity of restriction to the fraction field: the image of the residue-field transfer is its kernel. Quillen formulated the general regular-local conjecture and proved it for regular semilocalizations of finite-type algebras over a field \cite[\S7, Conjecture 5.10 and Theorem 5.11]{QuillenHigher}. Sherman established the equicharacteristic DVR case \cite[p.~498]{Sherman}, and Panin proved the full equicharacteristic case \cite[Theorem A]{Panin}. These results place the remaining integral question in mixed characteristic. In mixed characteristic, smoothness over a DVR and the choice of coefficients lead to different positive results. Gillet and Levine's relative theory reduces the smooth-over-DVR setting to the DVR case \cite{GilletLevine}. Geisser and Levine proved finite-coefficient Gersten exactness for semilocal rings essentially smooth over a DVR, including coefficients of residue-characteristic order \cite[Theorem 8.2]{GeisserLevine}. Druzhinin's preprint states exactness for essentially smooth local Henselian schemes over a DVR \cite[Theorem 4.5]{Druzhinin}. A separate result of Skalit gives finite-coefficient exactness for every unramified regular local ring \cite[Corollary 5.7]{Skalit}; in mixed characteristic $(0,p)$, unramified here means $p\in\mathfrak m\setminus\mathfrak m^2$. His integral result is a reduction to DVRs essentially smooth over $\Z$ \cite[Theorem 5.4]{Skalit}. Our ring satisfies $5\in\mathfrak m_A^2$, and its special fiber over the chosen $V$ is singular at $m$; it lies outside the unramified and smooth-over-$V$ settings just described. The distinction between integral and coefficient classes governs the proof. A finite-coefficient kernel class may have a nonzero coefficient boundary and hence fail to lift integrally. Even when it lifts, its integral generic restriction may be nonzero but divisible by the coefficient prime. Feld's mod-three degree-two example and its integral divisibility corollary illustrate this second issue \cite[Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 5.1]{Feld}. Feld's construction also provides a methodological antecedent: push a Bott-type class from a divisor and detect its image after a flat cyclotomic base change and inversion of three. The symbol sketch in \cite[Remark 5.3, footnote 3]{Feld} gives a related use of symbols.
