=== MANUSCRIPT: The Subcritical Hénon–Lane–Emden Conjecture (September-24-2026) [preprints/The-Subcritical-Henon-Lane-Emden-Conjecture-September-24-2026/build/main.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove the subcritical Hénon–Lane–Emden conjecture: for every dimension n ≥ 2, positive powers p, q, and real weights A, B, the system has no strictly positive entire solution when $`(n+A)/(p+1)+(n+B)/(q+1)\gt n-2`$. Solutions need only be continuous at the origin and classical elsewhere, without a condition at infinity. For n ≥ 3 and $`A,B\gt -2`$, combining this result with the radial existence theorem of Bidaut-Véron and Giacomini proves Phan's Conjecture C in full: a positive radial entire solution exists whenever the strict inequality fails.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} The H\'enon--Lane--Emden system is \begin{equation}\label{eq:system} -\Delta u=|x|^A v^p,\qquad -\Delta v=|x|^B u^q \qquad\text{in }\R^n\setminus\{0\}. \end{equation} Its subcritical nonexistence conjecture asks whether the weighted hyperbola alone rules out positive solutions, without symmetry or conditions at infinity. The unweighted case \(A=B=0\) is the classical Lane--Emden system. The scalar subcritical Liouville theorem of Gidas and Spruck is a starting point for this question~\cite{GidasSpruck1981}. For systems, Mitidieri developed a Rellich identity and early nonexistence regions~\cite{Mitidieri1993,Mitidieri1996}. Serrin and Zou proved the unweighted three-dimensional assertion under a growth condition; Pol\'a\v{c}ik, Quittner, and Souplet removed that condition, and Souplet settled dimension four~\cite{SerrinZou1996, PolacikQuittnerSouplet2007,Souplet2009}. Li, Li, and Wei obtained a further unweighted nonexistence region for \(p,q\ge1\) in every dimension \(n\ge5\)~\cite[Theorem~1.1]{LiLiWei2025}. Liouville theorems also serve as inputs to rescaling arguments for a priori bounds and singularity estimates~\cite{GidasSpruckBounds1981,PolacikQuittnerSouplet2007}. For the weighted system, Bidaut-V\'eron and Giacomini identified the critical hyperbola in the regular radial theory under admissible weights~\cite{BidautVeronGiacomini2010}. Phan formulated the unrestricted weighted question for \(A,B>-2\) as Conjecture~C and established bounded, low-dimensional, and conditional cases~\cite{Phan}. Fazly and Ghoussoub obtained bounded and stability-based results for nonnegative weights~\cite{FazlyGhoussoub2014}; Li and Zhang subsequently proved the weighted conjecture in dimension three without a boundedness assumption~\cite{LiZhang2019}. Results in restricted exponent ranges extend to dimensions four and five~\cite{Li2022}. More recently, Huang and Zou stated the all-real-weight, origin-continuous assertion as Conjecture~B and proved cases with stability, decay, or energy hypotheses~\cite{HuangZou}. Xu and Luo obtained a higher-order weighted theorem over the full exponent range for radial solutions subject to regularity and growth conditions~\cite{XuLuo2026}. The full nonradial question in higher dimensions is the subject of this paper. [statement] [statement] In particular, positivity at the origin is part of the hypothesis, but differentiability there is not. No radial symmetry, boundedness, integrability, finite energy, stability, or growth or decay condition at infinity is required. Theorem~\ref{thm:main} resolves Huang and Zou's Conjecture~B positively and yields Corollary~\ref{cor:lane-emden} by setting \(A=B=0\). Corollary~\ref{cor:phan-classification} records this derived classification. The weak regularity at the origin is genuine: positivity and continuity, rather than a prescribed order of vanishing or smoothness there, are all that is assumed. The weighted assertion cannot be inferred merely by applying the unweighted assertion at the same exponents. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(n\ge2\) be an integer, let \(p,q>0\), and let \(A,B\in\R\). Suppose that \begin{equation}\label{eq:subcritical} \frac{n+A}{p+1}+\frac{n+B}{q+1}>n-2. \end{equation} There are no functions \[ u,v\in C^2(\R^n\setminus\{0\})\cap C(\R^n), \qquad u(x)>0,\quad v(x)>0\quad(x\in\R^n), \] satisfying Equation~\eqref{eq:system} at every \(x\ne0\).
[corollary] [Unweighted Lane--Emden conjecture] \label{cor:lane-emden} For every integer \(n\ge3\) and \(p,q>0\) satisfying \[ \frac{1}{p+1}+\frac{1}{q+1}>\frac{n-2}{n}, \] there is no strictly positive \(C^2(\R^n)\) solution of \[ -\Delta u=v^p,\qquad -\Delta v=u^q\qquad\text{in }\R^n. \]

  continuous_u : Continuous u
  continuous_v : Continuous v
  regular_u : ContDiffOn ℝ 2 u ({0}ᶜ : Set (Space n))
  regular_v : ContDiffOn ℝ 2 v ({0}ᶜ : Set (Space n))
  positive_u : ∀ x, 0 < u x
  positive_v : ∀ x, 0 < v x
  equation_u : ∀ x, x ≠ 0 →
    -(Laplacian.laplacian u) x = Real.rpow ‖x‖ A * Real.rpow (v x) p
  equation_v : ∀ x, x ≠ 0 →
    -(Laplacian.laplacian v) x = Real.rpow ‖x‖ B * Real.rpow (u x) q
