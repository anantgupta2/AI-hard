=== MANUSCRIPT: The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations (September-25-2026) [preprints/The-trace-cone-classifies-Razak-Jacelon-stabilizations-September-25-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We prove that the canonical topological cone of all extended lower-semicontinuous tracial weights determines a separable nuclear C∗-algebra after tensoring with the Razak–Jacelon algebra and the compact operators, answering Robert's trace-cone classification question positively. The isomorphism realizes the prescribed cone map, with arbitrary ideal structure and without a density assumption on the finite domains of the weights.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} The Razak--Jacelon algebra \(\W\) is a simple, monotracial, stably projectionless nuclear \(C^*\)-algebra \cite{Jacelon}. Tensoring by \(\W\) removes the \(K\)-theoretic part of the classification problem and makes the order on the Cuntz semigroup visible through traces. The question is whether the resulting trace data determine the algebra even when its ideal structure is unrestricted. We prove that they do. \subsection{The invariant and the main theorem} For a \(C^*\)-algebra \(C\), let \(T(C)\) consist of all maps \(\tau:C_+\to[0,\infty]\) satisfying \[ \tau(0)=0,\qquad \tau(a+b)=\tau(a)+\tau(b),\qquad \tau(ra)=r\tau(a)\quad(r>0), \] together with \(\tau(x^*x)=\tau(xx^*)\) and lower semicontinuity for norm-convergent nets in \(C_+\). Arithmetic is extended nonnegative arithmetic. We impose no density condition on the finite domain. In particular, every closed two-sided ideal \(I\) contributes the weight \begin{equation}\label{intro:ideal-weight} \tau_I(a)= \begin{cases} 0,&a\in I_+,\\ \infty,&a\in C_+\setminus I_+. \end{cases} \end{equation} The canonical topology is specified by \begin{equation}\label{intro:trace-topology} \tau_i\longrightarrow\tau \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \limsup_i\tau_i((a-\eps)_+) \leq\tau(a)\leq\liminf_i\tau_i(a) \quad(a\in C_+,\ \eps>0). \end{equation} An isomorphism of these topological cones is a homeomorphism preserving addition, the zero weight, and multiplication by every positive real scalar. \Needspace{3\baselineskip} [statement] The conclusion is an actual \(C^*\)-algebra isomorphism. The algebras may be nonunital, may have both finite and infinite subquotients, and may have an arbitrary primitive ideal space. Their only regularity assumptions are separability and nuclearity. Moreover, the isomorphism can realize the prescribed cone map: after transporting the given map to \(F:T(B\otimes\W\otimes\K)\to T(A\otimes\W\otimes\K)\), we obtain an isomorphism \(\Phi\) with \(\tau\circ\Phi=F(\tau)\) for every extended trace \(\tau\). This refinement is proved at the end of \Cref{pt:intertwining}. The presence of \eqref{intro:ideal-weight} makes the invariant sensitive to this ideal structure. For example, \[ I\subseteq J \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \tau_I+\tau_J=\tau_I. \] The finite and zero ideals of a general weight are also encoded by the two limiting operations obtained by letting its positive scalar multiple tend to zero and to infinity. These features will let us localize each approximation on the ideal where its controlling trace is finite. \subsection{History and significance} Razak's classification of simple inductive limits of stably projectionless building blocks made the cone of traces, together with its scale, an effective classification invariant \cite[Theorems~1.1 and~2.1]{Razak}. Jacelon's construction singled out the simple monotracial algebra $\W$ in this setting \cite{Jacelon}. The existence, uniqueness, and approximate-intertwining organization of those classification arguments also provides the broad method used here, with new local estimates needed for arbitrary ideals. The trace-cone question is due to Leonel Robert. It was recorded in Santiago's 2012 conference abstract \cite{SantiagoWStable} and appears as Problem~LXVIII in the survey of Schafhauser, Tikuisis, and White \cite{STW}. Their formulation retains every extended trace, including the zero/infinity ideal weights. Their May 2026 discussion describes the known simple and traceless cases and the difficulty already presented by one proper nonzero ideal. \Cref{thm:main} gives a positive answer to that problem for the full cone and the unrestricted class of separable nuclear algebras in its statement. Elliott, Robert, and Santiago developed the compact topological cone of extended lower-semicontinuous traces and its relation to functionals on the Cuntz semigroup \cite{ERS}. [theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \(A\) and \(B\) be separable nuclear complex \(C^*\)-algebras. If \(T(A)\) and \(T(B)\) are isomorphic as topological cones, then \[ A\otimes\W\otimes\K\ \cong\ B\otimes\W\otimes\K, \] where \(\K=\K(\ell^2(\N))\) and all tensor products are spatial.

abbrev Positive := {a : A // 0 ≤ a}
namespace Positive
variable {A}
def zero : Positive A := ⟨0, le_rfl⟩
def add (a b : Positive A) : Positive A := ⟨a.1 + b.1, add_nonneg a.2 b.2⟩
def scale (r : ℝ≥0) (a : Positive A) : Positive A :=
  ⟨(r : ℝ) • a.1, smul_nonneg r.2 a.2⟩
def starMul (x : A) : Positive A := ⟨star x * x, star_mul_self_nonneg x⟩
def mulStar (x : A) : Positive A := ⟨x * star x, mul_star_self_nonneg x⟩
def cut (a : Positive A) (ε : ℝ≥0) : Positive A :=
  ⟨cfcₙ (fun t : ℝ => max (t - (ε : ℝ)) 0) a.1,
    cfcₙ_nonneg (fun _ _ => le_max_right _ _)⟩
end Positive
structure ExtendedTrace where
  toFun : Positive A → ℝ≥0∞
  map_zero : toFun Positive.zero = 0
  map_add : ∀ a b, toFun (Positive.add a b) = toFun a + toFun b
  map_scale : ∀ (r : ℝ≥0), 0 < r → ∀ a,
    toFun (Positive.scale r a) = (r : ℝ≥0∞) * toFun a
  tracial : ∀ x : A, toFun (Positive.starMul x) = toFun (Positive.mulStar x)
  lowerSemicontinuous : LowerSemicontinuous toFun
instance : CoeFun (ExtendedTrace A) (fun _ => Positive A → ℝ≥0∞) :=
  ⟨ExtendedTrace.toFun⟩
def CanonicalConvergence {ι : Type*} (l : Filter ι) (τ : ι → ExtendedTrace A)
    (σ : ExtendedTrace A) : Prop :=
  ∀ (a : Positive A) (ε : ℝ≥0), 0 < ε →
    Filter.limsup (fun i => τ i (Positive.cut a ε)) l ≤ σ a ∧
    σ a ≤ Filter.liminf (fun i => τ i a) l

def scale (r : ℝ≥0) (τ : ExtendedTrace A) : ExtendedTrace A where
  toFun a := (r : ℝ≥0∞) * τ a
  map_zero := by rw [τ.map_zero, mul_zero]
  map_add a b := by rw [τ.map_add, mul_add]
  map_scale s hs a := by rw [τ.map_scale s hs]; ac_rfl
  tracial x := by rw [τ.tracial x]
  lowerSemicontinuous :=
    (ENNReal.continuous_const_mul ENNReal.coe_ne_top).comp_lowerSemicontinuous
      τ.lowerSemicontinuous (fun _ _ h => mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_left h bot_le)
