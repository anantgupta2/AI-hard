FAMILY 227: Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition
SUBJECT: Probability and statistical mechanics
OPENAI SUMMARY: For zero-field Gaussian SK heat-bath dynamics with rate-one updates per spin, proves worst-start cutoff on the $`\log n`$ scale for fixed $`0\le\beta\lt 1`$, mixing time $`n^{2/3+o(1)}`$ at β = 1, and stretched-exponential mixing from a Gibbs-sampled fixed starting configuration for β &gt; 1, in probability over disorder.
