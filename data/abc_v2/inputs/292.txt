FAMILY 292: Kirchberg's $`\mathcal O_2`$ norm-ultrapower embedding problem
SUBJECT: Operator algebras
OPENAI SUMMARY: Constructs an explicit separable unital full group C∗-algebra that cannot embed unitally into the norm ultrapower of any fixed nonzero unital nuclear C∗-algebra, for any free ultrafilter on the natural numbers. Taking the target to be $`\mathcal O_2`$ answers Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem negatively.

=== MANUSCRIPT: An explicit obstruction to nuclear norm-ultrapower embeddings (September-23-2026) [preprints/An-explicit-obstruction-to-nuclear-norm-ultrapower-embeddings-September-23-2026/build/paper.tex]
ABSTRACT: We give a negative answer to Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem. We construct an explicit separable unital full group C∗-algebra that admits no unital embedding into Bω, for any nonzero unital nuclear C∗-algebra B and any free ultrafilter ω on ℕ. In particular, it does not embed unitally into $`\mathcal O_2^\omega`$.
INTRODUCTION (start): \label{sec:introduction} Throughout, $C^*$-algebras are complex, and an \emph{embedding} is an injective $*$-homomorphism. We state unit preservation explicitly. For a nonzero unital $C^*$-algebra $B$ and a free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\N$, its \emph{norm ultrapower} is \[ B^\omega=\ell^\infty(\N,B)/I_\omega(B),\qquad I_\omega(B)=\{(b_n):\lim_\omega\|b_n\|=0\}. \] Here $\ell^\infty(\N,B)$ is the algebra of bounded sequences, and the quotient uses the operator norm. The Cuntz algebra $\Otwo$ is the universal unital $C^*$-algebra generated by two isometries $s_1,s_2$ satisfying $s_1s_1^*+s_2s_2^*=1$; it was introduced by Cuntz~\cite{Cuntz1977}. The embedding theorem of Kirchberg--Phillips~\cite{KirchbergPhillips2000} states that every separable unital exact $C^*$-algebra embeds unitally into $\Otwo$. Here exactness means that minimal tensoring preserves short exact sequences. Passing from $\Otwo$ to its norm ultrapower asks how far this universality extends beyond exact algebras. The techniques behind the embedding theorem led Kirchberg to consider this possibility, as recounted in \cite[Section~1]{GoldbringSinclair2015}. Kirchberg's embedding problem asks whether every separable unital $C^*$-algebra admits a unital embedding into $\Otwo^\omega$; see Goldbring--Sinclair~\cite[Section~3.1]{GoldbringSinclair2015}. We give an explicit counterexample. The unital formulation is equivalent to the formulation without unit preservation~\cite[Section~3.1]{GoldbringSinclair2015}. This question concerns norm ultrapowers of a nuclear $C^*$-algebra. It is distinct from the tensor-norm formulation of Kirchberg's conjecture and from Connes' embedding problem, as emphasized in~\cite[Section~1]{GoldbringSinclair2015}. \Needspace{14\baselineskip} [statement] The group is countable and independent of both $B$ and $\omega$; no separability assumption on $B$ is needed. The proof treats an arbitrary fixed nuclear $B$ throughout. Specializing to the nuclear algebra $\Otwo$ gives the negative answer to the embedding problem; for its nuclearity, see~\cite[Section~4, pp.~80--82]{PaulsenZheng2016}. Section~\ref{sec:consequences} gives the consequences for existential closure, permanence of $\Otwo^\omega$-embeddability under crossed products, and good nuclear witnesses, with the relevant notions defined there. \subsection{Previous work and the new obstruction} \begingroup \setlength{\emergencystretch}{2em} Recent approaches study computability and tensor norms. Fox--Goldbring--Hart~\cite{FoxGoldbringHart2024} derive computability-theoretic consequences of a positive answer, using the computable presentation of $\Otwo$. Goldbring--Sinclair give a conditional route to a negative answer through a \emph{weakly explicit minimal tensor norm}: an effective choice of universal formulas approximating the minimal tensor-norm predicates to a fixed accuracy. They leave this property of $\Otwo$ open \cite[Definition~4.16, Theorem~4.18 and Question~4.20]{GoldbringSinclair2025Tensor}. Our obstruction instead comes from the representation theory of an explicit discrete group.\par \endgroup The group configuration already appears in the nonfiniteness arguments of Sauers~\cite[Section~2.4]{Sauers2026NonMF} and Eckhardt~\cite[Theorem~3.1 and Example~3.2]{Eckhardt2026}. They show that the full algebra of the ascending HNN extension $G$ is nonfinite: it contains a proper isometry $v$, so $v^*v=1$ but $vv^*\ne1$. Its property~(T) base group $\Gamma=\Z^3\rtimes\SL_3(\Z)$ and its finite-index scalar self-embeddings are recorded in de Cornulier~\cite[Proposition~1.1 and its proof]{deCornulier2007}. Sauers and Eckhardt also construct non-MF groups: groups admitting no injective homomorphism into the unitary group of any norm matrix corona $\prod_j M_{d_j}/\bigoplus_j M_{d_j}$, with positive integer dimensions $d_j$. Here the product consists of bounded sequences and the direct sum is the ideal of sequences tending to zero in norm \cite[Theorem~4.3]{Eckhardt2026}\cite[Sections~3--5 […]
[theorem] \label{thm:main} Let \[ G=\Z[1/2]^3\rtimes\bigl(\SL_3(\Z)\times\Z\bigr), \qquad (M,k)\cdot v=2^kMv, \] and let $A=C^*(G)$ be its full group $C^*$-algebra. Then $A$ is separable and unital. For every nonzero unital nuclear $C^*$-algebra $B$ and every free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\N$, there is no unital embedding \[ A\longrightarrow B^\omega. \] In particular, $A$ does not embed unitally into $\Otwo^\omega$.
